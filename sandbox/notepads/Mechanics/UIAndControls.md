## Cutscene & Warp Scripting (Turns 11–20)
- **Dialogue Advancement:** Pressing `B` with `sleep 1000` advances standard dialogue boxes cleanly at `FAST` text speed and is completely inert once overworld control returns (preventing accidental NPC/sign re-interactions).
- **Warp + Cutscene Combo in `run_code`:** A `tools.press_buttons` call that steps onto a warp tile automatically terminates upon leaving the overworld. A subsequent `tools.press_buttons(["B", "sleep 1000", ...])` call in the same `run_code` script executes during the post-warp cutscene, clearing both the warp and cutscene in one turn.

## Battle & Menu Persistence Mechanics (Turns 29–42)
- **No In-Battle Bag Healing (Turn 36):** Selecting `POTION` in the `BAG` during battle only shows `▶CANCEL`. Healing items from the Bag can only be used in the overworld. Held items (e.g. `BERRY JUICE`) still activate automatically in battle.
- **Battle Menu Cursor Memory & Drain Move Timing (Turns 36, 238, 293, 1113, 2438):** The 2x2 battle menu (`FIGHT` / `BAG` / `POKéMON` / `RUN`) resets to `▶FIGHT` at the start of each new battle, and remembers the last used quadrant within the same battle; always press bounded `Up, Left, A` on the action menu to guarantee `▶FIGHT`. The move menu (`FIGHT`) and Bag pocket (`ITEMS` / `POKé BALLS`) persist across battles. Note that on a 3-move Pokémon (where Slot 4 `-` is empty), pressing `Down` from Slot 2 (`top-right`) is blocked by the empty Slot 4—always press **`Left, Down`** (never `Down, Left`) to deterministically select Slot 3 (`bottom-left`). Also, when two HP-draining moves (e.g., `Mega Drain` + `Absorb`) execute in the same turn, the animations and drain text take ~15.0s total—use `["Up", "Left", "A", "sleep 600", "<Move>", "sleep 9500", "B", "sleep 5000", "B", "sleep 1500"]` (16.6s/turn) in multi-turn loops to prevent input desync.
- **Start Menu, Bag Row & Party Slot Cursor Memory (Turns 41–42, 296, 306, 575, 926, 1092, 1096, 1349):** The Start menu remembers its cursor row index across openings (`0: POKéDEX`, `1: POKéMON`, `2: BAG`, `3: ORION`, `4: SAVE`, `5: OPTION`, `6: EXIT`). Note: if `intro_runner` sets `TEXT SPEED: FAST` from the pre-game Main Menu without opening the overworld `Start` menu, the first overworld `Start` press starts at **Row 0 (`▶POKéDEX`)** (`Down` 5 times to `OPTION`); if `intro_runner` opened `OPTION` in the overworld before obtaining the Pokédex, the first `Start` press after obtaining the Pokédex starts at **Row 3 (`▶ORION`)**. Inside the `BAG`, switching pockets (`Left`/`Right`) wraps between Dot 1 (`ITEMS`) and Dot 5 (`KEY ITEMS`) and preserves the vertical row index across pockets (`Row 1` in `BERRIES` stays `Row 1` in `ITEMS`); always press bounded `Up` inside `ITEMS` to guarantee Row 0 (`POTION`). Also, using an item (`USE`) from the `BAG` in the overworld opens the Party screen with the cursor on whichever Party slot was last selected during an in-battle switch, whereas selecting `GIVE` from the `BAG` always starts the Party cursor on **Slot 1**; **NEVER press `Up` from Slot 1 on the Party screen because `Up` wraps vertically to `CANCEL` (and `Left` from `CANCEL` stays on `CANCEL`!)**—instead, press `Left` alone in the overworld to select Slot 1, or `Right` to move from Slot 1 to Slot 2. When backing out of a Pokémon's `SUMMARY` screen with `B`, the Party screen reappears with that Pokémon's `▶SUMMARY / SWITCH / ITEM / CANCEL` popup menu still open (press `B` a second time to close the popup before moving the Party cursor). Note that when a Pokémon knows a field move (such as `SECRET POWER`, `CUT`, `SURF`, etc.), its overworld Party popup menu inserts the field move in blue at Row 1 (`0: SUMMARY`, `1: SECRET POWER`, `2: SWITCH`, `3: ITEM`, `4: CANCEL`), so selecting `SWITCH` on that Pokémon in the overworld requires **`Down, Down, A`** (whereas a Pokémon with no field move has `SWITCH` at Row 1: `Down, A`). Note that **in battle**, switching Pokémon swaps their Party screen boxes so the **currently active Pokémon on the field is ALWAYS in the Left Box (Slot 1)** and the benched Pokémon is in the **Right Column (Slot 2)**—so switching back to a benched Pokémon in battle requires pressing **`Right, A, sleep 600, A`**! Furthermore, in battle (and during overworld `POKéMON -> SWITCH`), pressing `Right` from Slot 1 (`Left Box`) **ALWAYS highlights Row 0 of the Right Column (`Slot 2`)** regardless of prior switches (verified Turns 2694–2695, 2764), whereas in the **overworld Party screen (`BAG -> USE`)**, pressing `Right` from Slot 1 remembers the last-visited right-column row (`sPartyMenuInternal->slotId`; verified Turn 2726), so when healing the same right-column Pokémon multiple times in a row in the overworld, the cursor is already on that Pokémon when the Party screen reopens (`A` alone applies the next Potion!). Opening or closing the `BAG` or `POKéMON` menu triggers a ~2.0–2.5s screen fade; always wait `sleep 2500` after selecting or backing out of those submenus.
- **Weakening Wild Encounters Without Crit Risk (Turn 1090):** Never use `Water Gun` (40 BP) to weaken a full-HP wild Pokémon when `Bubble` (Slot 2: `Up, Right`, 20 BP) can safely chip it without risking a `1/16` critical-hit OHKO.
