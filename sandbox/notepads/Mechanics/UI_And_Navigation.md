# Verified UI, Menu & Spatial Mechanics

## Overworld, Fades & Scripting
- **Screen Fades & Real-Time Thinking:** Fades take ~1.8s (`sleep 2200–2400` *within* the same turn's `press_buttons` sequence). Because the emulator runs in real time during `[THINKING]`, any fade still occurring at the end of Turn `N` finishes completely before Turn `N+1`'s first button executes—never prepend a dummy input/sleep at the start of a new turn to wait out a prior turn's fade! *(Verified Turn 156 `#01–#48`)*.
- **Indoor Welcome Mats:** Approaching a south exit mat horizontally (`Left`/`Right`) requires TWO `Down` presses (first turns `Down`, second warps). Approach vertically from `(x, y-1)` whenever possible, and inside NPC houses step down to an open floor row before aligning `x` with the mat *(Turns 215, 224)*.
- **Multi-Warp Chaining & Long NPC Speeches:** Insert `sleep 2200–2400` immediately after an indoor warp step to continue overworld movement on the destination map. Long NPC speeches (such as Gym Guides, ~10–12 pages) eat subsequent directional inputs if under-counted *(Turn 515)*; clear with `12x ["B", "sleep 650"]` (harmless no-ops once closed) or verify `mgba.get_coordinates()` changes on the first post-dialogue step.

- **Python Script Hygiene:** Keep `run_code` scripts bounded (`<60` buttons, `<40s` total sleeps) to stay well clear of the 150-button and 120s script limits, and **always use a bounded `for _ in range(N):` loop (never an uncapped `while`)** when polling `mgba.get_coordinates()` so unexpected trainer `!` sight never spins to 150 buttons *(Turn 907)*.

## Menus, Bag & Berries
- **Cursor Persistence & Auto-Refill Reordering:** Both **Start Menu** (last row) and **Bag** (last pocket + per-pocket item row, including in-battle pocket switches!) persist across warps/battles. `BAG -> USE` preserves the Party cursor (`gPartyMenu.slotId`) **ONLY while remaining inside the same `BAG` session**—closing `BAG` to `Start`/overworld resets `gPartyMenu.slotId` back to **`Slot 1`**, just like `BAG -> GIVE` and `Start -> POKéMON` *(Verified Turn 839–841; always use `Left + (k-1)x Down` on the first `USE` after opening `BAG`!)*. **Caution:** Overworld auto-refill places `RARE CANDY x99` at **Row 0** of `ITEMS`, shifting other items down *(Turn 153 `#51`)*.
- **Summary `NEXT LV.` Multi-Slot Scan, Exit Quirk & `1x` Held-Item Icon Caution:** In `POKéMON -> SUMMARY`, pressing `Right` opens `Page 2 (POKéMON SKILLS)` showing exact integer `NEXT LV.` EXP and held `ITEM` (`Page 1` does NOT list held `ITEM` in Emerald; at `1x` native filmstrip resolution, Mudkip's orange cheek-fin and Hoppip's leaf sit right on the `4x4` held-item icon spot, so always verify held items on `10x` `<CurrentScreen>` or `SUMMARY Page 2` — *Turns 872, 964–965*), and pressing `Down` cycles through each party member directly on `Page 2` *(Verified Turn 662 `#21–#31`)*. Exiting `SUMMARY` with `B` (`sleep 2500`) returns to `PARTY` **with the `SUMMARY/SWITCH/ITEM/CANCEL` popup STILL OPEN** (`►SUMMARY`)! Press a second `B` (`sleep 300`) to close the popup before moving `Up`/`Down` between party slots *(Verified Turn 133 `#75–#80`)*.
- **Party `SWITCH` Animation & Distance-2 Step-In for Hidden Items / Stationary NPCs:** The two-sprite swap slide in `POKéMON -> SWITCH` takes ~1.6s (`sleep 2200` required before `B` or a second `SWITCH`, verified Turns 389 & 397). Pressing a direction into an occupied NPC/desk tile triggers a wall-bump stride rather than an instant turn and can eat an immediate `A` press *(Turns 503, 507)*; always approach both walkable hidden-item tiles and stationary NPCs `(tx, ty)` by stepping from `(tx - 2*dx, ty - 2*dy)` onto the empty adjacent tile `(tx - dx, ty - dy)` so Gem lands cleanly facing `(tx, ty)` *(Verified Turns 405, 508)*.
- **Options Binary Toggles & Intro Bedroom Clock/Houses:** `Left` and `Right` both wrap/toggle 2-state options; visually inspect before pressing. In the 2F bedroom Wall Clock UI, pressing `A` opens `"Is this the correct time?"` defaulting to **`►NO`**, so confirm with **`A -> Up -> A`** *(Turns 728–729)*. Playing as the Girl protagonist places our home in the **East house `(14, 8)`** (`1F stairs (2, 2)`, `2F PC (8, 1)`, `Wall Clock (3, 0)` from `(3, 2)`) and **Rival Brendan** in the **West house `(5, 8)`** (`1F stairs (8, 2)`, `2F Poké Ball trigger at (3, 4)` — *Turns 728–732*).

  - **Harvesting:** Requires **`3x A`** (`1: Interact` -> `2: "You found X BERRIES!"` -> `3: "Do you want to pick...?" [YES]`), then `sleep 3500` for jingle, then **`3x B`** (use `B` afterward to avoid opening replant prompt) *(Turns 178–179)*.
