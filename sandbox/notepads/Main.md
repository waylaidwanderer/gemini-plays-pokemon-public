## Engine / Harness Mechanics Verified
- Start Menu Memory: In Pokémon Emerald, the Start Menu remembers the last selected option (e.g. BAG or POKéMON) rather than resetting to POKéDEX.
- TM/HM Move Replacement Workflow: Booting HM/TM in Bag -> select ABLE Pokémon -> confirm 'Delete a move' (default YES) -> BATTLE MOVES screen displays moves vertically -> select move to replace -> confirm deletion (default YES) -> teaches new move cleanly.
- HM02 Fly Field Usage: HM02 Fly can only be used outdoors. Accessed via Start -> POKéMON -> select flyer (Guava) -> FLY option appears at top of pop-up menu.
- Hoenn Fly Map Coordinates: From Fortree City, Fallarbor Town is located exactly 3 grid steps Left along the northern latitude (step 1=Route 119, step 2=Route 111, step 3=Fallarbor Town). Confirming with A on Fallarbor Town prompts 'Fly to FALLARBOR TOWN?' with default YES.
- Giant Tall Grass Mechanics: In Gen 3 (RSE), giant tall grass cannot be entered while mounted on a Bicycle (the bike is completely blocked by the grass). The player must dismount (press Select) onto foot to traverse giant tall grass.
- Mach Bike In-Place Turning: Tapping a directional button while stationary on the Mach Bike turns the player in place without taking a step. On foot, pressing a direction takes a step immediately.
- Route 114 Topology & Navigation: The col 29 eastern path from row 44 is blocked at (29, 47) by a stationary unsmashable boulder and Acro Bike rails. The correct progression route to the lower valley floor and Meteor Falls uses the Western Stairs at (15, 49..50) -> Western Terrace col 12 -> row 55 -> col 17 -> row 60 dirt highway -> white staircase at (24, 60) down to (24, 61).
- Soft Loamy Soil Collision: Soft loamy soil tiles (empty or planted) are impassable terrain in Gen 3. Walking into an empty soil tile triggers the berry planting prompt ("Want to plant a BERRY?"). To navigate around berry patches, route along adjacent grass/sand tiles (e.g. row 6).
- Map transitions/warps automatically abort pending movement sequences.
- Button sequences must place `sleep` strictly between button taps (no leading/trailing sleeps).
- In PC menu, Home PC directly opens player's item storage.
- Birch bag starter order: Left = Treecko, Middle = Torchic (default), Right = Mudkip.
- Poké Mart interior: Customer counter is at (1, 4), talk to shopkeeper from (1, 5) facing Up.
- Oldale Town Mart does not stock Poké Balls prior to receiving the Pokédex.
- Outdoor Mart clerk (with free promotional Potion sample) stands in the south-central area of Oldale Town near (14, 14).
- In Gen 3, all Grass-type moves (such as Absorb) are Special, so their damage is unaffected by physical Attack drops (Growl).
- Medicine use outside battle: Start -> BAG -> select item -> USE -> select Pokémon.
- Text speed set to FAST in game Options menu (verified turn 417).
- Berry harvesting: When inspecting ripe berry trees, the prompt "Do you want to pick the [BERRY]?" defaults cursor to YES. Use exclusively A presses with ~500ms delays to inspect and confirm YES without any risk of B cancelling. Once 'JADE picked...' appears, use B presses to advance and dismiss text, which prevents accidentally opening the planting menu on newly emptied soil. Interacting with empty soft loamy soil prompts to plant berries from the Bag.
- Mart purchasing bonus: Buying 10 or more Poké Balls at once awards 1 bonus Premier Ball.
- Match Call (PokéNav): Devon Researcher installs Match Call upon exiting Devon Corp. Contacts registered: #1 Mr. Stone, #2 Brendan (Rustboro south exit), #3 Dad / Norman (received incoming call during boat voyage to Dewford). When navigating PokéNav or phone calls, pressing B advances dialogue and safely cancels out of menus without triggering repeat calls.
- Field Move Mechanics: Teleport and Dig cannot be used inside dungeons, caves, or Pokémon Gyms ('Can't use that here.'). HM04 Strength also cannot be activated inside Pokémon Gyms ('Can't use that here.'). Statues in Gyms are puzzle elements rotated by switches, not movable Strength boulders. Escape Ropes or walking must be used to exit.
- Battle Move Layout (Sage): Slot 1 (top-left) = Fury Cutter, Slot 2 (top-right) = Pursuit, Slot 3 (bottom-left) = Leaf Blade, Slot 4 (bottom-right) = Quick Attack.
- Battle Move Memory: In Pokémon Emerald, opening the FIGHT menu automatically remembers and defaults cursor to the move used on the previous turn.
- Naming Screen Mechanics: Pressing Start moves the cursor directly to the [ OK ] button on the right; pressing A on [ OK ] confirms and submits the name. Pressing B activates [BACK] (erases previous character).
- PokéNav Navigation: On the PokéNav main menu (HOENN MAP, CONDITION, MATCH CALL, SWITCH OFF), pressing B does NOT exit. You must select 'SWITCH OFF' (accessible immediately by pressing Up from HOENN MAP to wrap to the bottom) to close the PokéNav.
- Whiteout Mechanics: Whiting out restores the entire party to 100% HP and PP, placing the player right outside the last visited Pokémon Center.
- In-Battle Menu Timing: Using A to advance combat text can cause race conditions if the battle menu appears during thinking frames, leading to accidental move selection. Using B to dismiss combat text avoids accidental selections because B does not confirm menu options.
- Gen 3 Berry Mechanics: In Generation 3 (R/S/E), Berries cannot be used directly as medicine from the Bag outside of battle. They can only be held by a Pokémon to trigger automatically in battle, planted in soft soil, or blended into Pokéblocks.
- Evolution Mechanics: Pressing B during the evolution animation cancels evolution; avoid pressing B to allow evolution to finish safely.
- Special Move Mechanics: In Gen 3, all Grass-type moves (including Leaf Blade) are Special, so enemy physical Attack drops (Growl) have no effect on their damage.
- Multi-Step D-Pad Navigation: Consecutive identical D-pad presses require a 'sleep' (e.g. 'sleep 100') between them to ensure key release is registered by the emulator; without sleeps, multiple identical directional presses only result in a single turn/step.
- TM/HM Replacement Navigation: In Gen 3, teaching a TM/HM to a Pokémon with 4 moves displays the BATTLE MOVES screen. Move selection box starts on move 1; pressing Down navigates to the move to replace. Confirming with A prompts 'Delete [MOVE] and make room for [NEW MOVE]?' with default YES. Confirming deletes old move and teaches new move.
- Menu Input Timing: When transitioning between sub-menus (e.g. PokéNav to Start menu, or Bag to Party), allow ~300-400ms for UI animations to settle before sending navigation presses.
- Double Battle Priority & Targeting: Quick Attack (+1 priority) executes before normal moves regardless of paralysis speed reduction. When a single opponent remains in a double battle, single-target attacks automatically default target to that opponent.
- Mid-Battle Move Replacement: When learning a move upon level up with 4 moves, selecting No to "Stop learning" and Yes to "Delete a move" opens the vertical Battle Moves list. HM moves (Cut, Rock Smash) cannot be deleted; regular moves (like Mud-Slap) can be replaced.
- Pokémon Center Nurse Joy Healing: Opening text requires A presses to advance to the YES/NO prompt. Never press B near the prompt as B selects NO and cancels healing. Allow ~4.0s for the healing animation and jingle before pressing B to dismiss completion text.
- File Encoding: When accessing markdown files in python scripts, always specify encoding='utf-8' to safely handle gender symbols (♂, ♀).
- Python Console Encoding: When printing strings with gender symbols (♂, ♀) in python scripts on Windows, call sys.stdout.reconfigure(encoding='utf-8') to avoid charmap UnicodeEncodeErrors.
- Battle Menu Root Cursor: When closing sub-menus (like BAG) during battle with B, the root battle menu remembers cursor position on BAG. Pressing Down from BAG navigates directly to RUN.
- Party Screen TM/HM Navigation: In the Party screen, Slot 1 (lead) is on the left; Slot 2 is on the top right. Pressing Right from Slot 1 moves directly across to Slot 2. Allow ~1.5s for the Bag-to-Party transition to settle before sending directional inputs.
- Field Surf Mechanics: In Gen 3 (RSE), interacting with water using 'A' while mounted on the Bicycle directly triggers the Surf prompt ('Would you like to SURF?'). Confirming YES automatically transitions the player into Surfing without requiring manual dismounting.
- Field Dive Mechanics: Pressing 'B' while surfing on the surface does NOT activate Dive (B is only used while underwater to surface). To submerge from the surface, stand on a dark indigo water tile and either interact with 'A' (triggers prompt: 'The sea is deep here. Would you like to use DIVE?') or open the Party Menu (`Start -> POKéMON -> LILZ -> DIVE`).
