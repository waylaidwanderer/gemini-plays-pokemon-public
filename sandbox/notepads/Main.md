## Mechanics & Harness Notes
- **Teaching TMs & Global Bag Pocket Persistence (`gBagPosition`):** Teaching a TM from the `TMs & HMs` Pocket requires **5 `A` presses** to open the Party screen (`USE` -> `"Booted up a TM."` -> `"It contained [MOVE]."` -> `[YES / NO]` -> confirms `YES`) before pressing `Left` to select Lead Slot 1. Both in-battle and overworld `BAG` menus share global `gBagPosition` (`pocket` and `cursorPosition`), so switching pockets in battle persists to the next overworld `BAG` opening and vice versa. After using a Bag item in battle, wait `6000ms` after declining `"Will ORION change POKéMON?"` before pressing `Right` to select `BAG`, press `Left` on the Party screen to guarantee Lead Slot 1 is selected, and on the next turn press `Left` before `A` on the battle menu because the action cursor persists on `BAG` (top-right) after using an item.
- **Naming Screen Grid (7 cols x 4 rows, Select = toggle case, Start = jump to OK):**
  - Row 0: `A B C D E F .` (cols 0..6)
  - Row 1: `G H I J K L ,` (cols 0..6)
  - Row 2: `M N O P Q R S` (cols 0..6)
  - Row 3: `T U V W X Y Z` (cols 0..6)
- **Pokémon Fanfare Auto-Advance:** The `"received/caught [POKéMON]!"` fanfare text box automatically advances to the `"give a nickname?" [YES / NO]` prompt once the jingle finishes; press `A` only ONCE to enter the naming screen.
- **Overworld Warp Transitions:** Stepping onto a warp/door aborts remaining `press_buttons` inputs and leaves the next turn at the start of the fade-in; prepend `sleep 1000` when chaining actions immediately after a warp. Exiting an exterior building door automatically steps the player 1 tile `Down`.
- **Clearing NPC Dialogue Safely:** When facing an NPC, use `B` (not `A`) to advance and close the final text box so extra presses cannot accidentally re-initiate dialogue.
- **Pokémon Center Healing Sequence:** Nurse Joy has 3 opening lines before `[YES / NO]` appears; press `A` **4 times** (`A, sleep 1000, A, sleep 1000, A, sleep 1000, A, sleep 6500`), then press `B` 4 times (`B, sleep 1000` x4) to clear her closing dialogue safely.
- **Trainer Mid-Battle Faint Sequence (With 2+ Party Pokémon):** Use **5 `B` presses** for neutral KOs (`B, sleep 3000, B, sleep 1800, B, sleep 1800, B, sleep 1800, B, sleep 5500`) or **6 `B` presses** for super-effective / not-very-effective / critical-hit KOs (`B, sleep 2000` prepended for the effectiveness/crit box) to clear fainted text, full EXP bar animation, trainer's next Pokémon announcement, and `"Will ORION change POKéMON?"` (`No`). Use `5500ms` after the final `B` so longer Pokémon send-out animations/cries complete before pressing `A` on the battle menu.
- **Berry Tree Harvesting Sequence:** Requires **3 `A` presses** (`A` #1: found berries, `A` #2: opens `[YES / NO]` prompt, `A` #3: confirms `YES` to pick berries), followed by `sleep 3500` and `B` x 3.
- **Overworld Bag & Party Cursor Persistence:** Both the Start menu and Party menu remember their last cursor index across the session (`slotId` in the Party menu can start on Slot 2 `OBERON` after catching/viewing a Pokémon; press `Left` in the Party menu to move from the right column back to Lead Slot 1 `ATRITON`, and never press `Up` from Lead Slot 1 because `Up` wraps around to the `CANCEL` button).
- **Python `run_code` Sleep & Timeout Budget:** Each `"sleep <ms>"` entry in `press_buttons` must be `<= 10000` ms, and cumulative sleeps across a single `run_code` script should stay `<= 85s` so emulator button-press overhead never hits the `120s` script timeout.
- **Double Battle Single-Mon Bypass:** Overworld Double Battle trainers (such as Twins Gina & Mia on the Route 104 North bridge) cannot initiate a battle if the player has only 1 conscious Pokémon (`GetMonsStateToDoubles() != PLAYER_HAS_TWO_USABLE_MONS`).
- **Evolution Screen Safety:** Never press `B` when a Pokémon is about to evolve after battle (`B` cancels evolution); use `A` to advance post-battle victory text when a level-up triggers evolution.
- **Side-Talking Trainers to Avoid Double Battles:** When two Single Battle trainers have overlapping lines of sight (such as Youngster Johnson at `(36, 13)` and Hiker Clark at `(36, 17)` on Route 116), walking up to the side of one trainer and pressing `A` initiates a 1v1 Single Battle instead of stepping into their shared line of sight for a 2v2 Double Battle.
- **Double Battle Target Selection & Mid-Turn Replacements:** Single-target moves (such as `Mud Shot` or `Tackle`) require **3 `A` presses** per Pokémon in a Double Battle (`A` for `FIGHT`, `A` for the move slot, and `A` to confirm the default enemy target). On the target selection screen, the cursor defaults to `B_POSITION_OPPONENT_LEFT` (the **upper enemy HP box / right-side enemy sprite**); press `Left` to target `B_POSITION_OPPONENT_RIGHT` (the **lower enemy HP box / left-side enemy sprite**), and never press `Up` or `Down` (which moves the cursor onto your own ally `B_POSITION_PLAYER_RIGHT`, especially when only one enemy remains on the field and is already highlighted by default). In the Double Battle Party screen, both active battlers occupy the left column (`Slot 0` top-left, `Slot 1` bottom-left) while the 3rd party member is at `Slot 2` top-right (press `Right` from `Slot 0`). Also, in Gen 3 Double Battles, when a Pokémon faints mid-turn, EXP/level-ups and enemy replacement send-outs occur immediately before slower Pokémon on the field execute their actions for that turn.
- **Post-Battle Match Call Registration:** After obtaining the PokéNav, defeating a Match Call-eligible trainer triggers an automatic overworld dialogue box immediately after the post-battle fade-in (including a ~3s registration fanfare) that must be cleared with `B` before overworld movement can resume.
- **Overworld Rock Smash Sequence:** Facing a breakable boulder and pressing `A` prints `"This rock appears to be breakable. Would you like to use ROCK SMASH?"` and opens `[YES / NO]` (~1200ms); wait `1500ms` after the first `A` before pressing `A` a second time to confirm `YES` (`A, sleep 1500, A, sleep 1000, B, sleep 3500`).
- **Declining a New Move on Level-Up (4 Moves Known):** When `"Delete a move to make room for [MOVE]?" [Yes / No]` appears, press `B` (`No`), which opens `"Stop learning [MOVE]?" [Yes / No]` with the cursor on `Yes`; you must press **`A`** (not `B`) on `"Stop learning [MOVE]?"` to confirm `Yes` and skip the move.
- **Gen 3 Physical/Special Split, Stat-Drop Pivoting & Non-Wrapping 2x2 Menus:** In Gen 3, Ground (`Mud Shot`), Rock (`Rock Tomb`), and Fighting (`Rock Smash`) are **Physical** (affected by `Intimidate`), whereas Water (`Water Gun`) is **Special** (ignores `Intimidate`). Switching a Pokémon out and back in clears all `Intimidate` Attack drops and `Sand-Attack` Accuracy drops. Neither the 2x2 battle action menu nor the 2x2 move menu wraps around, so corner pairs (`Up, Left` for `FIGHT`/Slot 1, `Up, Right` for Slot 2, `Down, Right` for Slot 4) deterministically select the intended option regardless of prior cursor memory.
