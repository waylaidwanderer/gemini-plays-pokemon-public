## Mechanics & Harness Notes
- **Teaching TMs & Global Bag Pocket Persistence (`gBagPosition`):** Teaching a TM from the `TMs & HMs` Pocket requires **5 `A` presses** to open the Party screen (`USE` -> `"Booted up a TM."` -> `"It contained [MOVE]."` -> `[YES / NO]` -> confirms `YES`) before pressing `Left` to select Lead Slot 1. If the Pokémon has <4 moves, it learns the move immediately (1 text box: `"[MON] learned [MOVE]!"` with ~4000ms fanfare); if replacing an existing move, after selecting the move slot and waiting `4500ms` for the Poof/fanfare, **3 `B` presses** (`B, sleep 1200, B, sleep 1500, B, sleep 1200`) are required to clear `"1, 2, and... ... ... Poof!"`, `"forgot how to use [OLD MOVE]."`, and `"And... [MON] learned [MOVE]!"` back to the `TMs & HMs` Pocket. Both in-battle and overworld `BAG` menus share global `gBagPosition` (`pocket` and `cursorPosition`), so switching pockets in battle persists to the next overworld `BAG` opening and vice versa. After using a Bag item in battle, wait `6000ms` after declining `"Will ORION change POKéMON?"` before pressing `Right` to select `BAG`, press `Left` on the Party screen to guarantee Lead Slot 1 is selected, and on the next turn press `Left` before `A` on the battle menu because the action cursor persists on `BAG` (top-right) after using an item.
- **Naming Screen Grid (7 cols x 4 rows, Select = toggle case, Start = jump to OK):**
  - Row 0: `A B C D E F .` (cols 0..6)
  - Row 1: `G H I J K L ,` (cols 0..6)
  - Row 2: `M N O P Q R S` (cols 0..6)
  - Row 3: `T U V W X Y Z` (cols 0..6)
- **Pokémon Fanfare Auto-Advance:** The `"received/caught [POKéMON]!"` fanfare text box automatically advances to the `"give a nickname?" [YES / NO]` prompt once the jingle finishes; press `A` only ONCE to enter the naming screen.
- **Overworld Warp Transitions:** Stepping onto a warp/door aborts remaining `press_buttons` inputs and leaves the next turn at the start of the fade-in; prepend `sleep 1000` (or `sleep 2000` for exterior doors that auto-step 1 tile `Down`) when chaining actions immediately after a warp. In `run_code`, `mgba.get_coordinates()` called immediately after a warp-aborted `press_buttons` still returns the pre-warp coordinates until the next `press_buttons` call advances the emulator through the fade transition; appending a second `tools.press_buttons(["sleep 1500", "B"])` call right after a warp settles the screen before the turn ends so the next turn starts with a clean overworld `<CurrentScreen>` and `latest_grid.png`.
- **Clearing NPC Dialogue Safely:** When facing an NPC, use `B` (not `A`) to advance and close the final text box so extra presses cannot accidentally re-initiate dialogue. For overworld trainer triggers with 3–4 pre-battle text boxes, use `B, sleep 10000, B, sleep 6500` (or `B, sleep 9500` if the trainer's lead has `Intimidate` or weather messages play) after clearing their pre-battle dialogue so `"would like to battle!"`, the enemy send-out, and `"Go! ATRITON!"` finish completely before pressing `A` on `FIGHT`.
- **Pokémon Center Healing Sequence:** Nurse Joy has 3 opening lines before `[YES / NO]` appears; press `A` **4 times** (`A, sleep 1000, A, sleep 1000, A, sleep 1000, A, sleep 6500`), then press `B` 4 times (`B, sleep 1000` x4) to clear her closing dialogue safely.
- **Trainer Mid-Battle Faint Sequence (With 2+ Party Pokémon):** Use **5 `B` presses** for neutral KOs (`B, sleep 3000, B, sleep 1800, B, sleep 1800, B, sleep 1800, B, sleep 5500`) or **6 `B` presses** for super-effective / not-very-effective / critical-hit KOs (`B, sleep 2000` prepended for the effectiveness/crit box) to clear fainted text, full EXP bar animation, trainer's next Pokémon announcement, and `"Will ORION change POKéMON?"` (`No`). Use `5500ms` after the final `B` so longer Pokémon send-out animations/cries complete before pressing `A` on the battle menu.
- **Berry Tree Harvesting Sequence:** Requires **3 `A` presses** (`A` #1: found berries, `A` #2: opens `[YES / NO]` prompt, `A` #3: confirms `YES` to pick berries), followed by `sleep 3500` and `B` x 3.
- **Overworld Bag & Party Cursor Persistence:** Opening `POKéMON` from the Start menu always starts the cursor on Lead Slot 0 (`ATRITON`), whereas opening the Party menu from the `BAG` (when using an item or teaching a TM/HM) starts on `gLastViewedMonIndex` (`slotId` can start on Slot 2+ after catching/viewing a Pokémon; press `Left` in the Party menu to move from the right column back to Lead Slot 1 `ATRITON`, and never press `Up` from Lead Slot 1 because `Up` wraps around to the `CANCEL` button). Backing out of the Bag to the overworld after using an item on the Party screen requires `B, sleep 1800` (closes heal text and fades Party -> Bag), `B, sleep 1800` (closes Bag and fades Bag -> Overworld Start menu), and `B, sleep 1000` (closes Start menu). In a Pokémon's Party context menu, `SUMMARY` is index 0 and field moves (`FLY`, `ROCK SMASH`, `SURF`) follow starting at index 1. On the Hoenn Region Map (`FLY to where?`), horizontal taps scroll 3 grid cells (`±3`) while vertical taps scroll 2 grid cells (`±2`), clamped at `X_MIN=1, X_MAX=28, Y_MIN=2, Y_MAX=16`. Because `X_MIN=1` and `X_MAX=28` both have `x ≡ 1 (mod 3)` (`x ∈ {1, 4, 7, 10, 13, 16, 19, 22, 25, 28}`), use 2-tile-wide **Mauville City `(9..10, 8)`** as a horizontal parity bridge: fly to `(10, 8)` (lands at `Mauville (22, 6)` in the eastern map section `grid_x=10`), then walk `Left` 3+ to `Mauville (19, 6)` (western map section `grid_x=9`, `0 mod 3`) to reach `x ∈ {3, 6, 9, 12}`. Similarly, because `Y_MIN=2` and `Y_MAX=16` have the same vertical parity and vertical taps scroll `±2`, vertical parity (`y mod 2`) is invariant unless bridged via a 2-cell-tall city such as **Rustboro City `(1, 8..9)`**: clamping `["Left"]*10 + ["Up"]*10` and stepping `Down` along `x=1` is guaranteed by the Pigeonhole Principle to land on 2-cell-tall Rustboro City; landing at the Rustboro Pokémon Center `(16, 39)` places the player in the southern cell `(1, 9)` (odd `y`), whereas walking north to `(20, 19)` (`y < 20`) places the player in the northern cell `(1, 8)` (even `y`), from which `["Right"]*10 + ["Left"]` moves horizontally along `y=8` directly onto **Mossdeep City `(25, 8)`**. Key Fly grid coordinates: Rustboro `(1, 7..8)` (landing at `(16, 39)` starts the Fly cursor at `(1, 7)`, from which `["Right"]*6 + ["Up"]` with a single `Up` lands on **Lilycove City `(19, 5)`**; pressing a 2nd `Up` clamps to `Y_MIN=2` even parity), Petalburg `(2, 11)`, Fallarbor `(4, 2)`, Verdanturf `(5, 8)`, Oldale `(5, 11)`, Littleroot `(5, 13)`, Lavaridge `(6, 5)`, Mauville `(9..10, 8)`, Slateport `(9, 12..13)`, Fortree `(13, 2)`, Lilycove `(19..20, 5)`, Mossdeep `(25..26, 8)` (reached deterministically from any map position via `["Right"]*10 + ["Left"] + ["Up"]*10 + ["Down", "Down"]`, i.e., **2 `Down` presses** from the top clamp), **Ever Grande City — Pokémon League `(28, 10)`** (reached deterministically from any map position via `["Right"]*10 + ["Up"]*10 + ["Down"]*4`, i.e., **4 `Down` presses** from the top-right clamp).
- **Python `run_code` Sleep & Timeout Budget:** Each `"sleep <ms>"` entry in `press_buttons` must be `<= 10000` ms, and cumulative sleeps across a single `run_code` script should stay `<= 85s` so emulator button-press overhead never hits the `120s` script timeout.
- **Double Battle Single-Mon Bypass:** Overworld Double Battle trainers (such as Twins Gina & Mia on the Route 104 North bridge) cannot initiate a battle if the player has only 1 conscious Pokémon (`GetMonsStateToDoubles() != PLAYER_HAS_TWO_USABLE_MONS`).
- **Evolution Screen Safety:** Never press `B` when a Pokémon is about to evolve after battle (`B` cancels evolution); use `A` to advance post-battle victory text when a level-up triggers evolution.
- **Side-Talking Trainers to Avoid Double Battles:** When two Single Battle trainers have overlapping lines of sight (such as Youngster Johnson at `(36, 13)` and Hiker Clark at `(36, 17)` on Route 116), walking up to the side of one trainer and pressing `A` initiates a 1v1 Single Battle instead of stepping into their shared line of sight for a 2v2 Double Battle.
- **Double Battle Target Selection & Mid-Turn Replacements:** Single-target moves (such as `Mud Shot` or `Tackle`) require **3 `A` presses** per Pokémon in a Double Battle (`A` for `FIGHT`, `A` for the move slot, and `A` to confirm the default enemy target), whereas dual-enemy spread moves (`MOVE_TARGET_BOTH`, such as `Surf`, which in Gen 3 hits both opponents with a 0.5x modifier and does NOT hit your ally) skip target selection and require **only 2 `A` presses**. On the single-target selection screen, the cursor defaults to `B_POSITION_OPPONENT_LEFT` (the **upper enemy HP box / right-side enemy sprite**); press `Left` to target `B_POSITION_OPPONENT_RIGHT` (the **lower enemy HP box / left-side enemy sprite**), and never press `Up` or `Down` (which moves the cursor onto your own ally `B_POSITION_PLAYER_RIGHT`, especially when only one enemy remains on the field and is already highlighted by default). In the Double Battle Party screen, both active battlers occupy the left column (`Slot 0` top-left, `Slot 1` bottom-left) while the 3rd party member is at `Slot 2` top-right (press `Right` from `Slot 0`). Also, in Gen 3 Double Battles, when a Pokémon faints mid-turn, EXP/level-ups and enemy replacement send-outs occur immediately before slower Pokémon on the field execute their actions for that turn.
- **Post-Battle Match Call Registration:** After obtaining the PokéNav, defeating a Match Call-eligible trainer triggers an automatic overworld dialogue box immediately after the post-battle fade-in (including a ~3s registration fanfare) that must be cleared with `B` before overworld movement can resume.
- **Overworld Rock Smash, Surf, Strength & Dive Sequences:** Facing a breakable boulder or water edge and pressing `A` prints `"Would you like to use ROCK SMASH? / SURF?"` and opens `[YES / NO]` (~1200ms); wait `1500ms` after the first `A` before pressing `A` a second time to confirm `YES`, then wait `1000ms` and press `B` to close `"used ROCK SMASH! / used SURF!"` and play the field move animation (`A, sleep 1500, A, sleep 1000, B, sleep 3500`). For **Strength** (`HM04`), interacting with a boulder before Strength is active requires `A, sleep 1500, A, sleep 1500, A, sleep 3000, B, sleep 1500, B, sleep 1000`. Once Strength is active, **every boulder push temporarily locks player movement and aborts the current `press_buttons` call**; in `run_code`, place each boulder push at the end of its own `tools.press_buttons(...)` call and start the next call with `"sleep 1000"`. For **Dive** (`HM08`), while surfing on dark-blue deep water tiles (`MB_DIVEABLE`), ensure you are not facing an adjacent NPC/Swimmer (facing an adjacent NPC prioritizes talking to the NPC over opening the Dive prompt; step onto an unobstructed trench tile first), then press **`A`** to dive down (`"The sea is deep here. Would you like to use DIVE?" [YES / NO]`, followed by `sleep 1500, A, sleep 1000, B, sleep 4000`); while underwater beneath light-filtered surface tiles, press **`B`** to surface (`"Light is filtering down from above. Would you like to use DIVE?" [YES / NO]`, followed by `sleep 1500, A, sleep 1000, B, sleep 4000`; never append a stray `"B"` press after underwater movement unless you intend to surface). For **Waterfall** (`HM07`), bumping into a waterfall tile (`MB_WATERFALL`) with `Up` from the base tile (`(18, 68)`) triggers a pushback current that automatically turns the player `Down` (`DIR_SOUTH`); instead, step `Down` 1 tile to `(18, 69)`, take a single `Up` step onto `(18, 68)` without pressing `Up` again, and press **`A`** (`A, sleep 1500, A, sleep 1000, B, sleep 6500`).
- **Declining a New Move on Level-Up (4 Moves Known):** When `"Delete a move to make room for [MOVE]?" [Yes / No]` appears, press `B` (`No`), which opens `"Stop learning [MOVE]?" [Yes / No]` with the cursor on `Yes`; you must press **`A`** (not `B`) on `"Stop learning [MOVE]?"` to confirm `Yes` and skip the move.
- **Gen 3 Physical/Special Split, Stat-Drop Pivoting, Non-Wrapping 2x2 Menus & Full Restore Bypass:** In Gen 3, Ground (`Mud Shot`), Rock (`Rock Tomb`), and Fighting (`Rock Smash`) are **Physical** (affected by `Intimidate`), whereas Water (`Water Gun`, `Surf`, `Waterfall`) is **Special** (ignores `Intimidate`). Switching a Pokémon out and back in clears all `Intimidate` Attack drops, `Icy Wind`/`Rock Tomb` Speed drops, and `Sand-Attack` Accuracy drops. Neither the 2x2 battle action menu nor the 2x2 move menu wraps around, so corner pairs (`Up, Left` for `FIGHT`/Slot 1, `Up, Right` for Slot 2, `Down, Right` for Slot 4) deterministically select the intended option regardless of prior cursor memory. Trainer AI only uses `Full Restore` when its active Pokémon starts a turn below 25% HP (red bar); chipping first (`Waterfall` ~17% -> `Return` ~46% -> `Return` ~46% on Drake's `Kingdra Lv. 53`) transitions the target from ~37% HP (>25%) straight to 0% HP without triggering `Full Restore`.
- **Wild Battle Faint Prompt & Trainer Replacement Screen:** In a wild battle, when your active Pokémon faints, `"Use next POKéMON?" [Yes / No]` appears (`B` flees, `A` opens the Party menu), whereas in a Trainer battle `"Choose a POKéMON."` opens directly with no `[Yes / No]` prompt. On the Party screen, pressing `Left` always jumps to Lead Slot 0 (left column), from which pressing `Up` wraps to `CANCEL` (bottom-right) and subsequent `Up` presses deterministically select slots from the bottom of the right column upward (`Up` x2 = Slot 5, `Up` x3 = Slot 4, `Up` x4 = Slot 3, `Up` x5 = Slot 2, `Up` x6 = Slot 1). Switching out immediately clears all volatile statuses (`Curse`, `Torment`, `Attract`, `Confusion`, `Encore`, `Leech Seed`, `Grudge` target lock, and stat/accuracy drops).
