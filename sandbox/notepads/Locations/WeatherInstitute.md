# Location: Weather Institute

## Overview
A large meteorological research institute located in northern Route 119 beside the river. Currently occupied by Team Aqua, who are searching for weather-controlling Pokémon and blocking the bridge east toward Fortree City.

## Connection Points
- South Exit: Front entrance doors at (9, 12) lead outside to Route 119 (rows 30..35).
- 2F Stairs: Located in the western area of 1F leading up to 2F.

## Key Landmarks & Features
- 1F Lobby: Entrance mat at (9, 12). Partition wall with archway at cols 5..6, row 8 leading into main research floor.
- 2F Recovery Bed: Located in the back room on 2F; allows full party recovery (HP, PP, status conditions).
- 2F Admin Office: Site of confrontation with Team Aqua Admin Shelly.
- Reward: Castform (Lv 25) holding Mystic Water gifted by head scientist after defeating Team Aqua.

## Progression Checklist - Weather Institute

3. [ ] Defeat Team Aqua Grunts on 1F.
4. [ ] Ascend to 2F and defeat Team Aqua Grunts and Admin Shelly.
