# Location: Route 117

## Overview
An east-west route connecting Mauville City (east) to Verdanturf Town (west). Famous for the Hoenn Pokémon Day Care, extensive flower beds, berry patches, and numerous trainers.

## Connection Points
- East Entrance: Mauville City (boundary at x=59, y=8..10).
- West Entrance: Verdanturf Town.

## Key Landmarks
- Pokémon Day Care: Located on the eastern half of Route 117. Offers Pokémon breeding and leveling. Outside fenced pen allows viewing deposited Pokémon.
- Berry Patches: Rich soil beds for harvesting and planting berries.
- Verdanturf Town Gate/Border: Western exit leading into Verdanturf Town.
