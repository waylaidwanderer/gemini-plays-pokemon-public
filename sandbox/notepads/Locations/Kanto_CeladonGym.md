# Celadon City Gym (Erika)

## Overview
Grass-type Pokémon Gym led by Erika. Trainers use Grass and Poison-type Pokémon.
Defeating Erika awards the Rainbowbadge and TM21 (Mega Drain).
Rainbowbadge allows Pokémon up to Level 50 to obey, and permits the use of Strength (HM04) outside battle.

## Geography & Layout
- Entrance Mat: (4..5, 17). Exits south to Celadon City.
- Statues: (3, 14..15) and (6, 14..15).
- Central Aisle: Cols 4..5 running north from entrance mat.
- Shrubs / Trees: Rows at (2..3, 13) and (6..7, 13), with central gap at cols 4..5.
- Flower patches: Flank the entrance along row 13 at cols 0..1 and 8..9.
