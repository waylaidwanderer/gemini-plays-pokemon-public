# Location: Route 112

## Overview
A rugged, mountainous route wrapping around the southern base of Mt. Chimney. Connects Route 111 (east) to the Fiery Path tunnel, Jagged Pass, and the Mt. Chimney Cable Car station.

## Connection Points
- East Entrance: Route 111 (boundary at x=36, y=50..52).
- North/West: Fiery Path (subterranean passage through Mt. Chimney toward Route 113).
- North/East: Cable Car Station to Mt. Chimney summit.
- Jagged Pass: Downhill path from Mt. Chimney summit leading south onto Route 112.
