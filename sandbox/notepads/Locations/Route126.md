# Location: Route 126

## Overview
A large ocean route in southeastern Hoenn dominated by a massive white volcanic caldera rising from the sea.
- Key Landmark: Sootopolis City is nestled entirely inside the crater of the caldera!
- Marine Topography: Deep ocean waters surround the crater, with a dark indigo underwater dive trench running around its southern base.
- Story Climax: Groudon and Kyogre are clashing inside Sootopolis City, triggering catastrophic weather across Hoenn.

## Connection Points
- North: Route 124.
- East: Route 127 (connected around rows 45..55).
- Center (Underwater): Sootopolis City (entered via underwater dive passage at the southern caldera base).
