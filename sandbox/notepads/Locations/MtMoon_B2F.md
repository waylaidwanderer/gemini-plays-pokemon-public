# Mt. Moon B2F

## Overworld Layout & Navigation
- **Access Ladders:**
  - (25, 9): Leads up to B1F at (17, 11) (accessed from the starting platform).
  - (21, 17): Leads up to B1F at (21, 17) (accessed from the vertical chamber).
- **Starting Platform & Chambers:**
  - **Starting Platform:** Columns 24 to 28, rows 8 to 11. Bounded by rock walls on row 7, but connects to the vertical chamber on row 12.
  - **Vertical Chamber:** Columns 20 to 23, rows 14 to 19. Bounded by rock walls on columns 18-19 on the left and column 24 on the right. Connects to the starting platform on row 12.
  - **Upper Niche:** Columns 20 to 22, rows 5 to 7. Bounded by rock walls on row 4, column 19, and column 23.
  - **Wall Blockage:** Columns 18-19 form a solid, vertical rock wall dividing this starting platform area from the main B2F cavern.
