# Lavender Town Geography & Points of Interest

## Connections
- North: Route 10 South at cols 9..11, row 0 (Route 10 South (9..11, 71) <-> Lavender Town (9..11, 0)).
- West: Route 8.
- South: Route 12 (Silence Bridge).

## Geography & Layout
- Northern Entrance (Cols 8..11, Rows 0..2): Open green lawn entering from Route 10 South.
- Signpost at (9, 3): Silph Scope advertisement ("New SILPH SCOPE! Make the Invisible Plain to See! SILPH CO.") [Turn 4843].
- Pokémon Tower (Northeast): Large multi-story landmark building spanning cols 12..19, rows 0..11+.
- Central Lawn (Rows 6..7, Cols 2..10): Wide open east-west lawn connecting northern entrance to Pokémon Center and central buildings.
- South-Central Building: Located at cols 6..9, rows 8..9+ with orange door at (7, 9) and window at (8, 9).
- Signpost at (5, 9): Located west of south-central building.
- Signpost at (11, 9): Located east of south-central building.
- NPC at (11, 7): Stationed in eastern lawn.
