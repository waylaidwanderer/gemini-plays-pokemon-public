# Location: Ever Grande City

## Overview
The ultimate destination for Pokémon Trainers in the Hoenn region, located atop a high mountain plateau at the far eastern edge of Route 128.
- Environment: Massive stone cliffs, cascading waterfall, flower-filled mountain plateau, Victory Road cave, and Pokémon League Headquarters.
- Key Landmark: Scaled with HM07 Waterfall at Route 128 eastern boundary.

## Facilities & Key Points
- Pokémon Center (South Plateau): Outside southern entrance of Victory Road. Registers Hoenn Fly Map checkpoint.
- Victory Road: Connects south plateau to Pokémon League HQ.
- Pokémon League HQ (North Plateau): Houses the Hoenn Elite Four (Sidney, Phoebe, Glacia, Drake) and Champion Wallace.
  - Mart Counter (16, 4): Sells Revives (¥1,500), Hyper Potions (¥1,200), Full Heals (¥600).
  - Gate (9, 1): Leads to Grand Staircase and Elite Four chambers.
