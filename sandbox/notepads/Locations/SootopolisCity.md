# Location: Sootopolis City

## Overview
A mystical city built entirely inside the hollow crater of an extinct volcanic island, surrounded by water.
- Environment: Massive white stone caldera walls, terraced white stone stairs, a central lagoon, and ancient architecture.
- Story Climax: Groudon and Kyogre are clashing in the central lagoon, causing catastrophic weather across all of Hoenn!
- Key Figures: Steven Stone (former Champion), Wallace (former Gym Leader / ancient lorekeeper), and Juan (current Gym Leader).

## Connection Points
- Underwater Entrance / Exit: Submerged cave tunnel leading south out to Route 126.
- Dive Spot in Lagoon: Located at (29, 53) in the southern lagoon.
- Sky / Exterior: Sealed off by volcanic caldera walls; outdoor flying unlocks once visited!

## Discovered Topography & Navigation
- Submerged Entrance Tunnel: Connects Route 126 cave at (45, 65) to Sootopolis City lagoon. Spawns at (9, 8) facing North. Light rays illuminate cols 8..10, rows 4..7. Pressing B / Dive under light rays prompts to surface.
- Caldera Lagoon & Dive Spot: Surfaced at (29, 53) facing North on the dive spot. Extreme torrential downpour active in the lagoon! Open water extends North into the central caldera.
