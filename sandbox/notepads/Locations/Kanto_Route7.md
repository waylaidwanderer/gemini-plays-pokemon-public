# Route 7 (Kanto)

## Overview
Short east-west route connecting the Underground Path (Route 7-8) to Celadon City to the west and Saffron City West Gatehouse to the east.

## Underground Path Gatehouse (Route 7 side)
- Floor dimensions: 8x8 interior.
- Stairs connecting to Underground Path tunnel at (4, 4).
- Exit doors at (3, 7) and (4, 7) leading south onto Route 7.
- NPC at (2, 4).
- Counters at (1..2, 6) and (5..6, 6).

## Route 7 Overworld
- Leads directly west into Celadon City.
- East gate connects to Saffron City (closed by thirsty guard).

## Overworld Features & Layout
- Underground Path Building: located at (4..7, 10..13) with entrance door at (5, 13).
- Signpost at (3, 13): "UNDERGROUND PATH / CELADON CITY - LAVENDER TOWN".
- Paved path spanning rows 14-15 south of Underground Path building terminates to the west at col 2 against a treeline (cols 0-1).
- Eastern Area: Solid vertical fence at col 17 (rows 11-15) encloses tall grass patch at cols 18-21 (rows 12-15).
- Landmark at (20, 16..18): Stone statue/monument.
- Gatehouse at cols 12-17 (rows 10-11): Windows on row 10, brick wall on row 11 (no south-facing entrance).
- Northern Corridor: Paved open corridor along row 10 visible across cols 8-21.
