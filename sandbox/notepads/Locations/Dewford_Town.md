# Dewford Town & Granite Cave — Coordinates & Routing

## Dewford Town
- **Mr. Briney's Boat Dock:** `(11, 9)` (Mr. Briney stands at `(12, 9)`). After delivering Steven's Letter, speaking to Mr. Briney opens a `[PETALBURG / SLATEPORT / EXIT]` destination menu (with the cursor starting on `EXIT`; press `Up, A` to select `SLATEPORT`, then `B` twice to board the boat to Route 109).

- **Dewford Gym Door:** `(8, 17)` (exiting steps to `(8, 18)`). Reachable from outside the Pokémon Center `(2, 11)` via column `x = 3` (between the tree at `(2, 15..16)` and the Gym wall at `x = 4..10, y = 13..16`): walk `Right` 1 to `(3, 11)`, `Down` 7 along `x = 3` to `(3, 18)`, `Right` 5 to `(8, 18)`, and `Up` 1 into `(8, 17)`.
  - **Interior Entrance Mat:** `(5, 27)..(6, 27)`. Gym statues are at `(4, 26)` and `(4, 24)`, and the Gym Guide stands at `(5, 24)`.
  - **Zero-Trainer Route to Gym Leader Brawly (`(5, 27)` -> `(4, 4)`):**
    - From `(5, 27)`, walk `Up` 2 to `(5, 25)`, `Right` 1 to `(6, 25)`, `Up` 3 to `(6, 22)`, `Right` 2 to `(8, 22)`, `Up` 1 to `(8, 21)`, and `Right` 3 to `(11, 21)`.
    - From `(11, 21)`, walk `Up` 7 along `x = 11` to `(11, 14)`, `Right` 1 to `(12, 14)`, and `Up` 9 along `x = 12` to `(12, 5)`.
    - From `(12, 5)`, walk `Left` 9 along `y = 5` to `(3, 5)`, `Up` 1 to `(3, 4)`, `Right` 1 to `(4, 4)`, and press `Up` + `A` to challenge **Gym Leader Brawly** at `(4, 3)` (`Machop Lv. 16`, `Meditite Lv. 16`, `Makuhita Lv. 19` [Defeated — Knuckle Badge & TM08 Bulk Up obtained]).
  - **Exit Route from `(4, 4)`:** `Down` 1 to `(4, 5)` -> `Right` 8 to `(12, 5)` -> `Down` 9 to `(12, 14)` -> `Left` 1 to `(11, 14)` -> `Down` 7 to `(11, 21)` -> `Left` 3 to `(8, 21)` -> `Down` 1 to `(8, 22)` -> `Left` 2 to `(6, 22)` -> `Down` 3 to `(6, 25)` -> `Left` 1 to `(5, 25)` -> `Down` 3 out `(5, 27)`.
  - **Gym Leader Brawly Strategy:** Use 100%-accurate Special-type `Water Gun` against `Machop Lv. 16` (bypassing `Bulk Up` Defense boosts) and `Meditite Lv. 16` (guaranteed to break `Focus Punch` every turn). Against `Makuhita Lv. 19`, use `Growl` twice to reduce `Vital Throw` to ~9 damage per hit, then attack with `Mud Shot` into Torrent-boosted `Water Gun`.
## President Stone Quest Chain (Dewford -> Granite Cave -> Slateport)
- **Step 1 (Dewford Town / Granite Cave):** Travel northwest from Dewford Town across Route 106 into Granite Cave, obtain **HM05 (Flash)** from the Hiker at the entrance, and deliver **Steven's Letter** (`Letter`) to Steven deep inside Granite Cave.
- **Step 2 (Slateport City):** Return to Mr. Briney at the Dewford Town dock `(12, 9)` to sail to Route 109 / Slateport City and deliver the **Devon Goods** to Capt. Stern (and report back to President Stone in Rustboro City afterwards for the Exp. Share).
- **Dewford Hall:** Occupies `x = 1..5, y = 0..3` (door at `(3, 3)`). To walk north from the Pokémon Center `(2, 11)` onto **Route 106**, walk `Right` 4 to `(6, 11)` and `Up` along `x = 6` across `y = 0`.
