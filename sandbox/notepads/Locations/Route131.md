# Location: Route 131

## Overview
An ocean route in southern Hoenn connecting Route 130 on the east to Pacifidlog Town on the west.
- Critical Landmark: Sky Pillar (entrance in the northern rock formations, unlocked by Wallace to awaken Rayquaza).

## Connection Points
- East: Route 130 (boundary at col 60).
- West: Pacifidlog Town (boundary at col 0).
- North: Sky Pillar entrance cave (sandy shore at cols 34..37, rows 3..6).

## Consolidated Topography & Navigation Guide
1. Southern Main Highway (rows 25..28): Clear, open deep water highway running unobstructed east-west across Route 131 between Pacifidlog Town and Route 130. Bypasses western dead-ends.
2. Western Rocks & Cul-de-sacs (cols 0..34): Northern advances from cols 0..25 are blocked by continuous rock barriers, submerged rocks at (16, 11) / (17, 11), and dead-end coves.
3. Eastern Channel (cols 40..46): The only viable northward entrance from the southern highway. Located at (41, 26) past Swimmer Richard. Heads straight North along col 41 to row 18.
4. Sky Pillar Approach Channel:
   - From (41, 18), loop west along row 18 through (35, 18) and (33, 18).
   - Continue west along row 18 past col 30 to clear the row of rock cones at (30..34, 16..17).
   - Once past col 30, turn North into the wide, open northern bay (cols 26..32, rows 7..16).
   - Surf north through the bay directly onto the sandy shore (cols 34..37, rows 4..6).
   - The dark cave opening to Sky Pillar sits at row 3 above the sand.
