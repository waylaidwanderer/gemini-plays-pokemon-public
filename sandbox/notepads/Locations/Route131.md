# Location: Route 131

## Overview
An ocean route in southern Hoenn connecting Route 130 on the east to Pacifidlog Town on the west.
- Critical Landmark: Sky Pillar (entrance in the northern rock formations, unlocked by Wallace to awaken Rayquaza).

## Connection Points
- East: Route 130.
- West: Pacifidlog Town.
- North: Sky Pillar entrance opening.

## Progression Checklist - Route 131
1. [ ] Enter Route 131 from Route 130.
2. [ ] Scout northern rock formations to locate the opening leading to Sky Pillar.
3. [ ] Enter Sky Pillar and meet Wallace.
