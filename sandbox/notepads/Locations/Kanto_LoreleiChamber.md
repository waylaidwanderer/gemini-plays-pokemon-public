# Indigo Plateau - Lorelei's Chamber (Elite Four Room 1)

## Overview & Geography
- First chamber of the Pokémon League Elite Four gauntlet.
- Entered from Indigo Plateau lobby via northern doorway at (8, 0) [Turn 24667].
- Player arrives at (4, 11) and auto-walks to (4, 5) facing North. Entrance gate locks behind player at (3, 8)-(3, 9).
- Room layout: Central ice arena platform spanning cols 2-7, rows 2-5. Flanked by ice water pools.
- Northern exit: Doorway at the north end of the chamber, unlocks upon Lorelei's defeat.

## Elite Four Member
- Lorelei: Located at (5, 2) facing South.
  - Specializes in Ice/Water Pokémon.
  - Team: Dewgong Lv 54, Cloyster Lv 53, Slowbro Lv 54, Jynx Lv 56, Lapras Lv 56.
  - Defeated Turn 24759 by Sheldon Lv 67 (89/213 HP). Northern exit doorway unlocked.
