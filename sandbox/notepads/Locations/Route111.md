# Location: Route 111

## Overview
A major north-south route connecting central Hoenn (Mauville City) to the northern mountain region (Route 112, Mt. Chimney, Fallarbor Town) and the central Hoenn Desert. Divided into southern, desert, and northern sections.

## Connection Points
- South Entrance: Mauville City (northern border at cols 14..18).
- West Fork: Route 112 (leading to Cable Car and Fiery Path).
- North Fork: Desert (requires Go-Goggles to traverse sandstorm).

## Key Landmarks
- Winstrate House: Home of the Winstrate family (Victor, Victoria, Vivi, Vicky). Defeating all 4 in a gauntlet awards the Macho Brace!
- Winstrate House Interior: Entrance mat at (3, 7). Dining table at cols 5..6, rows 4..5. Victor at (4, 5), Vicky at (4, 4), Victoria at (7, 4), Vivi at (7, 5). Victoria awards the Macho Brace!
- TV Interviewers (Gabby & Ty): Roving reporters available for battle and interview.
- Route 111 South Signpost: Directional signpost.
- Item Ball: (19, 118) - [OBTAINED] Elixir!

## Winstrate Family Gauntlet Mechanics
- Structure: 4 consecutive 1-on-1 battles against Victor, Victoria, Vivi, and Vicky.
- After each victory, the defeated member runs inside and the next member steps outside to (13, 114).
- Interacting with each member using 'A' starts their battle. Do not press 'B' on challenge prompts.
- Defeating all 4 members awards the Macho Brace inside the house from Victoria!
- Western Pond / Shore: Located at cols 8..13, rows 105..110. Shoreline at (12, 104) is a dead-end cove. Main route to Route 112 and Desert continues north through the central rocky valley at cols 16..22 (north of row 104).
