# Viridian Gym - Layout & Navigation Log

## Overview
- **Gym Leader:** Giovanni (Ground-type specialist).
- **Badge:** Earth Badge (Badge #8) - Unlocks the path to Victory Road / Indigo Plateau.
- **Gym Status:** Unlocked and active now that we possess 7 Gym Badges!

## Physical Layout & Walkable Paths
- Located in the northeast corner of Viridian City (entrance doorway at `(32, 7)`).
- Inside is a tile-spinner maze (similar to Rocket Hideout) with several trainers.
- Giovanni is located in the northernmost chamber of the Gym.

## Battle Strategy
- **Ground-type Weaknesses:** Weak to Water, Grass, Ice.
- **SHELLBY (Blastoise) move pool:** HYDRO PUMP, ICE BEAM, BITE, SURF.
- **Strategy:** Surf or Hydro Pump will completely sweep Giovanni's Ground-type team!
