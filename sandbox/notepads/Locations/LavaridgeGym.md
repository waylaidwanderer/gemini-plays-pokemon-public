# Location: Lavaridge Gym

## Overview
The fourth official Pokémon Gym in the Hoenn League, located in Lavaridge Town.
- Gym Leader: Flannery
- Type Specialty: Fire
- Badge: Heat Badge (enables HM04 Strength in the field, Pokémon up to Lv 50 obey)
- Reward TM: TM50 Overheat
- Puzzle Mechanics: Hot spring steam pit vents connecting multiple rooms/levels. Bathers and trainers emerge from hidden steam pits.

## Entrance Room (1F)
- Entrance / Exit mat: (12..13, 18).
- Gym Guide: Located at (12, 16) facing North/East.
- Gym Statues: Located at (10, 14) and (15, 14).
- Steam Pit 1 (Entrance West): Located at (10, 18) west of entrance mat. This is the starting steam pit on the lower floor!
- Elevated Platform Ledge: Row 13 is an elevated ledge running across the room. Impassable on foot from below. Steam Pit 2 at (12, 11) and the bather trainer are on this upper tier.
- Upper Platform / Doorway: Located north at rows 7..9.
- Gym Guide Advice: Flannery uses Fire-type Pokémon. Use Water, Ground, or Rock moves. Steam pits shoot you between rooms/levels.
## Room 2 (B1F Lower Puzzle Room)
- Warp Source: Entrance Room Steam Pit 1 at (10, 18) drops here at (10, 18) facing North.
- Layout & Collision (Fully Verified):
  - Arrival Steam Pit: Located at (10, 18) (two-way warp returning to 1F Entrance Room).
  - Accessible Lower Area: Columns 10..12, rows 16..18, plus col 13 row 18, and eastern hall (cols 14-16, rows 11-18).
  - Eastern Hall (cols 14-16, rows 11-18): Exhaustively mapped. Contains NO steam pits, NO trainers, and NO exits. Bounded north at row 10, east at col 16 border, south at row 18 border, and west by solid col 13 pillar (rows 11-16).
  - Western Partition: Column 9 is solid to the west across rows 16-17; no passage to west room.
  - North Ledge at Row 15: Impassable from the south across cols 10..12.
  - Upper Platform: Elevated tier north of row 15 containing a steam pit at (11..12, 12..13). Cannot be climbed on foot from the lower floor.
  - Eastern Lower Steam Pit: Located at (13, 17). Ambush trap pit housing Kindler Jeff. Defeated Jeff remains standing permanently at (13, 17), blocking the tile.
  - Usable Exits: The steam pit at (10, 18) is the ONLY walkable exit from the lower level of Room 2.

## Gym Navigation & Mechanics (Verified)
- Vertical Partitions: The vertical yellow/red columns (e.g. at col 7) are solid impassable room dividers spanning the entire height of the gym. There are NO walkable openings or crossways between wings on foot.
- Room Navigation: All inter-room and inter-floor travel MUST be accomplished via steam pit warps.
- 1F Row 13 Ledge: Row 13 is an impassable elevated ledge running continuously across the entire width of the entrance foyer (including between the statues at (11, 14)). Cannot be climbed on foot from the south.
- 1F Entrance Foyer: Bounded by west partition (col 7), north ledge (row 13), and east wall. Contains the entrance mat at (12..13, 18), Gym Guide at (12, 16), and Gym Statues at (10, 14) and (15, 14).
- Steam Pit 1 at (10, 18): The sole entrance into the gym puzzle network, warping between 1F Entrance Foyer and B1F Room 2.
