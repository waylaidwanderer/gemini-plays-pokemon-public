# Location: Lavaridge Gym

## Overview
The fourth official Pokémon Gym in the Hoenn League, located in Lavaridge Town.
- Gym Leader: Flannery
- Type Specialty: Fire
- Badge: Heat Badge (enables HM04 Strength in the field, Pokémon up to Lv 50 obey)
- Reward TM: TM50 Overheat
- Puzzle Mechanics: Hot spring steam pit vents connecting multiple rooms/levels. Bathers and trainers emerge from hidden steam pits.
