# Location: Dewford Town

## Overview
A peaceful island community in southwestern Hoenn surrounded by the sea. Accessible via Mr. Briney's boat from Route 104 South.

## Connection Points
- North/Northwest: Route 106 (leading to Granite Cave).
- Sea Routes: Route 107 (East) and Route 106 (North).
- Boat Dock: Located on the eastern beach at (11..12, 9). Mr. Briney and his boat wait here to sail back to Route 104 or forward to Slateport City (after delivering Letter to Steven).
- Signpost near dock: Located at (10, 10).
- Blue-roofed house: Located directly northwest of the dock at (6..9, 4..8); front door at (7, 7).
- Pokémon Center: Located on the western beach of Dewford Town at (1..4, 6..10). Sliding door entrance at (2, 10) approached from row 11. Interior warp at (7, 8); talk to Nurse Joy at (7, 4) facing Up. Respawn checkpoint registered!
- Dewford Gym: Leader Brawly (Fighting-type). Located southeast of Pokémon Center; roof spans rows 13-17. Gym sign is at (11, 16). Protruding hexagonal entrance foyer spans columns 6-9, with white pillars at col 6 and col 9. Sliding glass entrance doors are at (7..8, 17) approached from row 18. Awards Knuckle Badge and allows HM05 Flash outside battle.
- Dewford Hall: Located at (1..5, 0..3) north of Pokémon Center. Front entrance door at (3, 3), hall signpost at (2, 4). Boy NPC stands outside at (2, 6).
- North Exit to Route 106: Wide open beach corridor at columns 6..8 heading north past row 0 directly into Route 106 toward Granite Cave.
- Fisherman: Stands at (11, 14) directly north of Dewford Gym sign; gave the OLD ROD!
- Pier Access: Pier connects to the beach at col 10; walking south off pier at (11, 9) is blocked.
- Gym Approach Route: From dock, step west to col 10, south to row 12/15, jog east to col 12 to bypass the Gym sign at (11, 16), down to row 18, then west to (8, 18) and Up into gym doors at (7..8, 17).
- Granite Cave (via Route 106): Hiker at entrance gives HM05 Flash. Steven Stone is located in the deepest chamber.

## Progression Checklist - Dewford Town
1. [x] Arrive at Dewford Town via Mr. Briney's boat.
2. [x] Speak to Fisherman to receive the Old Rod.
3. [x] Visit Granite Cave on Route 106, speak to Hiker for HM05 Flash.
4. [x] Fully heal party at Dewford Pokémon Center (Sage Lv 18 full HP 51/51, Lilz Lv 9, Cipher Lv 10).
5. [x] Challenge and defeat Gym Leader Brawly in Dewford Gym to earn the Knuckle Badge and TM08 Bulk Up.
6. [x] Return to Granite Cave with Flash enabled, locate Steven Stone, deliver President Stone's Letter, and receive TM47 Steel Wing.
7. [x] Speak to Mr. Briney at Dewford Town dock to sail to Slateport City.

## Mechanics & Navigation Discoveries
- Nurse Joy Interaction: Stand at (7, 4) facing Up. Advance dialogue strictly with A presses to reach and confirm the YES prompt on "Would you like to rest your POKéMON?". Avoid pressing B during the prompt sequence, as B defaults to NO and aborts the heal.
