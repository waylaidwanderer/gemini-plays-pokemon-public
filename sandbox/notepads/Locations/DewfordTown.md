# Location: Dewford Town

## Overview
A peaceful island community in southwestern Hoenn surrounded by the sea. Accessible via Mr. Briney's boat from Route 104 South.

## Connection Points
- North/Northwest: Route 106 (leading to Granite Cave).
- Sea Routes: Route 107 (East) and Route 106 (North).
- Boat Dock: Mr. Briney's boat docks on the beach; provides transport back to Route 104 South or forward to Slateport City (after delivering Letter to Steven).

## Key Buildings & Landmarks
- Pokémon Center: Located near the dock/beach.
- Dewford Gym: Leader Brawly (Fighting-type). Awards Knuckle Badge and allows HM05 Flash outside battle.
- Dewford Hall: Trendy phrase hall north of the Pokémon Center.
- Fisherman: Stands by the gym / water; gives the OLD ROD!
- Granite Cave (via Route 106): Hiker at entrance gives HM05 Flash. Steven Stone is located in the deepest chamber.

## Progression Checklist - Dewford Town
1. [ ] Arrive at Dewford Town via Mr. Briney's boat.
2. [ ] Speak to Fisherman to receive the Old Rod.
3. [ ] Visit Granite Cave on Route 106, speak to Hiker for HM05 Flash.
4. [ ] Locate Steven Stone in Granite Cave and deliver President Stone's Letter (receive TM47 Steel Wing).
5. [ ] Challenge and defeat Gym Leader Brawly to earn the Knuckle Badge.
