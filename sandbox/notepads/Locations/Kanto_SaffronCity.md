# Saffron City (Kanto)

The sprawling central metropolis of Kanto. Houses Silph Co. Head Office, Saffron Gym (Sabrina), Fighting Dojo, Copycat's house, Mr. Psychic's house, and connections to Routes 5 (North), 6 (South), 7 (West), and 8 (East).
