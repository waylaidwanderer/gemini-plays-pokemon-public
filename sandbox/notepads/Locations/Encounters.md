Always finish `#1 Nosepass`, `#2 Anorith`, `#3 Lileep`, `#4 Relicanth`, and `#5 Shuckle` with a **non-Poison/non-Fighting Pokemon (`Kabuto`, `Omanyte`, `Azurill`, `Luvdisc`, `Wingull`, `Goldeen`, `Paras`, or `Marshtomp`)** so `Lunatone` comes out `#6 LAST`! Also note that `Goldeen` is in the `Medium Fast` (`817 EXP`) group at Lv 16 (verified Turn 6154), and `Natu` can have `Early Bird` (`585/817 EXP` group).
