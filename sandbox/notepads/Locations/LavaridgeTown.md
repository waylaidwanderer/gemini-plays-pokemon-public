# Location: Lavaridge Town

## Overview
A hot spring town situated at the southern base of Mt. Chimney. Famous for its volcanic sand baths, medicinal herbs, and hot springs.
- Motto/Sign: "POKéMON CENTER HOT SPRINGS / An excellent place for relaxing!"

## Connection Points
- East Exit: Route 112 at (18..19, 10).

## Key Landmarks
- Pokémon Center: Located in northeast Lavaridge Town with front entrance at (8..9, 4..5). Connected to the outdoor hot springs in the rear. Checkpoint destination!
- Poké Mart: Located at (14..15, 4..5) with entrance at (14, 5).
- Ambient Hot Springs Lady: Located at (8, 7) outside Pokémon Center entrance. Dialogue: "Oh, you like hot springs, do you? / That's surprising for one as young as you." (Does NOT give the Egg).
- Sand Bath Egg Lady: Located in the hot sand bath area at (4, 7) facing South. Approach from (4, 8) facing North. Offers the Wynaut Egg!
- Hot Sand Baths: Located at cols 2..6, rows 7..9. Left sand mound at (3, 8), right sand mound with buried old man at (5, 8). Hot spring pool above at rows 3..6.
- Lavaridge Gym: Located at cols 1..7, rows 8..13 in southwest Lavaridge Town. Gym Leader Flannery (Fire type).
  - Entrance doors: (5..6, 13) entered from (5..6, 14) facing North.
  - Gym Signpost: (7, 13) read from (7, 14) facing North.
- Herb Shop: Sells herbal medicine (EnergyPowder, Energy Root, Heal Powder, Revival Herb).
- Residential Houses: Two houses located at rows 11..16, cols 10..18. House 1 door at (12, 13), House 2 door at (17, 13).
- Little Girl NPC: Located at (10, 14) wearing a red bow and yellow dress.
