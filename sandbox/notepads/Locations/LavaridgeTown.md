# Location: Lavaridge Town

## Overview
A hot spring town situated at the southern base of Mt. Chimney. Famous for its volcanic sand baths, medicinal herbs, and hot springs.
- Motto/Sign: "POKéMON CENTER HOT SPRINGS / An excellent place for relaxing!"

## Connection Points
- East Exit: Route 112 at (18..19, 10).

## Key Landmarks
- Pokémon Center: Located in northeast Lavaridge Town with front entrance at (13..14, 4). Connected to the outdoor hot springs in the rear. Checkpoint destination!
- Lavaridge Gym: Located in western/southwestern area of town. Gym Leader Flannery (Fire type).
- Herb Shop: Sells herbal medicine (EnergyPowder, Energy Root, Heal Powder, Revival Herb).
- Residential Houses: Two houses located at rows 12..16, cols 8..18.
