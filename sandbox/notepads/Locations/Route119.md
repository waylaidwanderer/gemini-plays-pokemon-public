# Location: Route 119

## Overview
A vast, rainy temperate route connecting Route 118 in the south to Fortree City in the north. Features dense giant tall grass, rivers, the Weather Institute, and a bridge where Rival Brendan challenges the player.

## Connection Points
- South Entrance: Route 118 (connects at cols 16..18, rows 138..139).
- North Exit: Fortree City.

## Key Landmarks
- Giant Tall Grass: Enormous grass where wild Pokémon hide and the Bicycle cannot be ridden at high speed.
- Fishing Spots: River runs through the route (Feebas can be found in 6 random water tiles).
- Weather Institute: Large two-story research facility invaded by Team Magma/Aqua.
  - Defeating the Team Magma/Aqua Admin awards the Pokémon Castform!
- Rival Battle (Brendan): Takes place on the bridge/trail north of the Weather Institute.
  - Defeating Brendan awards HM02 Fly!
- Fortree City Gate: Northernmost border leading directly into Fortree City.

## Progression Checklist - Route 119
1. [x] Enter Route 119 from Route 118.
2. [ ] Navigate through lower giant grass and defeat trainers.
3. [ ] Reach and enter the Weather Institute.
4. [ ] Defeat Team Magma/Aqua inside Weather Institute and rescue scientists (receive Castform).
5. [ ] Cross the bridge and defeat Rival Brendan (obtain HM02 Fly).
6. [ ] Continue north into Fortree City.

## Trainers & NPCs
- Cyclist NPC: (17..22, 133) - Non-combat flavor NPC. Warns that the giant tall grass snares bike tires.
