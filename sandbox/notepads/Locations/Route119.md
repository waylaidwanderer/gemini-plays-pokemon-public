# Location: Route 119

## Overview
A vast, rainy temperate route connecting Route 118 in the south to Fortree City in the north. Features dense giant tall grass, rivers, the Weather Institute, and a bridge where Rival Brendan challenges the player.

## Connection Points
- South Entrance: Route 118 (connects at cols 16..18, rows 138..139).
- North Exit: Fortree City.

## Key Landmarks
- Route 119 House: Located on the eastern bank at cols ~32..35, rows ~106..109 with red tiled roof and yellow siding. Interior (Wingull House): Home to a woman and numerous Wingulls. Woman shares lore about the CAVE OF ORIGIN (does not offer party healing). Outdoor NPC at (31, 109) is a civilian discussing HM02 Fly.
- Giant Tall Grass: Enormous grass where wild Pokémon hide and the Bicycle cannot be ridden at high speed.
- Fishing Spots: River runs through the route (Feebas can be found in 6 random water tiles).
- Weather Institute: Large two-story research facility invaded by Team Magma/Aqua.
  - Defeating the Team Magma/Aqua Admin awards the Pokémon Castform!
- Rival Battle (Brendan): Takes place on the bridge/trail north of the Weather Institute.
  - Defeating Brendan awards HM02 Fly!
- Fortree City Gate: Northernmost border leading directly into Fortree City.

## Route Mechanics Verified
- Giant Tall Grass Bicycle Collision: Giant tall grass is impassable while riding a Bicycle. The bike is completely blocked from entering giant tall grass tiles. The player must press Select to dismount onto foot before walking through giant tall grass.
- Water-to-land interaction: In Gen 3 (RSE), interacting across the shoreline boundary from water to an NPC on land does not trigger dialogue or battle. The player must disembark onto land to interact with land NPCs.
