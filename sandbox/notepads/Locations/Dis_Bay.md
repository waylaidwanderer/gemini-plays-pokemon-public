# Dis Bay

## Geography & Connections
- **Northern Entrance (`16-18, 0` <-> Hupest Wilds South `44-46, 49`, Verified Turn 36978)**: Seamless transition south of the Hupest Wilds.
- **Dis Bay Soaring & Metro Unlock (`17, 7`, Verified Turn 36979)**: Stepping south onto `(17, 7)` along the northern entrance road displays `"Dis Bay has been added to available locations!"`, unlocking **Dis Bay** for Latias Soaring (`27, 16` on the Hupest Sky Map) and Hupest Metro Line travel (`7: Dis Bay`).
- **Dis Bay Pokémon Center (`22, 8`, Verified Turn 36981)**: Nurse Joy at `(7, 2)` (counter at `7, 4`); healed party and registered checkpoint in Turn 36981. Features the PokéMart mezzanine stairs in the northwest corner (`2, 1`).
- **Dis Bay Metro Station (`13, 8`) & Gated Item Ball (`12, 6`, Verified Turn 36983)**: Located west of the central boulevard (`cols 16-18`). The golden item ball behind the station at `(12, 6)` is enclosed by wooden fences (`8, 5-7`, `10-11, 7`, `14, 5-6`) and gated by a rugged rock at `(9, 7)`.
- **Dis Bay Ferry Pier & Sailor to Cord Island (`18, 23`, Verified Turns 36984-36986)**:
  - Walking south down the eastern lane of the middle pier (`col 18`) around the green shipping container (`15-17, 17-20`) reaches the Sailor at `(18, 23)`.
  - Speaking to the Sailor at `(18, 23)` with the **Cord Ticket** prompts *"A-anyway! Shall we get going? Yes/No"*; selecting *Yes* flashes the Cord Ticket and sails to **Cord Island** (`36, 27`).
