# Cinnabar Island - Landmarks & Navigation Guide

## Key Locations
- **Pokémon Gym:** Locked. Requires the Secret Key to open.
- **Pokémon Mansion:** Entrance at the top-left of Cinnabar Island.
- **Cinnabar Lab:** Located next to the Pokémon Gym.
- **Pokémon Center:** Heals our party and acts as a Fly checkpoint.
