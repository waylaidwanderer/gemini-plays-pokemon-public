## Layout Obstacles & Safe Overworld Bypass Paths
- **The Pokémon Lab Building:** Occupies columns 2-7, rows 4-10. Column 5 and 6 are blocked on these rows.
- **The Poké Mart Building:** Occupies columns 14-17, rows 10-11.
- **The Cinnabar Gym Building:** Occupies columns 14-18, rows 1-3. Row 3 is completely blocked at column 13 by a signpost and column 14 by the Gym.
- **Safe Eastern Road (Columns 18-19):** Walkable green grass corridor extending vertically from row 12 to row 3, bypassing both the Mart and Gym.
- **Safe Horizontal Road (Row 4):** Completely open horizontally across columns 10 to 18, providing a clean bypass route around the Gym and Mart.
