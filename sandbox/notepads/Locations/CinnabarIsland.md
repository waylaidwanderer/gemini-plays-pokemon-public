# Cinnabar Island - Overworld Layout & Navigation Guide

## Landmarks & Points of Interest
- **Cinnabar Lab (Pokémon Lab):** Located in the southwest quadrant of Cinnabar Island.
  - **Entrance Door (Warp):** Located at `(6, 9)` on Cinnabar Island map.
  - **Interaction Guard:** Walking into `(6, 9)` warps the player inside the Pokémon Lab.
- **Pokémon Center:** Located in the center-left. Door is at `(11, 12)` (Fly waypoint).
- **Poké Mart:** Door is at `(16, 12)`.
- **Cinnabar Gym:** Located in the northeast at `(18, 4)`. Door is permanently locked until the **Secret Key** is obtained.
- **Pokémon Mansion:** Located in the northwest. The entrance door is at `(6, 3)` facing south (landing at `(5, 27)` on 1F West).

## Critical Overworld Layout Constraints & Detours
- **Cinnabar Lab Entrance Warp Hazard at `(6, 9)`:**
  - The Cinnabar Lab entrance is at `(6, 9)`.
  - When walking north from the south-east or south-west towards the Pokémon Mansion, walking straight UP Column 6 will trigger the warp at `(6, 9)` and enter the Lab!
  - **The Eastern Road Bypass Route:**
    - To safely bypass the Lab door and walk to the Mansion, the player MUST use the eastern road detour.
    - Walk east to Column 14 or 15.
    - Walk UP along Column 15 to Row 4 (completely open and bypasses the Lab door and the middle signposts).
    - Walk Left along Row 4 to Column 6, and walk UP to the Mansion entrance at `(6, 3)`.
