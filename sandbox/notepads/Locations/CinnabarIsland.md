# Cinnabar Island - Overworld & Building Mapping

## Overworld Landmarks & Door Coordinates
- **Pokémon Lab:** Located at the bottom-left of Cinnabar Island. The entrance door is at `(6, 9)`. Walking UP onto `(6, 9)` warps the player inside the Lab Lobby.
- **Cinnabar Gym:** Located at the top-right of Cinnabar Island. The entrance door is at `(18, 4)`. It is locked and requires the Secret Key from Pokémon Mansion B1F to open.
- **Poké Mart:** Located on the east side of Cinnabar Island. The entrance door is at `(15, 11)`.
- **Pokémon Center:** Located on the east side of Cinnabar Island, just west of the Poké Mart. The entrance door is at `(11, 11)`.
- **Pokémon Mansion:** Located at the top-left of Cinnabar Island. 

## Pokémon Lab Layout (Internal)
- **Room 1 (Meeting Room):** Access door is at `(2, 7)` / `(3, 7)` (red-checkered warp carpet tiles).
- **Eevee Diary:** A computer terminal at `(1, 4)` contains an e-mail detailing the three legendary birds (Articuno, Zapdos, Moltres) and their locations, as well as a cavern close to Cerulean.
- **Founder's Photo:** A photo frame on the wall at `(3, 2)` shows Dr. Fuji, the founder of the Lab.
- **Trade NPC:** A Scientist/Burglar NPC at `(0, 3)` / `(1, 3)` offers to trade a PONYTA for a SEEL.

## Navigation Channels
- **Row 12 / 13:** Horizontal open grass bypass beneath the Lab, connecting column 6 (west) to column 18 (east).
- **Column 18 / 19:** Vertical open grass corridor on the east side, connecting the south (row 12) to the Gym area (row 4).
- **Row 7:** A completely open horizontal grass bypass on rows 5 to 7, connecting column 18 (east) to the western side of the island.
