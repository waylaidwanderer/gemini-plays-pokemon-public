# Cinnabar Island - Locations & Landmarks Log

## Physical Layout & Walkable Areas
- **Size:** 20 Columns x 18 Rows.
- **Horizontal Fence Partition:** Divided horizontally along Row 8 on Columns 10-17.
- **Bypass Route:** Walk along Column 18 down to Row 12, then left to Column 6.

## Points of Interest & Coordinates
- **Pokémon Mansion Entrance:** Door at `(6, 3)`.
- **Pokémon Lab Entrance:** Door at `(6, 9)`.
- **Pokémon Center:** Door at `(11, 11)`.
- **Poké Mart:** Door at `(15, 11)`.
- **Cinnabar Gym (Blaine):** Door at `(18, 4)`. (Requires Secret Key from Pokémon Mansion B1F West).
