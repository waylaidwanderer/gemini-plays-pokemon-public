# Location: Route 104

## Overview
Route 104 is split into southern and northern sections by Petalburg Woods.
- South Section: Connects Petalburg City to the south entrance of Petalburg Woods. Contains coastal beach, Mr. Briney's cottage, and trainers.
- North Section: Connects the north exit of Petalburg Woods to Rustboro City. Contains the Pretty Petal Flower Shop.

## Connection Points
- South Section East Exit: Connects to Petalburg City at (40, 63).
- South Section North Entrance: Leads into Petalburg Woods.

## Landmarks & Features (South Section)
- East entrance pond / tree line: x=39..40, y=63..64.
- Main dirt clearing: x=27..36, y=60..65.
- Boy NPC: Stands at (27, 64) giving wild Pokémon catching advice (weaken target, inflict status like sleep/poison/burn).
- Signpost: Located south of the boy at roughly (27, 66).
- South Tall Grass Field: Spans x=20..27, y=54..59.
- Rocky ledge border: Extends west along the coast and east around y=53..56.
- Elevated Ledge Item Ball: Poké Ball item visible at (30, 52) behind vertical ledge wall at col 28 and southern one-way jump ledge at row 55.
- Northern trail / Highway: Extends north along col 23, bends west at row 44.
- Soft Soil Berry Trees: Located at (22..24, 41) north of the road bend. Spot (22, 41) harvested (2 Oran Berries collected, soil returned to soft and loamy); spot (23, 41) is empty soil; spot (24, 41) is a Pecha plant in flower stage.
- Petalburg Woods South Gate: White picket fence opening located at column 11, rows 39..40 leading north into the woods.
