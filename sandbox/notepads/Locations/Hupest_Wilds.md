# Hupest Wilds (Wild Area)

## Geography & Connections
- **Western Entrance (`0, 14-15` <-> Aurin Town `50, 17-18`, Verified Turn 36909)**: Reached from Aurin Town by climbing the eastern side-facing wooden cliff staircase at `(40, 20 -> 44, 19)` onto the Eastern Aurin Town plateau (`43-50, 17-18`) and walking east along `row 18` past `column 50`, which transitions seamlessly onto `(0, 15)` in the **Hupest Wilds**.

## Key Events & Landmarks
- **Latias Joins Asher (`2, 15`, Turns 36909-36911)**: Stepping east onto `(2, 15)` at the entrance to the **Hupest Wilds** triggers a post-game cutscene where the Legendary Pokémon **Latias** materializes at `(4, 15)` (`"Huh? It's Latias! / What is it doing here? / It's looking at Asher's Poké Ball. Wait, maybe it wants to join me?! / Asher: Do you want to join me? / Latias poked Asher's Poké Ball..."`).
- **Entrance Signboard (`3-4, 14`) & Northern Staircase (`6-8, 12-14`)**: Located immediately east of `(2, 15)`.
