# Hupest Wilds (Wild Area)

## Geography & Connections
- **Western Entrance (`0, 14-15` <-> Aurin Town `50, 17-18`, Verified Turn 36908)**: Reached from Aurin Town by climbing the diagonal wooden cliff staircase at `(40, 20 -> 42, 18)` onto the Eastern Aurin Town plateau (`43-50, 17-18`) and walking east past `column 50`, which transitions seamlessly onto `(0, 15)` in **Hupest Wilds**.

## Key Events & Landmarks
- **Latias Cutscene & Gift Recruitment (`2, 15` -> `3, 15`, Turns 36908-36910)**: Stepping east onto `(2, 15)` at the entrance to **Hupest Wilds** triggers a post-game cutscene where the Legendary Pokémon **Latias** materializes at `(3, 15)` in front of Asher (`"Huh? It's Latias! What is it doing here? / It's looking at Asher's Poké Ball. Wait, maybe it wants to join me? / Asher: Do you want to join me? / Latias poked Asher's Poké Ball! / Latias has joined Asher! / If your party is full, Latias will be transferred to your PC Box!"`), recruiting **Latias** into the PC Box as Asher's **100th Owned species** in the PokéDex.
- **Entrance Signboard (`4-5, 14`) & Northern Staircase (`7-9, 12-14`)**: Located immediately east of `(3, 15)`.
