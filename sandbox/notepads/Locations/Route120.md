# Location: Route 120

## Overview
A scenic, expansive route connecting Fortree City (west) to Route 121 (east/south). Features rolling hills, ponds, wooden bridges, giant tall grass, and frequent rain. Steven Stone is located on the northern wooden bridge.

## Connection Points
- West Exit: Fortree City (connects at cols 0..2, rows 7..9).
- East/South Exit: Route 121 (leading toward Safari Zone and Lilycove City).

## Key Landmarks & Features
- Interviewers Gabby & Ty: Located at (36..37, 5) on the northern bend. Optional Double Battle (Magneton & Loudred).
- Entrance Signpost: Located at (27, 3), read from (27, 4) facing North. Text: "ROUTE 120 / ⬅ FORTREE CITY".
- Western Clearing: Open grass corridor connecting to Fortree City.
- Puddle / Pond Network: Water bodies requiring Surf or crossing via bridges.
- Northern Wooden Bridge: Location where Steven Stone is waiting with the Devon Scope!
- Ancient Tomb: Located in southwest lake (Registeel sealed chamber).
