# Location: Trick House

## Overview
A quirky challenge house located on Route 110 with rotating puzzle mazes managed by the Trick Master. Each puzzle rewards experience, trainer battles, and items upon completion.

## Front Room
- Entrance door: (5, 7) facing North from inside.
- Elevated tatami platform: row 6 to row 2.
- Low table with teacup: columns 5..6, rows 3..4.
- Cushions: (4, 3), (4, 5), (7, 3), (7, 5).
- Trick Master Puzzle 1 Hiding Spot: Hiding under the table at (6, 3)! Discovered by interacting from (6, 2) facing South.
- Scroll Doorway: (5, 1) - Trick Master jumped into the scroll, leaving the entrance open to Puzzle 1. Interacting with 'A' facing North from (5, 2) triggers prompt: "There is a big hole behind the scroll! Want to go in?" (defaults to YES). Confirming enters the maze.
## Puzzle 1 (Cut Maze)
- Walkways: Maze consists of elevated yellow wooden plank paths over sunken tatami floor.
- Entrance warp mat: (0, 21), player enters at (0, 20).
- Trainer 1: Lass Sally at (9, 20) [DEFEATED].
- Cut Tree #1: (13, 18) [CUT - CLEARED].
- Cut Tree #4: (8, 10) [CUT - CLEARED].
- Column 9 walkway: Runs vertically from (9, 11) down to the horizontal boardwalk at (9, 16).
- Row 16 boardwalk: Runs horizontally across the southern section, connecting column 9 west to the scroll area.
- Secret Scroll: Located at the bottom-left corner (near col 2, row 21) on the floor.
- Trainer 2: Lass located at (1, 19/20) guarding the Secret Scroll in the southwest corner.
