# Location: Trick House

## Overview
A quirky challenge house located on Route 110 with rotating puzzle mazes managed by the Trick Master. Each puzzle rewards experience, trainer battles, and items upon completion.

## Front Room
- Entrance door: (5, 7) facing North from inside.
- Elevated tatami platform: row 6 to row 2.
- Low table with teacup: columns 5..6, rows 3..4.
- Cushions: (4, 3), (4, 5), (7, 3), (7, 5).
- Trick Master Puzzle 1 Hiding Spot: Hiding under the table at (6, 3)! Discovered by interacting from (6, 2) facing South.
- Scroll Doorway: (5, 1) - Trick Master jumped into the scroll, leaving the entrance open to Puzzle 1. Interacting with 'A' facing North from (5, 2) triggers prompt: "There is a big hole behind the scroll! Want to go in?" (defaults to YES). Confirming enters the maze.
## Puzzle 1 (Cut Maze)
- Maze Structure: The floor consists of walkable green tatami mats. The yellow wooden structures with rivets are solid divider walls / obstacles.
- Entrance warp mat: (0, 21), player enters at (0, 20).
- Trainer 1: Lass Sally at (9, 20) [DEFEATED]. Used Oddish Lv 16. Awarded ¥256.
- Status: COMPLETED! (All 3 trainers defeated, Secret Scroll code obtained, exit scroll door unlocked, Rare Candy claimed).
- Obstacles: 8 cut shrubs cleared using HM01 Cut.
- Trainers Defeated: Lass Sally (9, 20), Lass Robin (2, 14), Youngster Eddie (13, 8).
- Secret Scroll: (3, 16) - Code: "TRICK MASTER is fabulous."
- Exit Scroll Door: (13, 1) - Opened with memorized code, granting access to Reward Room.

## Reward Room
- Entered via unlocked scroll doorway at (13, 1) from Puzzle 1, warping to (10, 2).
- Low table with cup at (4..5, 4..5), cushions at (5, 3), (6, 5), and (3, 5).
- Trick Master sits at (3, 5) on the western cushion.
- Secret exit hole in back wall: (2..3, 1), leading back to the front room / entrance.
- Reward: Rare Candy for solving Puzzle 1.
- Secret Exit: Hole at (2, 1) in Reward Room leads through backstage passage back to the Front Room entrance mat.
