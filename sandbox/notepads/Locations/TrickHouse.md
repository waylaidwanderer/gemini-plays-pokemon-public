# Location: Trick House

## Overview
A quirky challenge house located on Route 110 with rotating puzzle mazes managed by the Trick Master. Each puzzle rewards experience, trainer battles, and items upon completion.

## Front Room
- Entrance door: (5, 7) facing North from inside.
- Elevated tatami platform: row 6 to row 2.
- Low table with teacup: columns 5..6, rows 3..4.
- Cushions: (4, 3), (4, 5), (7, 3), (7, 5).
- Trick Master Puzzle 1 Hiding Spot: Hiding under the table at (6, 3)! Discovered by interacting from (6, 2) facing South.
- Scroll Doorway: (5, 1) - Trick Master jumped into the scroll, leaving the entrance open to Puzzle 1. Interacting with 'A' facing North from (5, 2) triggers prompt: "There is a big hole behind the scroll! Want to go in?" (defaults to YES). Confirming enters the maze.
## Puzzle 1 (Cut Maze)
- Entrance warp mat: (0, 21), player enters at (0, 20).
- Walkway along row 20 extends east to col 8..9.
- Trainer 1: Lass Sally at (9, 20) facing West [DEFEATED]. Used Oddish Lv 16. Awarded ¥256.
- Cut Tree #1: Located at (13, 18), blocking the passage to the upper floor from row 19/20. Interacted from (13, 19) facing North.
- Secret Scroll: Located on tatami mat at (4, 15).
- Trainer 2: Lass at (4, 13) facing South guarding the area above the scroll.
- Objective: Find the secret scroll to memorize the password, cut through trees, and reach the back room scroll door.
- Cut Tree #2: Located at (14, 14), blocking the eastern corridor heading north along col 14. Interacted from (14, 15) facing North.
- Cut Tree #3: Located on the boardwalk at (7, 10).
- Round green hedges: Decorative barriers on the elevated boardwalks (e.g. at (10, 12) and (10, 16)).
