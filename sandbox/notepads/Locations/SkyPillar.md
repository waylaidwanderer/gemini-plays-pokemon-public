# Location: Sky Pillar

## Overview
Ancient, legendary tower located in northern Route 131 where the super-ancient dragon Pokémon Rayquaza rests. Wallace unlocked the ancient seals so we can awaken Rayquaza and quell the catastrophic conflict between Groudon and Kyogre in Sootopolis City.

## Connection Points
- South: Route 131 sandy shore (cave doorway at 36, 6; exit at 6, 16).
- North: Sky Pillar exterior base / tower entrance.

## Progression Checklist - Sky Pillar
1. [x] Navigated Route 131 and entered Sky Pillar Entrance Cave at (36, 6) -> arrived at (6, 16) (Turn 18351).
2. [x] Ascended cave stairs and exited through light patch at (14, 4) by pressing Down -> arrived outside at (17, 14) (Turn 18377)!
3. [x] Met Wallace outside the Sky Pillar tower entrance at (16, 14) (Turn 18378)!
4. [x] Completed Wallace's dialogue unlocking the tower entrance (Turn 18378)!
5. [ ] Enter Sky Pillar tower and ascend to the summit.
6. [ ] Awaken Rayquaza!

## Discovered Topography & Navigation
- Entrance Cave:
  - South entrance/exit doormat to Route 131 at (6, 16).
  - Walkway: 3-tile wide lower corridor (cols 5..7, rows 12..16).
  - First staircase at (6, 11) up to middle landing (rows 8..10).
  - Second staircase at (6, 7) up to top landing (rows 5..6).
  - Cave Exit Warp: The bright oval light patch at (14, 4) is the exit warp! Stepping DOWN on (14, 4) warps player outside to Sky Pillar Exterior at (17, 14).
- Exterior Base:
  - Sandy mountain plateau surrounded by sea rocks.
  - Tower approach path leads to the base at (14, 7).
  - Wallace stands at (14, 6) directly in front of the Sky Pillar tower entrance doorway at (14, 5).
  - Extreme weather effects active on the summit exterior.
## Floor 1F
- Entrance from Exterior Base at (6, 13).
- Arrived at (6, 12).
- Layout: Central pit/barrier with perimeter corridors leading around it.
- Staircase to 2F located in north wall around cols 10..11, row 2.
## Floor 2F
- Arrived from 1F via stairs at (10, 2).
- Layout: North corridor (rows 2..5) completely clear and intact without cracked tiles.
- Story visit mechanic: In Pokémon Emerald's story event, Sky Pillar floors have intact solid floors (no cracked tiles/bike puzzles required).
- Staircase to 3F located in northwest wall at (3, 1..2).
## Floor 3F
- Arrived from 2F via stairs at (3, 1..2).
- North corridor blocked by rock rubble at (4, 2), (5, 3), (5, 4) and central pit.
- Target staircase to 4F visible in north wall at (7, 1..2).
- Route: Move South along western corridor (col 3), loop around south edge of central pit, and ascend eastern corridor to (7, 1).
- Right staircase located at col 11, row 1 with unobstructed approach corridor along col 11.
- Middle staircase located at col 5..6, row 1.
## Floor 4F
- Arrived from 3F right staircase at (11, 1..2).
- Staircase to 5F visible in north wall around cols 5..6 (middle) and cols 1..2 (left).
- North passage blocked by diagonal rock wall at (10, 2), (9, 3), (8, 4) and pit.
- Route: Move South along eastern corridor (col 10), loop around south edge of central pit, and ascend western corridor to the 5F stairs.
- 5F stairs located at (6..7, 1). Western approach: North along col 3 to (3, 2), East along row 2 to (6, 2), North into (6, 1).
## Floor 5F
- Arrived on 5F at middle archway (7, 2) from 4F.
- The true staircase to the Summit (6F / Rayquaza) is the NORTHWEST ARCHWAY at (3, 1)!
- Row 2 connects directly from (7, 2) west to (3, 2) -> North into (3, 1) to enter the Summit.
