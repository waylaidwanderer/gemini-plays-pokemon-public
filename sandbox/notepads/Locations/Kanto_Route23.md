# Route 23 Points of Interest & Geography

## Connections
- South: Pokémon League Reception Gate at (7..8, 140) [doorway at (4..5, 0) inside gatehouse].
- North: Victory Road & Indigo Plateau.

## Layout & Badge Checkpoints
- Southern Entrance: Starts at (7, 139) immediately north of the Reception Gate doorway. Water borders the south (rows 140+).
- Cascadebadge Guard: Stationed at (8, 136) facing down. Trigger tile at row 136 (e.g. (7, 136)). Verifies Cascadebadge possession: "You can pass here only if you have the CASCADEBADGE! Oh! That is the CASCADEBADGE! Go right ahead!".
- Thunderbadge Guard: Stationed at (8, 119) facing down. Gateposts at (7, 119) and (10, 119). Verifies Thunderbadge possession.

## Survey & Barriers
- Tile (7, 131): Solid barrier blocking northbound movement on foot from (7, 132).
- Tile (7, 132): Attempting to Surf north from (7, 132) yields "No SURFing on SHELDON here!".
- Eastern Corridor (Cols 14-15): Open 2-tile-wide avenue running north through rows 131..128+ between statues at col 13 and col 16, bypassing the central water/ledge barrier.
- Row 123 Opening (Cols 8-9): Open northbound avenue between statues at (7, 123) and (10, 123), leading north to the Thunderbadge checkpoint at row 119.
