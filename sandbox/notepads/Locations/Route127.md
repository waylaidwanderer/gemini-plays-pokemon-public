# Location: Route 127

## Overview
A vast open ocean route in eastern Hoenn connecting Mossdeep City on the north to Route 128 on the south.
- Environment: Deep ocean water, scattered sea rocks, sandbar islets, swimmer and fisherman trainers, and underwater dive trenches.
- Key Progression Goal: Traverse south toward Route 128 to locate the deep water trench leading to Seafloor Cavern.

## Connection Points
- North: Mossdeep City (south ocean border).
- South: Route 128.
- West: Route 126.

## Progression Checklist - Route 127
1. [ ] Enter Route 127 from Mossdeep City.
2. [ ] Navigate south through open water corridors past sea rocks and trainers.
3. [ ] Transition south into Route 128 toward the Seafloor Cavern trench.
