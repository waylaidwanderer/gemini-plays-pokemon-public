# Location: Route 127

## Overview
A vast open ocean route in eastern Hoenn connecting Mossdeep City on the north to Route 128 on the south.
- Environment: Deep ocean water, scattered sea rocks, sandbar islets, swimmer and fisherman trainers, and underwater dive trenches.
- Key Progression Goal: Traverse south toward Route 128 to locate the deep water trench leading to Seafloor Cavern.

## Connection Points
- North: Mossdeep City (south ocean border at rows 30..35).
- South: Route 128.
- West: Route 126.

- Open Water Highway: Col 25+ heading South is unobstructed.
- Shallow Water Islet 1: Located at cols 18..21, rows 12+ southwest of the northern entrance.
- Dark Water Dive Trench 1: Located at cols 24..27, rows 14..18+ extending south along the main navigation channel.
- Sea Rock 2: Pointed brown rock located at (31, 16) east of the dive trench.
- Sea Rock 3: Pointed rock at (20, 26) and rounded reef at (18..19, 27..28) west of the trench.
- Trench & Western Cliff: Dark water dive trench 1 extends from row 14 to row 36 across cols 24..27. South of row 36, open ocean returns to standard deep water waves heading south along col 25; western cliff island runs along cols 18..20.
