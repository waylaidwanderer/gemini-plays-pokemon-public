# Location: Team Aqua Hideout

## Overview
The secret high-tech coastal base of Team Aqua carved into the cliffs of Lilycove City's northeastern cove.
- Environment: Subterranean water channels, metal grates, laboratories, teleportation warp pads, and submarine docking bays.
- Objectives:
  1. Infiltrate the base and defeat patrolling Aqua Grunts.
  2. Navigate the warp pad maze to locate the hidden Master Ball (must not be missed!).
  3. Confront Team Aqua Admin Matt before Archie departs in the submarine.

## Connection Points
- Cove Entrance / Exit: South doorway at (13, 28) exits to Lilycove City eastern cove at (70, 5). Player spawns at (13, 27) facing North.

## Topography & Navigation - 1F (100% Fully Mapped & Verified)
- Entrance Water Channel: (13, 27) entered from cove, leads north to dock stairs.
- Central Water Basin: cols 7..14, rows 12..24. Bounded on north (row 12) and west (col 6) by impassable rock cliffs.
- Dock Stairs: (13..14, 11..12). Only dismount/boarding point between land and water on 1F.
- Central Indoor Pool: cols 7..18, rows 5..8. Bounded by grey railing. Walkways run above (row 4) and below (row 9).
- Western Corridor: cols 2..6, rows 4..25. Connects row 4 and row 9 around pool; extends south past distillation machines. Terminates at southern scenic catwalk (rows 25..26, cols 2..10).
- Eastern Corridor: col 23, rows 9..25. Extends south past machinery to scenic overlook pier.
- North Doorway at (22, 1/2): Sole entrance to B1F Hub at (29, 1).

## Topography & Navigation - B1F Hub, Canal Chamber & Laboratory
- B1F Hub: Entrance at (29, 1). Contains Left Pad (27, 4) to Canal Chamber and Right Pad (31, 4) to Generator Room (Max Elixir at 29, 12).
- Canal Chamber (North Catwalk): Catwalk along row 15 from (3, 15) to (16, 15). At (16..19, 14), a ledge allows dropping down into the Laboratory at (19, 17).
- Laboratory (rows 15..26, cols 16..32):
  - Defeated female Grunt at (20, 18).
  - Defeated Grunt duo at (28, 17..19) in Double Battle.
  - Catwalk warp pad at (32, 19) connects to Crate Room at (20, 4).
  - South Canal Walkway: Along rows 21..22 below the water canal, a metal-grate walkway connects col 19 directly West to (3, 20), reaching the South Canal teleporter pad!
- South Canal Teleporter: Pad at (3, 20) teleports directly to Warp Maze Tier 1 at (45, 3).

- Eastern Machinery Chamber:

  - Defeated male Aqua Grunt at (20, 7) (Carvanha Lv 32).
  - Large generators at north (cols 19..28, rows 1..5).

- Western Machinery Chamber:
  - Southern catwalk along row 13 connects to Nest Ball at (3, 13). Solid facility wall at row 11/12 separates catwalk from northern rooms.
  - Northern sector contains Grunt at (13, 5), computer desks, and northern staircase.

  - Tier 2: Row 9. Pads at (42, 9), (45, 9), (48, 9).
