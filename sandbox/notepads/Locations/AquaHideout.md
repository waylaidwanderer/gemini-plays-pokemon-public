# Location: Team Aqua Hideout

## Overview
The secret high-tech coastal base of Team Aqua carved into the cliffs of Lilycove City's northeastern cove.
- Environment: Subterranean water channels, metal grates, laboratories, teleportation warp pads, and submarine docking bays.
- Objectives:
  1. Infiltrate the base and defeat patrolling Aqua Grunts.
  2. Navigate the warp pad maze to locate the hidden Master Ball (must not be missed!).
  3. Confront Team Aqua Admin Matt / Shelly before Archie departs in the submarine.
