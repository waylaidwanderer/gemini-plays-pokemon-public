# Route 9 Geography & Points of Interest

## Connections
- West: Cerulean City (connects at cols 0..1, rows 8..9 to Cerulean City (38..39, 16..17)).
- East: Route 10 (leading to Rock Tunnel).

## Geography & Layout
- Western Entrance (Cols 0..5, Rows 7..10): Bounded by stone fence posts along row 7 (north) and row 10 (south).
- Northern Corridor (Row 8): Open path from col 0 to col 4, blocked at (5, 8) by large tree.
- Southern Corridor (Row 9): Open path from col 0 to col 3, blocked at (4, 9) by a Cut tree.
- Cut Tree: Located at (4, 9), blocking the southern corridor into the main body of Route 9.
