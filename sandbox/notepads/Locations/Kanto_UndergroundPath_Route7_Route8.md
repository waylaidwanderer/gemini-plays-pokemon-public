# Underground Path (Route 7 - Route 8)

## Overview
Subterranean tunnel connecting Route 8 (east of Saffron City) to Route 7 (west of Saffron City, just outside Celadon City), bypassing the closed Saffron City gatehouses.

## Route 8 Entrance Gatehouse
- Floor dimensions: 8x8 interior.
- Entrance / Exit door at (3, 7)-(4, 7) leading south to Route 8.
- Counters at (1..2, 6) and (5..6, 6).
- NPC at (3, 4).
- Stairs descending to Underground Path tunnel at (4, 4).

## Tunnel Navigation
- Direct subterranean corridor between Route 8 and Route 7.

## Subterranean Tunnel (Route 7 - Route 8)
- East entrance stairs connect to Route 8 gatehouse; entry tile at (47, 2).
- Eastern boundary wall at col 48.
- Horizontal corridor spans rows 1..5; north wall at row 0, south wall at row 6.
- Tunnel runs west from col 47 toward Route 7 gatehouse (cols 0..4).
