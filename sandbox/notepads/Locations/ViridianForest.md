# Viridian Forest - Locations & Landmarks

## Overworld Layout
- **Southern Entrance (from Route 2 Gatehouse):** Player transitions to Viridian Forest at (17, 47) facing Up.
- **Immediate Entry Area:**
  - **Signpost:** Located at (18, 45).
  - **Wandering NPC:** Sits/moves near (16, 43) (blue cap, trainer sprite).
  - **Boundaries:** Tree trunks on column 14, and columns 19-22. Walkable columns are 15, 16, 17, 18.
