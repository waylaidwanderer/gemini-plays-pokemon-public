# Location: Jagged Pass

## Overview
A rugged volcanic mountainside route leading south from Mt. Chimney summit down to Route 112 (near the lower Cable Car Station and Lavaridge Town).
- Constant falling white volcanic ash.
- Features one-way downhill ledges, Acro Bike rail bars, and rocky terrain.
- Wild Pokémon encountered: Spoink (Lv 20, fled), Numel (Lv 22, fled).

## Connection Points
- North: Mt. Chimney summit entrance at (13, 4).
- South: Route 112 (north of Lavaridge Town).

## Discovered Coordinates & Layout
- Entrance from Mt. Chimney: Spawn at (13, 5).
- Acro Bike rail chutes along columns 13..14: Can be walked and hopped down on foot southward to bypass high cliffs!
  - Upper chute at (14, 13..14) connects row 12 terrace down to row 15 terrace.
  - Middle chute at (14, 18..19) connects row 17 terrace down to row 20 basin.
- Ledge jump at row 7 down to row 8 (Hiker Eric).
- Ledge jump at row 10 down to row 11 terrace.
- Row 16 Ledge: Jump ledge facing south down to row 17.
- Middle Basin (rows 17..23):
  - Rail bars at (13..14, 18..19).
  - Open western dirt corridor (cols 8..12).
  - Volcanic mound at (15..17, 17..18) outside the secret Team Magma Hideout.
- Eastern Terrace (rows 20..26):
  - Reached via eastern rail chute at (22, 21..22).
  - Item Ball at (23, 24): Burn Heal! [COLLECTED]
  - Bounded on the west by an impassable vertical cliff wall at column 19/20, sealing it from the sunken central terrace (Julio and Autumn).
  - Progression exit: One-way south-facing jump ledge at row 26, hopping down to (20, 27) into the lower basin.
