# Location: Jagged Pass

## Overview
A rugged volcanic mountainside route leading south from Mt. Chimney summit down to Route 112 (near the lower Cable Car Station and Lavaridge Town).
- Constant falling white volcanic ash.
- Features one-way downhill ledges, Acro Bike rail bars, and rocky terrain.
- Wild Pokémon encountered: Spoink (Lv 20, fled).

## Connection Points
- North: Mt. Chimney summit entrance at (13, 4).
- South: Route 112 (north of Lavaridge Town).

## Discovered Coordinates & Layout
- Entrance from Mt. Chimney: Spawn at (13, 5).
- Acro Bike rail chutes along columns 13..14.
- Ledge jump at row 7 down to row 8.
- Ledge jump at row 10 down to row 11 terrace.
- Row 12/13 Terrace: Row 13 is an impassable high cliff wall across cols 11..18. The true exit from this terrace is on the far east: follow row 12 east past cols 17..19 to (20, 12), where the path turns south around the cliff corner down column 20 to row 15, connecting to the lower eastern slope.
