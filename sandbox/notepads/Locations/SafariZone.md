

### Western Plateau Bypass (East-to-West Shortcut)
- **Ascent:** Climb the stairs at `(22, 23)` to reach the western plateau at `(22, 22)`.
- **Plateau Traversal:** Walk Left and Down to `(15, 24)`.
- **Descent:** Climb down the stairs at `(16, 27)` to land on the western ground level pocket at `(16, 28)`.
- **Note:** This bypass is a dead end on the ground level because Column 5 has an unbroken vertical wall of bushes/statues, completely blocking westward ground traversal from Column 16 to Column 0. Thus, this middle ground level (Columns 6-16) is a dead end pocket, and the only way to reach Area 3 (West) is via the main northern plateau land bridge (East Stairs at (33, 12)).
