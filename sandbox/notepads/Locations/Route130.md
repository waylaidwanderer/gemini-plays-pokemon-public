# Location: Route 130

## Overview
A vast open ocean route in southern Hoenn connecting Route 129 on the east (col 80) to Route 131 on the west (col 0).
- Key Destination: Route 131 and the Sky Pillar (Rayquaza awakening quest).

## Connection Points
- East: Route 129 (boundary at col 80).
- West: Route 131 (boundary at col 0).
