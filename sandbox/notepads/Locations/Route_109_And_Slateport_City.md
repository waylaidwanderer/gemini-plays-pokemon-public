## Route 109
- **Mr. Briney's Landing Spot:** `(21, 23)` (Mr. Briney stands at `(21, 24)` in front of his boat at `(21, 25)`).
- **Zero-Trainer Northbound Bypass (`(21, 23)` -> Slateport City `(16, 59)`):**
  - From `(21, 23)`, walk `Left` 3 to `(18, 23)` and `Up` 6 along `x = 18` to `(18, 17)`.
  - From `(18, 17)`, walk `Left` 1 to `(17, 17)`, `Up` 1 to `(17, 16)`, `Left` 2 behind the Sailor at `(15, 17)` to `(15, 16)`, and `Up` 4 along `x = 15` to `(15, 12)`.
  - From `(15, 12)`, walk `Left` 2 to `(13, 12)` and `Up` 6 along `x = 13` to `(13, 6)`.
  - From `(13, 6)`, walk `Right` 3 across the top of the orange umbrella canopy to `(16, 6)` and `Up` 7 along `x = 16` into **Slateport City at `(16, 59)`**.
