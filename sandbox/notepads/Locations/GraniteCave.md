# Location: Granite Cave

## Overview
A rugged, multi-level cavern on Route 106 north of Dewford Town. Contains wild Pokémon, complex lower floors requiring Flash for visibility and the Mach Bike for mud slopes, and the inner chamber where Steven Stone is studying rocks.

## Connection Points
- South Entrance (1F): Connects to Route 106 beach (interior warp at 37, 12).
- Interior Ladders: Connect 1F to B1F, B2F, and Steven's chamber.

## Landmarks & NPCs (1F Entrance Chamber)
- Entrance Warp: (37, 12) leading south to Route 106 beach.
- Flash Hiker: Located at (36, 9) facing East; gives HM05 Flash! (Obtained!).
- Granite Cave 1F Topography:
  - Entrance: (37, 12) at the southeast corner.
  - Flash Hiker: (36, 9) directly north of entrance; gave HM05 Flash!
  - 1F Main Path: Leads west from the Hiker along row 7/8 directly to the Southwest Ladder at (17, 11).
  - Item Ball: [COLLECTED] Escape Rope at (17, 7).
  - Southwest Ladder: Located at (17, 11); primary route descending into Granite Cave B1F!
  - Northeast Exit Plateau: The ladder at (35, 3) and row 6 jump ledge are the one-way return exit from Steven's chamber back to the entrance.

## Granite Cave B1F
- Southwest Ladder: Located at (4, 21) leading back to 1F entrance.
- Southeast Descent Ladder: Located at (28, 21) leading down to B2F.
- Closed Route Status: The accessible path on B1F is strictly a closed corridor connecting (4, 21) and (28, 21). Verified in Turns 1941, 1963, 2012, and 2069 that (22, 13) is impassable to both North and East, and col 28 is blocked north at row 19. B1F has NO northern exit to the northeast ladder; progression MUST go through B2F.
