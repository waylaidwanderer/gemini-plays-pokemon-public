# Location: Granite Cave

## Overview
A rugged, multi-level cavern on Route 106 north of Dewford Town. Contains wild Pokémon, complex lower floors requiring Flash for visibility and the Mach Bike for mud slopes, and the inner chamber where Steven Stone is studying rocks.

## Connection Points
- South Entrance (1F): Connects to Route 106 beach (interior warp at 37, 12).
- Interior Ladders: Connect 1F to B1F, B2F, and Steven's chamber.

## Grinding & Encounter Strategy (1F Runway)
- Row 8 Runway: Open horizontal corridor spanning x=28 to x=35. Walking back and forth between x=28 and x=34 provides 100% safe, fast wild encounters without bumping into walls or NPCs.
- Wild Encounter EXP Yields:
  - Makuhita Lv 6: ~74 EXP
  - Makuhita Lv 8: ~90-100 EXP
  - Makuhita Lv 9: ~111 EXP
  - Geodude Lv 8: ~98 EXP (4x weak to Grass; OHKO by Absorb which also restores full HP)
- Battle Menu Navigation: In Pokémon Emerald, the FIGHT menu remembers the last selected move across turns and battles. Inputting Down then Left from any move position reliably navigates to Slot 3 (Absorb, bottom-left).
- Start Menu Memory: The Start menu cursor retains the last opened item (e.g., BAG) rather than always defaulting to POKéDEX.
- EXP Bar Calibration: In Gen 3 Emerald battle box, the EXP bar is 64 pixels wide (X: 158..221).
- Move Targeting Formats:
  - Slot 3 (Absorb, bottom-left): Input `Down` then `Left` from any move position.
  - Slot 4 (Quick Attack, bottom-right): Input `Down` then `Right` from any move position. Quick Attack cleanly OHKOs wild Zubat Lv 7 before it can act.
- Quick Attack (Slot 4) OHKO Thresholds: At Level 22 (Atk 37), Quick Attack consistently OHKOs wild Zubat Lv 7-8 and wild Makuhita Lv 6-7 with +1 priority, taking 0 damage and preventing Sand Attack accuracy drops.
