# Location: Granite Cave

## Overview
A rugged, multi-level cavern on Route 106 north of Dewford Town. Contains wild Pokémon, complex lower floors requiring Flash for visibility and the Mach Bike for mud slopes, and the inner chamber where Steven Stone is studying rocks.

## Connection Points
- South Entrance (1F): Connects to Route 106 beach (interior warp at 37, 12).
- Interior Ladders: Connect 1F to B1F, B2F, and Steven's chamber.

## Landmarks & NPCs (1F Entrance Chamber)
- Entrance Warp: (37, 12) leading south to Route 106 beach.
- Flash Hiker: Located near entrance at roughly (36, 8); gives HM05 Flash!

## Progression Checklist - Granite Cave
- [ ] Receive HM05 Flash from Hiker near 1F entrance.
- [ ] Navigate through Granite Cave to Steven's chamber.
- [ ] Deliver President Stone's Letter to Steven (receive TM47 Steel Wing).
- [ ] Return to Dewford Town to challenge Gym Leader Brawly.
