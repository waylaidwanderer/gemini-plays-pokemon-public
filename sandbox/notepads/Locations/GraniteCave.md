# Location: Granite Cave

## Overview
A rugged, multi-level cavern on Route 106 north of Dewford Town. Contains wild Pokémon, complex lower floors requiring Flash for visibility and the Mach Bike for mud slopes, and the inner chamber where Steven Stone is studying rocks.

## Connection Points
- South Entrance (1F): Connects to Route 106 beach (interior warp at 37, 12).
- Interior Ladders: Connect 1F to B1F, B2F, and Steven's chamber.

## Landmarks & NPCs (1F Entrance Chamber)
- Entrance Warp: (37, 12) leading south to Route 106 beach.
- Flash Hiker: Located at (36, 9) facing East; gave HM05 Flash! (Obtained!).
- Granite Cave 1F Topography:
  - Entrance: (37, 12) at the southeast corner.
  - Flash Hiker: (36, 9) directly north of entrance.
  - 1F Main Path: Leads west from the Hiker along row 7/8.
  - Item Ball: [COLLECTED] Escape Rope at (17, 7).
  - Southwest Ladder: Located at (17, 11) leading down to optional basement B1F.
  - Northeast Plateau & Steven's Chamber: Doorway to Steven's chamber is located in the northeast section of 1F! The row 6 jump ledge is a one-way return shortcut back to the entrance after visiting Steven.
  - Wild Encounters (1F): Zubat, Geodude, Makuhita (Lv 9-10), Abra.

## Granite Cave B1F & B2F (Optional Deep Dungeon)
- B1F & B2F are completely optional lower cavern floors for catching Aron, Sableye, Abra, and collecting items (requires Mach Bike and Rock Smash for full exploration).
- B1F Southwest Ladder at (4, 21) connects to 1F (17, 11). Southeast Ladder at (28, 21) connects to B2F (28, 21).
- B2F Northwest corner features mud slope at (4, 13) and Rock Smash boulders at (2, 16), (3, 14), (5, 14). (2, 15) is solid cliff wall. B2F does not lead to Steven.
