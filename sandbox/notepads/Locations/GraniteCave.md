# Location: Granite Cave

## Overview
A rugged, multi-level cavern on Route 106 north of Dewford Town. Contains wild Pokémon, complex lower floors requiring Flash for visibility and the Mach Bike for mud slopes, and the inner chamber where Steven Stone is studying rocks.

## Connection Points
- South Entrance (1F): Connects to Route 106 beach (interior warp at 37, 12).
- Interior Ladders: Connect 1F to B1F, B2F, and Steven's chamber.

## Landmarks & NPCs (1F Entrance Chamber)
- Entrance Warp: (37, 12) leading south to Route 106 beach.
- Flash Hiker: Located at (36, 9) facing East; gives HM05 Flash! (Obtained!).
- Row 6 Ledge: A one-way jump ledge spanning cols 30..35 at row 6, allowing players to jump south from the upper ridge down to the Hiker / entrance area.
- Northern Elevated Plateau: Contains a ladder down to B1F at roughly (34..35, 5).
- Main Path: Wide corridor at rows 7..9 leading West beneath the row 6 ledge, looping north and east around to the upper plateau.
- Western Corridor: Runs along rows 6..8 from col 30 to col 18.
- Southwest Item Ball: [COLLECTED] Escape Rope at (17, 7).
- Southwest Ladder: Located at (17, 11) leading down to B1F at (4, 21).

## Granite Cave B1F
- Southwest Ladder (to 1F): Located at (4, 21). Player arrives at (4, 22).
- Visibility: Dark cavern (illuminated circle of radius ~2 tiles around player).
- North Ramp to Upper Plateau: Begins at (21..22, 6) leading to northern walkway and Northeast Ladder at (35, 5).

## Wild Pokémon (Granite Cave)
- Zubat, Geodude, Makuhita, Abra, Aron.
