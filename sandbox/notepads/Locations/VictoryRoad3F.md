# Victory Road 3F - Layout & Topology

## Ladders & Subsector Connectivity (VERIFIED Turn 31620)
- 3F is divided into TWO isolated vertical elevation levels:
  1. **Upper Dark Plateau (rows 0-7, cols 0-29)**:
     - Elevated checkerboard floor containing Ladder NE @ (27, 7), Eastern Plateau (cols 20-23) with Boulder 1 @ (22, 3), Northern Highway (Row 1, cols 6-23), Central Bridge (cols 6-7), NW Room (cols 0-5) with Switch Plate @ (3, 5), and NW Ladder @ (2, 0).
     - Arriving from 2F via **Ladder NE @ (27, 7)** places the player directly on this Upper Dark Plateau with immediate unobstructed access to Boulder 1 and the puzzle route!
  2. **Lower Purple Floor (rows 2-17, cols 1-29)**:
     - Separated from Upper Plateau by south-facing cliff ledges and solid rock walls:
       - Row 6 rock wall blocks vertical passage from (0..5, 7) north to NW room. Tile (1, 6) is solid rock.
       - Row 11 rock wall blocks vertical passage at (5..11, 11). Tile (9, 11) is solid rock.
       - Tile (7, 10) / (7, 9) is a solid rock protrusion (not a staircase).
       - Closed Shutter @ (17, 5) separates the northern purple room from the southern pit sector until Switch (3, 5) is activated.
     - Contains Ladder B @ (25, 14), The Pit @ (23, 15), and Pit Boulder @ (22, 15).

## Master Boulder 1 to Switch (3, 5) Solution (VERIFIED Turn 30921, 31486)
1. Arrive on 3F via Ladder NE at (27, 7) or NW Ladder at (2, 0) (player is on Upper Dark Plateau).
2. Activate Strength with ATLAS (Machop).
3. Walk directly along Dark Plateau to (22, 4) behind Boulder 1 at (22, 3).
4. Push North 2 times to (22, 1) on Row 1 (Northern Highway) [Boulder at (22, 1), Player at (22, 2)].
5. Reposition around Boulder: Step Right 1 to (23, 2) -> Step Up 1 to (23, 1) [Player at (23, 1) facing West].
6. Push West 16 times along Row 1: Boulder moves from (22, 1) to (6, 1) [Player ends at (7, 1)].
7. Reposition at Bridge Junction: Step Up 1 to (7, 0) -> Step Left 1 to (6, 0) [Player at (6, 0) facing South].
8. Push South 1 time onto Central Bridge: Boulder moves to (6, 2) [Player ends at (6, 1)].
9. Reposition to Bridge Entrance: Step Right 1 to (7, 1) -> Step Down 1 to (7, 2) [Player at (7, 2) facing West].
10. Push West 4 times into NW Room: Boulder moves from (6, 2) to (2, 2) [Player ends at (3, 2)].
11. Reposition in NW Room: Step Up 1 to (3, 1) -> Step Left 1 to (2, 1) [Player at (2, 1) facing South].
12. Push South 3 times down Column 2: Boulder moves from (2, 2) to (2, 5) [Player ends at (2, 4)].
13. Reposition at Switch Plate: Step Left 1 to (1, 4) -> Step Down 1 to (1, 5) [Player at (1, 5) facing East].
14. Push East 1 time onto Switch Plate (3, 5): Boulder moves to (3, 5) [ON SWITCH!].
15. Outcome: Switch (3, 5) ACTIVATED! Shutters at (17, 5), (15, 15), (21, 15), and (1, 9) OPEN!

## Pit Drop to 2F & Exit Gauntlet (Continuous Single Session)
1. Walk from NW Room through opened Shutter (17, 5) to (21, 15) beside Pit Boulder (22, 15).
2. Push Pit Boulder East into Pit at (23, 15).
3. Step East into Pit (23, 15) -> Drop to 2F at (22, 16) with fallen boulder at (23, 16).
4. On 2F: Push fallen boulder East along Row 16 to (28, 16) -> North along Col 28 to (28, 11) -> West across Row 11 onto Switch Plate.
5. Exit Shutter opens -> Ascend exit ladder to Route 23 North / Indigo Plateau!
