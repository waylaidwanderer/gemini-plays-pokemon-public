# Seafoam Islands B2F Geography & Layout

## Overview
- Cavern level of Seafoam Islands complex (elevation designated B2F).
- Arrived via ladder ascent from B3F at (25, 4) [Turn 29262].

## Key Landmarks & Layout
- Ladder (NE): Located at (25, 3) [Discovered Turn 29262]. Descent ladder returning to B3F (arrives at (25, 4) on B3F).
- Northern Terrace: Rows 0..4 across cols 21..28 form wide open dry cave floor.
- Dividing Cliff: Row 5 rock wall spans cols 21..28+, dividing northern terrace from southern floor.
- Southern Chamber: Rows 6..8 across cols 21..27 are confirmed dry cave floor (no water). Path to southern area wraps around cliff to the west.

## Empirical Floor Proof (B2F vs B3F)
- B3F (Lower): Has signpost at (23, 1) ('DANGER / Fast current!'), wooden dock at (23, 5), fast water current at rows 6..7.
- B2F (Current): Empty floor at (23, 1), rock wall at (23, 5), dry cave floor at rows 6..8.
