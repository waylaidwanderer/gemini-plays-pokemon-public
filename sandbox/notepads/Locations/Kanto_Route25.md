# Route 25 Geography & Points of Interest

## Connections
- West: Route 24 at (0, 8..9).
- East: Sea Cottage (Bill's House).

## Geography & Layout
- Western Gateway (Cols 0..5+, Rows 8..9): 2-tile wide pale mint avenue connecting directly from Route 24.
- Southern Barrier (Row 10): Line of stone bollards bounding the avenue from the southern lawn.
- Northern Elevation (Row 7): Brown elevation ridge bounding the avenue from the northern plateau.
- Northern Tree Canopy (Cols 2..5+, Rows 4..5): Dense forest canopy north of row 6 grass corridor.
