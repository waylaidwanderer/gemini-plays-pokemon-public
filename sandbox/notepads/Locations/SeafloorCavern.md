# Location: Seafloor Cavern

## Overview
An ancient subterranean cavern beneath Route 128, accessible only by diving into the deep oceanic trench.
- Story Climax: Team Aqua and Archie have taken the Submarine Explorer 1 here to reach the slumbering Legendary Pokémon Kyogre and awaken it using the Red Orb.
- Objectives:
  1. Surface from the underwater submarine docking cove into Room 1.
  2. Navigate the boulder strength puzzles and current water currents.
  3. Defeat Team Aqua Grunts and Admins.
  4. Reach the deepest shrine and confront Archie before Kyogre is awakened!

## Connection Points
- Submarine Cove (Underwater): Warp entrance from Route 128 underwater trench at cols 37..38, row 25. Submarine Explorer 01 is moored here at (6, 7).
- Surfacing warp: Pressing B / using Dive in the submarine cove surfaces directly into Seafloor Cavern Room 1.
