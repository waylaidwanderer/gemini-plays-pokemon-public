# Location: Seafloor Cavern

## Overview
An ancient subterranean cavern beneath Route 128, accessible only by diving into the deep oceanic trench.
- Story Climax: Team Aqua and Archie have taken the Submarine Explorer 1 here to reach the slumbering Legendary Pokémon Kyogre and awaken it using the Red Orb.
- Objectives:
  1. Surface from the underwater submarine docking cove into Room 1.
  2. Navigate the boulder strength puzzles and current water currents.
  3. Defeat Team Aqua Grunts and Admins.
  4. Reach the deepest shrine and confront Archie before Kyogre is awakened!

## Connection Points
- Submarine Cove (Underwater): Warp entrance from Route 128 underwater trench at cols 37..38, row 25. Submarine Explorer 01 is moored here at (6, 7).
- Surfacing warp: Pressing B / using Dive in the submarine cove surfaces directly into Seafloor Cavern Room 1.

## Discovered Topography & Navigation - Room 1 (Entrance Chamber)
- Surfacing Point: May surfaces at (10, 17) facing South in the entrance water pool.
- Entrance Pool: Extends from row 10 down to row 18 (south cliff wall at row 19).
- Northern Shoreline: Located at row 10 (cols 8..12). May disembarks onto land at (10, 10).
- Northern Avenue: Dry sandy cavern floor extending North from row 9 between rock bluffs at cols 0..4 (west) and cols 16..20 (east).
- Northern Cave Doorway: Located at (10, 1), entered stepping North from (10, 2). Leads to Seafloor Cavern Room 2.
## Discovered Topography & Navigation - Room 2
- Entrance: South doorway from Room 1 at (5, 19). Player spawns at (5, 18) facing North.
- Western Corridor: Cols 4..5 run North past western rock wall.
- Central Rock Mound: Rises at cols 6..12, rows 10..15.
- Southern Corridor: Row 18 runs East below the central rock mound.
