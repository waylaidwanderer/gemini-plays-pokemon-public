# Cerulean Cave (Unknown Dungeon) B1F - Layout & Mewtwo

## Overview
- Mewtwo's basement chamber in Cerulean Cave.
- Reached via the 2F pit drop at (24, 17) landing at (4, 11) -> (4, 12).

## Verified Topography & Landmarks
- **Western Arrival Platform**:
  - Spans cols 0-5, rows 11-13.
  - Landing spot: (4, 11) -> (4, 12).
  - Northern Golden Plateau: (0..4, 8..10) accessed via checkerboard slope at Row 11.
  - Southern Shoreline: (0..5, 14) facing south into blue water canal at (0..6, 15..16).
- **Water Canal Network**:
  - Spans (0..6, 15..16) and vertical canal along Column 6 (rows 8..14).
  - Separates western platform from eastern platform.
- **Eastern Platform**:
  - Stone pillar barrier along Column 7 (rows 8..17).
  - Blue staircase at (8, 10..11) and orange structure at (9, 11).
- **North Waterway Basin**:
  - Spans cols 6..11 across rows 4..5, connecting Column 6 canal east past the Column 7 stone pillar barrier.
  - Connects south to the Eastern Platform green terrace at (8..11, 6..7).
- **Eastern Platform Complex**:
  - Rows 6..7: Green lawn/trees at (8..11, 6..7).
  - Rows 8..9: Golden patterned platform at (8..11, 8..9).
  - Rows 10..11: Blue staircase at (8, 10..11) and orange entrance structure at (9, 11).
## Verified Mewtwo Encounter & Capture Protocol
- **Mewtwo Location**: Stationary boss sprite positioned at (11, 31) on the central wooden platform.
- **Access Route**: Disembark from water at Dock (6, 7) onto northern platform (6, 6) -> East to (11, 6) -> South down Columns 10-11 platform through rows 7-30 directly to Mewtwo at (11, 31).
- **Verified Inventory**: Master Ball in Bag (Slot 18).
- **Encounter Execution**:
  1. Stand adjacent to Mewtwo at (11, 30) facing South or (10, 31) facing East.
  2. Press `A` to initiate dialogue / wild battle.
  3. Turn 1: Select `ITEM` -> Scroll to `MASTER BALL` -> Press `A` to throw Master Ball.
  4. Complete capture and nickname Mewtwo.
