# Location: Route 124

## Overview
A massive open ocean route in eastern Hoenn connecting Lilycove City on the west to Mossdeep City on the east.
- Features: Deep water trenches (dive spots), scattered rocky islets, swimmer trainers, and the Treasure Hunter's house (Shard collector).
- Destination: Mossdeep City (Gym 7 - Tate & Liza, Mind Badge).

## Connection Points
- West: Lilycove City eastern shore (cove at col 70..74).
- East: Mossdeep City western entrance.
- North: Route 125.
- South: Route 126.

## Progression Checklist - Route 124
1. [ ] Surf east from Lilycove City through the sea rock passage.
2. [ ] Navigate across Route 124 to Mossdeep City.
3. [ ] Register checkpoint at Mossdeep City Pokémon Center.
4. [ ] Challenge Gym 7 (Tate & Liza) for the Mind Badge.

## Discovered Topography & Navigation
- Lilycove Eastern Sea Exit:
  - Sandbar shallows end at cols 68..69.
  - Deep water begins at col 70, row 24.
  - Sea rocks cluster at (75..78, 17..25): rock at (74, 25), grey rocks at (75, 24) and (75, 20), brown rocks at (76, 21..22) and (76, 17..18).
  - Clean bypass routes North along col 74 to row 16, then turns East into the wide open ocean corridor leading to Route 124.
