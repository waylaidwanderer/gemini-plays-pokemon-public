# Location: Meteor Falls

## Overview
A majestic cave formed by meteorite impacts, famous for its roaring waterfall and rare Dragon-type Pokémon. Connects Route 114 in the north to Route 115 in the west.

## Connection Points
- Route 114 Entrance: Connects to southern Route 114 at (8, 63).
- Route 115 Exit: Western exit leading out to the beach north of Rustboro City.

## Key Landmarks
- 1F Main Chamber: Large cavern with a bridge spanning the waterfall pool.
- Team Magma & Team Aqua Confrontation: Story event where Team Magma Leader Maxie / Admins steal Professor Cozmo's Meteorite, confronted by Team Aqua Leader Archie!
- High Waterfall: Requires HM07 Waterfall to climb to back chambers (B1F, B2F - Bagon chamber, TM02 Dragon Claw).

## Story Objectives
- Rescue Professor Cozmo.
- Confront Team Magma and retrieve or witness the Meteorite theft.
- Head to Mt. Chimney to stop Team Magma's volcanic eruption plot.
