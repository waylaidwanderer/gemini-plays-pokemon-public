# Location: Route 128

## Overview
A critical oceanic route in southeastern Hoenn, connecting Route 127 on the north to Ever Grande City on the east and Route 129 on the south.
- Environment: Open sea, sandbars, sea rocks, swimmer trainers, and the deep underwater trench housing the entrance to Seafloor Cavern.
- Story Climax: Team Aqua has taken the stolen Submarine Explorer 1 to the bottom of Route 128's underwater trench to infiltrate Seafloor Cavern and awaken Kyogre with the Red Orb!

## Connection Points
- North: Route 127 (entered at col 27, row 0).
- East: Ever Grande City (Victory Road / Pokémon League entrance; mountain barrier at col 102).
- South: Route 129 (boundary at row 40/41 at col 32 and col 43).

## Mechanics: Surfacing and Diving
- Overworld Dive Activation: Pressing 'B' while surfing on the surface does NOT activate Dive (B is only for surfacing while already underwater).
- Verified Submerge Protocol: Stand on a suspected dark water tile and either press 'A' (triggers Dive prompt: 'The sea is deep here. Would you like to use DIVE?') or open the Party Menu (`Start -> POKéMON -> LILZ -> DIVE`). If on valid dive tile, Lilz will use Dive to submerge underwater.
