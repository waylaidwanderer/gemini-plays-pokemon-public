# Location: Rustboro City

## Overview
A large metropolitan city in western Hoenn known for the Devon Corporation and the first Pokémon Gym.

## Connection Points
- South Exit: Connects to Route 104 North at (17, 58..59).
- East Exit: Connects to Route 116.
- North-East Entrance: Rusturf Tunnel (via Route 116).

## Key Buildings & Landmarks
- Pokémon Center: Located at (16, 38) (red roof, door at 16, 38).
- Poké Mart: Located at (16, 44) (blue roof, door at 16, 44).
- Cutter's House: Located at (9, 38) directly west of Pokémon Center. House signpost at (12, 38). Gives HM01 Cut!
- Pokémon Trainer's School: Located at (27, 33) east of Pokémon Center. Signpost at (25, 35). Teacher at blackboard gives Quick Claw!
- Rustboro Gym: Located at (27, 18) in north-central Rustboro City. Gym signpost at (23, 19). Leader: Roxanne (Rock-type).
- Devon Corporation Headquarters: Large multi-story building in the north. Company signpost at (17, 20).
