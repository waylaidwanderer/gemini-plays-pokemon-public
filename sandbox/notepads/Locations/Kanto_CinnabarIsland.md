# Cinnabar Island Geography & Points of Interest

## Overview
- Volcanic island town in southern Kanto.
- Connected to Pallet Town via Route 21 (north) and Seafoam Islands / Fuchsia City via Route 20 (east).

## Landmarks & Layout

- Pokémon Mansion: Large building in northwest occupying columns 4 to 9, rows 0 to 3, with entrance door at (6, 3) [Verified Turns 13676, 13678].
