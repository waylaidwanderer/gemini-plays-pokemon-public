# Locations/PokemonMansion2F - Map & Layout Log

## 2F West Layout & Physical Barriers
- **Stairs to 1F West:** Located at `(7, 10)`. Landing from 1F West places player at `(7, 11)`.
- **Stairs to 3F West:** Located at `(6, 1)`. Connects to 3F West corridor.
- **Mewtwo Switch at `(2, 11)`:** Active secret switch on 2F West. Interacting from `(2, 12)` facing UP toggles the Mansion state between State A and State B (verified Turns 73065, 74302).
- **Column 9 Shutter Gates `(9, 4)` & `(9, 5)`:**
  - **State A:** OPEN (allows horizontal crossing between 2F West and 2F East).
  - **State B:** CLOSED (blocks horizontal crossing).
- **Row 10 Corridor:** Continuous corridor along Row 10 connecting West and East wings.

## 2F East Main Wing
- **East Shutter Gate `(18, 8)` & `(19, 8)`:**
  - **State A:** CLOSED.
  - **State B:** OPEN (verified Turn 73513).
- **Stairs to 1F East:** Located in the eastern chamber past the gate.
- **Pitfall Landing at `(18, 14)`:** Dropping through 3F East pitfall at `(19, 14)` lands player at `(18, 14)`.

## 2F Southeastern Chamber
- **Stairs at `(25, 14)`:** Connects directly between 2F Southeastern Chamber and 3F East at `(25, 14)`.

## 2F Balcony Chamber (Isolated Enclosed Sector)
- **Access:** Reachable ONLY by dropping through 3F East pitfall at `(17, 14)`. Player lands at `(16, 14)`.
- **Scientist Trainer at `(17, 17)`:** Defeated on Turn 74424 (Electrode Lv 29, Weezing Lv 29; awarded ¥1450).
- **Balcony Barriers:** Hedges and urns on Rows 18-19 and 22-23.
- **Corridors:** Columns 12-13 and Columns 20-23 bypass hedges south to Row 26.
- **Item Ball:** Verified no item ball at `(18, 21)` (empty pink floor - Turn 74468).
- **Railing on Row 27:** Impassable continuous solid railing across Columns 12-23 (tested Turns 74440 & 74456).
- **Balcony Chamber Exit:** The ONLY exit from this isolated chamber is the ascending staircase at `(21, 23)` warping to 3F East at `(23, 22)`. It does NOT physically connect to the rest of 2F.
