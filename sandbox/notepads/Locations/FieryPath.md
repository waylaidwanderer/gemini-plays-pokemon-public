# Location: Fiery Path

## Overview
A subterranean volcanic cavern carved directly through the interior of Mt. Chimney. Serves as the vital bypass connecting the southern section of Route 112 to the northern section of Route 111 and Route 113. Contains wild Fire, Rock, and Poison Pokémon (Numel, Slugma, Torkoal, Koffing, Grimer, Machop).

## Connection Points
- South Entrance/Exit: Route 112 (warp at south end, leads to Route 112 upper terrace at 11, 35).
- North Entrance/Exit: Route 111 North / Route 113 (leads out toward the volcanic ash route to Fallarbor Town).
- Side Corridor (Optional): Blocked by heavy Strength boulders at row 27; requires Heat Badge (Gym 4 - Flannery) to use HM04 Strength outside battle. Return here after Gym 4!

## Progression Checklist - Fiery Path
1. [x] Enter Fiery Path from Route 112 south entrance.
2. [x] Navigate through the main cave tunnel north toward the northern exit.
3. [ ] Optional: Push Strength boulders with HM04 Strength to collect hidden items (requires Heat Badge).
4. [ ] Exit northern mouth onto Route 111 / Route 113.

## Wild Pokémon
- Numel (Fire/Ground), Slugma (Fire, Lv 15 verified), Torkoal (Fire), Koffing (Poison, Lv 15 verified), Grimer (Poison, Lv 14 verified), Machop (Fighting, Lv 15 verified).

## Discovered Coordinates & Layout
- South Entrance Warp: (26, 36), player enters at (26, 35) facing North (leads outside to Route 112 at 11, 35).
- Main Cavern: South section (cols 25..27) heads North to row 22, turns West to col 20, then runs straight North along cols 18..20 toward northern exit.
- Strength Boulder: Located at (17, 13) blocking the west side corridor. Main path bypasses on the east along cols 18..20.
- Northeast Alcove: (25, 5) is the rock ledge next to the exit. The true northern exit doorway and mat are located at (26..27, 5)! Approach from (26, 4) or (27, 4) and step Down to exit onto Route 111 North.
