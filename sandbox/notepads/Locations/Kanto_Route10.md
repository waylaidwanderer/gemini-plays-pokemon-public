# Route 10 Geography & Points of Interest

## Connections
- West: Route 9 (connects at cols 0..1, rows 6..9 to Route 9 (59..60, 6..9); Route 9 (59, 8) <-> Route 10 (0, 8) [Turn 4517]).
- South: Lavender Town (via Rock Tunnel).
- North: Power Plant (via water canal, requires Surf).

## Geography & Layout
- Western Entrance (Cols 0..1, Rows 6..9): Wide open pale mint paved avenue connecting from Route 9.
- Row 5: Stone structures at cols 0..1 and 4..5 with opening at cols 2..3.
- Row 10: Elevation boundary with diagonal corner at (1, 10).
- Row 11: South-facing ledges across cols 2..5.
- Central Lawn (Cols 2..3, Rows 6..10): Open green lawn strip.
- Eastern Tall Grass Strip (Cols 4..5+, Rows 6..10): Tall grass field on eastern perimeter.
