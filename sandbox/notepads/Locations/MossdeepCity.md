# Location: Mossdeep City

## Overview
A peaceful island city located on the eastern edge of Hoenn, surrounded by ocean waters. Known for its space research program and psychic twin Gym Leaders.
- Environment: Island bluffs, white stone staircases, sandy beaches, space launch facilities.
- Key Goal: Earn the Mind Badge from Gym Leaders Tate & Liza (Gym 7) to unlock HM08 Dive outside battle.

## Connection Points
- West: Route 124 (open sea).
- North: Route 125 (leading to Shoal Cave).
- South: Route 127.

## Key Landmarks & Buildings
- Western Shore / Beaches: Disembarkation points from Route 124.
- Pokémon Center: Located on the lower western tier of the city. Primary checkpoint!
- Poké Mart: Located near the center of town.
- Mossdeep Gym: Gym 7 (Psychic-type, Double Battle, Leaders Tate & Liza).
- Steven's House: Located on the northwest cliff plateau.
- Mossdeep Space Center: Rocket launch complex on the high eastern plateau.
