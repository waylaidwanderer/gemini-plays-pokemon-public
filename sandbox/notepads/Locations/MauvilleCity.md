# Location: Mauville City

## Overview
A major technological crossroads city in central Hoenn, linking Route 110 (south), Route 117 (west), Route 111 (north), and Route 118 (east). Home to the Mauville Gym (Leader Wattson), Rydel's Cycles (Bicycle), the Game Corner, and the Mauville Battle Tent.

## Connection Points
- South Entrance: Route 110 (arrives at 15, 16 on southern cobblestone avenue).
- West Entrance: Route 117 (leading toward Verdanturf Town).
- North Entrance: Route 111 (leading toward Route 112 / Mt. Chimney / Desert).
- East Entrance: Route 118 (leading across the sea toward Fortree City).

## Key Buildings & Landmarks
- Mauville City Signpost: Located in southwest lawn garden near the southern entrance.
- Southern Residential House: Pink roof, yellow siding, purple door at row 12..13, col 19.
- Pokémon Center: Red roof building (priority checkpoint and party heal).
- Poké Mart: Blue roof building.
- Rydel's Cycles: Bicycle shop where Rydel provides a free Mach Bike or Acro Bike!
- Mauville Gym: Gym Leader Wattson (Electric-type specialist).
- Game Corner: Sells coins and prizes (TMs, dolls). Requires Coin Case (obtainable from woman in Mauville house in exchange for Harbor Mail).

## Progression Checklist - Mauville City
1. [ ] Locate and enter Pokémon Center; heal party and set checkpoint.

3. [ ] Explore town landmarks (Game Corner, Battle Tent, houses).
