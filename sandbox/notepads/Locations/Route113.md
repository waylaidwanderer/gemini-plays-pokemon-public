# Location: Route 113

## Overview
A high-altitude mountain path located north of Mt. Chimney, blanketed in constantly falling volcanic ash. Connects Route 111 North to Fallarbor Town.
- Wild Pokémon encountered: Spinda (Lv 16, defeated), Slugma (Lv 15, fled).

## Connection Points
- East Exit: Route 111 North at (98, 9).
- West Exit: Fallarbor Town.

## Key Landmarks
- Glass Workshop: Home of the glass artisan. Obtained Key Item SOOT SACK! Artisan crafts flutes and furniture from collected volcanic ash (Blue Flute: 250 steps; Yellow Flute: 500 steps; Red Flute: 500 steps; White Flute: 1000 steps; Black Flute: 1000 steps; Pretty Chair: 6000 steps; Pretty Desk: 8000 steps). Located at cols 30..35, rows 2..7; entrance door at (33, 5), accessed from (33, 6) facing North. Workshop sign at (31, 6).

- Volcanic Ash Mounds: Patches of ash that vanish when walked through, revealing hidden items or Ninja Boys.

- Eastern Signpost: Located at (85, 6), read from (85, 7) facing North. Text: "ROUTE 113 / -> ROUTE 111".
- Northern Signpost: Located at (58, 4), read from (58, 5) facing North. Text: "TRAINER TIPS / You may register one of the KEY ITEMS in your BAG as SELECT. / Simply press SELECT to use the registered item."

- Item Ball 2: [COLLECTED] Located at (53, 7) on elevated ledge above row 9, accessible from the north via the row 2 corridor and south down column 53. Item: Max Ether!
