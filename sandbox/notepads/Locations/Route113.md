# Location: Route 113

## Overview
A high-altitude mountain path located north of Mt. Chimney, blanketed in constantly falling volcanic ash. Connects Route 111 North to Fallarbor Town.

## Connection Points
- East Exit: Route 111 North at (98, 9).
- West Exit: Fallarbor Town.

## Key Landmarks
- Glass Workshop: Home of the glass artisan who provides the Soot Sack to collect volcanic ash for crafting flutes (Blue Flute, Yellow Flute, Red Flute, etc.) and furniture.
- Volcanic Ash Mounds: Patches of ash that vanish when walked through, sometimes revealing hidden items or Ninja Boy trainers!

## Discovered Coordinates & Layout
- East entrance from Route 111 North: (98, 9) heading West.
- East corridor: Wide path along rows 8..9 bounded by northern cliff wall and southern ledges.
