# Location: Route 113

## Overview
A high-altitude mountain path located north of Mt. Chimney, blanketed in constantly falling volcanic ash. Connects Route 111 North to Fallarbor Town.

## Connection Points
- East Exit: Route 111 North at (98, 9).
- West Exit: Fallarbor Town.

## Key Landmarks
- Glass Workshop: Home of the glass artisan who provides the Soot Sack to collect volcanic ash for crafting flutes (Blue Flute, Yellow Flute, Red Flute, etc.) and furniture.
- Volcanic Ash Mounds: Patches of ash that vanish when walked through, sometimes revealing hidden items or Ninja Boy trainers!

## Discovered Coordinates & Layout
- East entrance from Route 111 North: (98, 9) heading West.
- East corridor: Wide path along rows 8..9 bounded by northern cliff wall and southern ledges.
- Eastern Signpost: Located at (85, 6), read from (85, 7) facing North. Text: "ROUTE 113 / -> ROUTE 111".
- Item Ball: Located at (79, 6) on the upper ledge west of col 80. Ledge at col 80 faces east (one-way dropoff), so the item ball must be accessed from the upper path to the west.
- Upper Trainer: Trainer spotted on upper level around (73, 2) in the trees.
- Ash Grass Patch 1: Located at cols 80..84, rows 6..11. Walking through ash grass knocks off the ash.
- Main Path West: Lower corridor continues west along rows 9..11 beneath the row 8 ledge.
- Rock Mound 1: Located at cols 64..68, rows 4..8 with one-way ledge along south (row 10) and east (col 68).
- Lower Path: Open corridor along row 11 (cols 60..68) bypassing Rock Mound 1 to the south.
- NPC Girl: Located at (66, 12). Dialogue: "It's fun walking through the volcano's ashes on the ground and grass. You can see where you walked--it's really neat!"
