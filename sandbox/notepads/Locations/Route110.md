# Location: Route 110

## Overview
A major north-south corridor connecting Slateport City (south) to Mauville City (north). Features the elevated Seaside Cycling Road, the lower pedestrian path, the Trick House, and various trainers.

## Connection Points
- South Entrance: Slateport City (gate at 18, 10; Route 110 begins at 18, 97).
- North Entrance: Mauville City (near row 0).
- Seaside Cycling Road: Elevated highway requiring a Bicycle (entered via toll gate buildings at south and north ends).
