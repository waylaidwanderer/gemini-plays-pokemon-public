# Location: Fallarbor Town

## Overview
A farming community blanketed in ash from Mt. Chimney. Located at the western end of Route 113 and the northern end of Route 114.
- Mottos/Signs: "A farm community with small gardens."

## Connection Points
- East Exit: Route 113 at (19, 10).
- West / South Exit: Route 114.

## Key Landmarks
- Pokémon Center: Red-roof building located in north-central area. Blue sliding glass door entrance is at (13..14, 7); right facade with P.C sign is at (15..16, 7). Enter from (14, 8) facing North. Checkpoint destination!
- Battle Tent: Red/white striped tent building located immediately west of Pokémon Center (entrance around x=10..11, y=7).
- Poké Mart: Blue-roof building located in southern area (around x=14..16, y=14..16).
- Professor Cozmo's House: Located in the western part of town.
- Move Relearner / Move Tutor House: Resident who teaches forgotten moves in exchange for Heart Scales.

## Discovered Coordinates & Layout
- Eastern Entrance from Route 113: (19, 10).
- Central paved path runs east-west along row 10.
- Paved branch heads north from (14..15, 10) up rows 9, 8 to Pokémon Center entrance at (14..15, 7).
- NPCs:
  - Old Man NPC: Wandering near path crossroad at (10, 8..9).
  - Little Girl NPC & Azurill: Standing near signpost at (9..10, 11..12).
