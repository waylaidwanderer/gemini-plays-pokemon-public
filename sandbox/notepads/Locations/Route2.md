# Route 2 - Overworld Mapping & Navigation

## Map Connections & Overworld Transitions
- **Diglett's Cave Entrance Building:**
  - Located on the eastern strip of Route 2.
  - Entrance door is at `(12, 9)` on Route 2 overworld.
  - Entering the building and taking the doormat at `(3, 7)` exits south back to Route 2 overworld at `(12, 10)`.
  - Standing on the ladder inside at `(4, 4)` warps the player back down to Diglett's Cave main tunnel at `(5, 5)`.

- **Route 2 Gatehouse (Aide's House) - NOT A THROUGH-PASSAGE:**
  - Located south of the Diglett's Cave building.
  - Entrance door is at `(15, 19)`, facing SOUTH.
  - **EMPERICAL EVIDENCE (Turn 45522):** Inside the gatehouse, a solid chain-link fence completely separates the left (west) hallway from the right (east) room. It is physically **impossible** to walk from the eastern strip to the western main road internally through this building.
  - **Winding Internal Path (within Eastern portion only):** Entering from `(15, 19)` warps inside to `(2, 7)`. To exit to the northern portion of the eastern strip, walk around the tables and Scientist: go right to `(5, 7)`, UP to `(5, 1)`, LEFT to `(2, 1)/(3, 1)`, then UP to exit to the northern overworld.

- **Southern Gatehouse - THE TRUE EAST-WEST TRANSITION:**
  - Located at the southern end of the eastern strip.
  - **Overworld Entrance (East Side):** Accessible from the pocket at `(16, 35)`. Walking DOWN from `(16, 35)` triggers a warp inside.
  - **Internal Transition:** Warps inside the gatehouse to `(5, 1)`.
  - **Overworld Exit (West/South Side):** Walking DOWN to `(4, 7)/(5, 7)` exits the building south onto the main road of Route 2 at `(15, 40)`. This connects directly to the southern main road leading to Viridian City.

## Eastern Strip Landmarks & Barriers (Verified Turn 45504 - 45541)
- **South Ledge Barrier (Row 15):**
  - A horizontal grey brick fence on Row 15 stretches across Columns 8-13, completely blocking direct south passage on those columns.
  - **Bypass Route:** Walk right from `(12, 14)` to Column 14/15 on Row 14 (into the open green grass area), then walk south past the fence line to Row 16.
- **Cuttable Bush (Row 22):**
  - A cuttable bush located at `(15, 22)` blocks the eastern corridor going south.
  - **Cleared (Turn 45528):** Hacked away using TRUFFLE's CUT, opening the path further south.
