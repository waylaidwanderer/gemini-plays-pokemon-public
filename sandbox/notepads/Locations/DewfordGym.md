# Location: Dewford Gym

## Overview
Dewford Town Gym, led by Gym Leader Brawly (Fighting-type).
The gym features a dynamic darkness mechanic: defeating gym trainers expands the spotlight radius (initial radius ~2 -> defeated Cristian radius ~3 -> defeated Takao radius ~4 -> defeated Laura radius ~5).

## Badges & Rewards
- Knuckle Badge: Boosts Pokémon obedience up to Lv 30 and enables HM05 Flash outside battle!
- TM08 Bulk Up: Awarded upon defeating Brawly.

## Gym Topography by Sector

   - Entrance door mat at (5..6, 27); Gym Guide at (5, 24) facing South.
   - Flanked by statues at (4, 25) and (7, 23).
   - Northward passage at row 21 is blocked by a horizontal water canal, forcing an eastern bypass along row 22.

2. Sector 2: East Wing & Water Loops (cols 8-14, rows 11-22)
   - Horizontal walkway along row 22 connects cols 8-13.
   - Column 8 pier (rows 18-20) dead-ends north at water (8, 17).
   - Column 11-12 Bypass Corridor: Column 12 runs between row 8 junction and row 15 (dead-ends south at row 16 wall). At (12, 15), walkway jogs west to (11, 15). Column 11 runs south from row 15 down to (11, 19), then jogs east to (12, 19) and south to row 22 walkway. Bypasses water canal at (12, 18).
   - Column 13-16 Corridor: Row 22 dead-ends east at (14, 22) beneath the water canal. The true East Wing corridor branches east from (12, 19) into (13, 19), then jogs east past water dead-ends at (14, 17) and (15, 17) to column 16. Column 16 is the true unobstructed vertical corridor (rows 13-18) leading north to Sailor Brenden and Battle Girl Lilith at rows 11-12.
   - Row 8 walkway runs west from (12, 8) to (9, 8) where Black Belt Cristian stands guard.
   - Southeast Branch: Column 12 runs south from (12, 22) to (12, 25), dead-ending at row 26 south wall. Battle Girl Laura was stationed at (12, 24). Completely cleared.

3. Sector 3: Central Connector & Hub (cols 5-12, rows 14-16)
   - Row 14 walkway extends west from (11, 14) beneath row 13 water canal across cols 7-11 to (5, 14).
   - Column 5-6 corridor connects row 14 south to row 16.
   - Row 16 bridge runs west from (5, 16) through (4..3, 16) between water (row 15) and wall (row 17) into the West Wing.

4. Sector 4: West Wing & Northern Lookout (cols 1-3, rows 9-18)
   - Column 2 vertical corridor connects to row 16 walkway. Black Belt Takao defeated at (2, 17).
   - Column 1 Pocket: (1, 10) connects north to (1, 9), dead-ending at water at (1, 8).
   - Column 2 North Pier: Tile (2, 9) dead-ends at water at (2, 8).
   - Row 10 East Alcove: Tile (3, 10) dead-ends north at water at (3, 9) and east at wall (4, 10).
   - The horizontal stripes at (2, 7) are retaining cliff walls of the raised central stage rising out of water, not walkable stairs. Sector 4 has no stage access. Completely mapped.

5. Sector 5: Northern Stage Approach & Access Stairs (cols 9-12, rows 1-8)
   - Row 8 Corridor: Extends west from (12, 8) to (9, 8). Black Belt Cristian permanently occupies (9, 8) (verified collision: impassable from east). Column 9 corridor extends south from Cristian's tile, and row 8 extends west behind him, accessible only from the southern/western network.
   - Central Stage Stairs: Confirmed horizontal stairs leading up into the elevated wooden stage at columns 9-10, rows 5-6, flanked by water to the south and east.
   - Upper Alcove: Column 10 dead-ends north at water at (10, 2) in an enclosed chute.
   - Stage Access Route: Sectors 3, 4, and 5 all dead-end at water, walls, or Cristian. Confirmed that the single viable path to Brawly's northern stage is continuing north past Brenden & Lilith along column 16.
