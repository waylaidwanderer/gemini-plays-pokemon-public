# Location: Dewford Gym

## Overview
Dewford Town Gym, led by Gym Leader Brawly (Fighting-type).
The gym features a dynamic darkness mechanic: the interior is shrouded in pitch black, with a small circle of light surrounding the player. Defeating gym trainers expands the circle of light.

## Badges & Rewards
- Knuckle Badge: Boosts Pokémon obedience up to Lv 30 and enables HM05 Flash outside battle!
- TM08 Bulk Up: Awarded upon defeating Brawly.

## Gym Topography & Mechanics
- Entrance Warp: (5, 27) / (6, 27) door mat.
- Entrance Starting Position: (5, 26) facing North.
- Gym Guide: Located at (5, 24) facing South.
- Entrance Left Statue: Located at (4, 25).
- Entrance Right Statue: Located at (7, 23).
- Entrance North Corridor: Column 6 leads north from row 25 to row 22.
- Row 21 Water Canal: Water canal spans rows 20-21 from column 4 through column 7, blocking northward movement in that section.
- Eastern Bypass: Column 8 bypasses the water canal to the east! Tile (8, 21) is open floor.
- Eastern Bypass & Pier: Column 8 runs north from row 22 to row 18, but rows 18-20 are a dead-end pier flanked by water and walls (no east/west exits at rows 18-19, water at 8, 17).
- South Corridor: Row 22 walkway continues east past column 8 through (9, 22) and (11, 22).
- Column 12 Junction at (12, 22): At (12, 22), the corridor branches North into row 21 and South into rows 23-24 between the column 11 and column 13 wall blocks. Directly East at (13, 22) is an impassable brown wall with water above it.
- Column 12 North Corridor: Column 12 continues north through (12, 21) and (12, 20) to (12, 19).
- Row 18 Water Barrier: At (12, 18), blue water blocks northward passage.
- Column 10 Wall: Impassable brown wooden wall at (10, 18..19) blocks westward movement from (11, 19).
- Column 11 North Corridor: Column 11 forms a straight 1-tile wide hallway running north through (11, 18) and (11, 17) to row 15.
- Central Chamber Hub at (11, 15): At row 15, the side walls end and the floor opens wide in all directions (West, East, and North into row 14).
- Light Mechanic: Initial spotlight radius is ~2 tiles. Expands as trainers are defeated.
