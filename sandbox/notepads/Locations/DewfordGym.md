# Location: Dewford Gym

## Overview
Dewford Town Gym, led by Gym Leader Brawly (Fighting-type).
The gym features a dynamic darkness mechanic: the interior is shrouded in pitch black, with a small circle of light surrounding the player. Defeating gym trainers expands the circle of light.

## Badges & Rewards
- Knuckle Badge: Boosts Pokémon obedience up to Lv 30 and enables HM05 Flash outside battle!
- TM08 Bulk Up: Awarded upon defeating Brawly.

## Gym Topography & Mechanics
- Entrance Warp: (5, 27) / (6, 27) door mat.
- Entrance Starting Position: (5, 26) facing North.
- Gym Guide: Located at (5, 24) facing South.
- Entrance Left Statue: Located at (4, 25).
- Entrance Right Statue: Located at (7, 23).
- Entrance North Corridor: Column 6 leads north from row 25 to row 22.
- Row 21 Water Canal: Water canal spans rows 20-21 from column 4 through column 7, blocking northward movement in that section.

- Eastern Bypass & Pier: Column 8 runs north from row 22 to row 18, but rows 18-20 are a dead-end pier flanked by water and walls (no east/west exits at rows 18-19, water at 8, 17).
- South Corridor: Row 22 walkway continues east past column 8 through (9, 22) and (11, 22).
- Column 12 Junction at (12, 22): At (12, 22), the corridor branches North into row 21 and South into rows 23-24 between the column 11 and column 13 wall blocks. Directly East at (13, 22) is an impassable brown wall with water above it.
- Column 12 North Corridor: Column 12 continues north through (12, 21) and (12, 20) to (12, 19).
- Row 18 Water Barrier: At (12, 18), blue water blocks northward passage.
- Column 10 Wall: Impassable brown wooden wall at (10, 18..19) blocks westward movement from (11, 19).
- Column 11 North Corridor: Column 11 forms a straight 1-tile wide hallway running north through (11, 18) and (11, 17) to row 15.
- Central Chamber Hub at (11, 15): At row 15, the side walls end and the floor opens wide in all directions.
- Row 13 Water Canal: Water canal blocks northward passage at (10..11, 13).
- Row 14 Junction at (11, 14): Row 14 extends west, while column 12 provides a northward bypass at (12, 14) and (12, 13) past the water canal.
- Row 14-15 West Corridor: At (11, 14), the corridor leads west into row 14, forming an open 1-tile walkway running west through (10, 14), (9, 14), and (7, 14) to (5, 14).
- Tile (4, 14) Wall: At (5, 14), west into column 4 is blocked by a wall/divider.
- Column 5-6 South Corridor: Leads south from (5, 14) down to (5, 16), dead-ending south at a row 17 wooden wall.
- Row 16 West Walkway: At (5, 16), row 16 forms an open 1-tile walkway running west through (4, 16) and (3, 16) to (2, 16), flanked by water at row 15 to the north and a wooden wall at row 17 to the south.
- Column 2 South Corridor: At (2, 16), the walkway turns south along column 2 past row 17 toward row 18.
- Water Canal Northwest: Blue water visible at (6, 13) bordering the north side of the corridor.
- Column 12 Upper North Corridor: Column 12 forms a straight 1-tile wide hallway running north through (12, 13) to (12, 9), bordered by walls on both sides.
- Row 8 Junction at (12, 8): Column 12 intersects row 8. West (11, 8) runs beneath water at (11, 7). East (13, 8) opens into a wide floor area. Column 12 continues straight north into row 7 along the eastern bank of the water.
- Column 12 North Pier: Column 12 dead-ends at (12, 5) surrounded by water at north (12, 4) and east (13, 5).
- Column 13 North Corridor: Column 13 dead-ends at (13, 6) facing water at (13, 5). Both columns 12 and 13 terminate at the row 4-5 water canal.
- Row 8 West Corridor: From row 8 junction (12, 8), row 8 forms an open horizontal corridor running west through (11, 8) and (10, 8) to (9, 8). Black Belt Cristian stands defeated at (9, 8).
- Column 9 South Corridor: Column 9 runs south beneath Cristian from row 9 down toward row 14.
- Light Mechanic: Initial spotlight radius is ~2 tiles. Expanded to radius ~3 after defeating Cristian!
