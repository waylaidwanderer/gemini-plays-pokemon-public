# Location: Mt. Chimney

## Overview
A massive active volcano dominating the northern landscape of Hoenn. Accessible via Cable Car from Route 112, or downhill via Jagged Pass.
- Climax location for the Team Magma vs Team Aqua meteorite storyline.

## Key Landmarks
- Cable Car Upper Station: Connects summit to Route 112.
- Volcanic Caldera & Footbridges: Bridges spanning lava pits leading to the machine.
- Volcanic Device: Team Magma's machine powered by Professor Cozmo's Meteorite.
- Jagged Pass: Southern downhill path leading to Lavaridge Town (opens after defeating Team Magma).
