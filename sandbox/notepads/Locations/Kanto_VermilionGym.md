# Vermilion Gym

## Geography & Layout
- Entrance Mat: (4..5, 17) facing North. Exit warp south at row 18.
- Statues: Located at (3, 14) and (6, 14) flanking the entrance aisle.
- Gym Guide: Stationed at (4, 14) facing South.
- Central Aisle: Column 4 and Column 5 running north into the main hall.

## Occupants & Trainers
- Gym Guide at (4, 14): Advises that Lt. Surge is the "Lightning American", an expert on Electric Pokémon. Birds and Water Pokémon are at risk, and beware of paralysis. Lt. Surge is very cautious and protected by a code lock.
- Sailor Dwayne at (3, 8): Facing South behind trash can at (3, 9).

## Mechanics & Locks
- Door Locks: Lt. Surge is behind a locked barrier. You must break a code / find switches to open the doors.
- Trash Can Grid: 5x3 matrix of 15 cans at cols 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 on rows 7, 9, 11. Cross-aisles at rows 8, 10, 12 and vertical aisles at cols 0, 2, 4, 6, 8.
