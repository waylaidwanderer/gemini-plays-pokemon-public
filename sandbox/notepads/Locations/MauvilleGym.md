# Location: Mauville Gym

## Overview
The third official Pokémon League Gym in Hoenn, led by Gym Leader Wattson. Specializes in Electric-type Pokémon. Defeating Wattson awards the Dynamo Badge and TM34 Shock Wave.

## Gym Mechanics & Puzzle
- Puzzle Type: Electric barrier gates controlled by lightning-bolt step switches on the floor.
- Two barrier states: BLUE barriers and MAGENTA/RED barriers.
- Stepping on a floor switch toggles between the two barrier states:
  - Default state: BLUE barriers ACTIVE (horizontal at 4, 14; horizontal at 1, 10). MAGENTA barriers OFF.
  - Toggled state (Switch 1 at 0, 15 pressed): BLUE barriers OFF. MAGENTA barriers ACTIVE (vertical along col 3 at rows 11-13; horizontal at row 10, cols 3-5).
  - Toggled corridor: Column 2 (x=2) is completely open from y=15 straight north past row 10 into the northern gym section!

## Gym Leader: Wattson
- Specialty: Electric-type
- Team (Emerald):
  - Voltorb ♂ Lv 20 (Electric) - Moves: Spark, Rollout, SonicBoom, Selfdestruct
  - Electrike ♂ Lv 20 (Electric) - Moves: Shock Wave, Thunder Wave, Quick Attack, Howl
  - Magneton Lv 22 (Electric/Steel) - Moves: Shock Wave, SonicBoom, Supersonic, Thunder Wave
  - Manectric ♂ Lv 24 (Electric) - Moves: Shock Wave, Quick Attack, Howl, Thunder Wave
- Rewards: Dynamo Badge (enables HM06 Rock Smash outside battle, increases Pokémon Speed stat) + TM34 Shock Wave!

## Battle Strategy
- Sage (Grovyle ♂ Lv 30, Lonely): Leaf Blade (70 BP STAB, high crit) sweeps Voltorb, Electrike, and Manectric. Grass-type naturally resists Electric attacks!
- Cobalu (Aron ♂ Lv 12, Impish): Has Mud-Slap (Ground-type super-effective damage). Resists Normal and Steel attacks.
- Caution: Beware of Voltorb's SonicBoom (fixed 20 damage) and Selfdestruct; Magneton's SonicBoom and Supersonic confusion.

## Gym Puzzle Complete Solution
1. Floor Switch 1 at (0, 15): Toggles default Blue barriers OFF, turns Magenta barriers ON. Opens column 2 (x=2) as a clear passage straight north past row 10 into the northern section.
2. Floor Switch 3 at (4, 12): Toggles Magenta barriers OFF, turns Blue barriers ON. Lowers row 10 magenta barriers to access Ben (5, 10).
3. Toggling back to Magenta (Switch 3 at 4, 12):
   - Blue barrier across Wattson's corridor (cols 4-5, rows 5-7) DEACTIVATES!
   - Blue barrier along col 6 DEACTIVATES!
   - Direct path south to Gym entrance is wide open along col 4!
   - Path to Wattson is wide open along col 2 -> col 4 north corridor!
