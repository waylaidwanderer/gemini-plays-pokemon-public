## Physical Topology & Collisions (1F)
- Entrance Landing: (24..25, 14..17).
- East Sector: (21..25, 6..14).
- Ledge / Pass at (21, 11): Fully walkable bidirectional floor tile connecting East Sector (21, 12) directly north to Northern Corridor (21, 10..7) and Ladder B at (23, 7).
- Ledge Water Access at (25, 9): South-facing ledge hopped from floor (25, 8) onto Eastern Waterway (25, 10).
- Solid Rock Barriers:
  - (19..20, 13..14) blocks direct West passage from (20, 14) into (19, 14).
  - (17..20, 6) blocks direct West passage across Row 6 from (21, 6) into (18, 6).
- Waterways:
  - Eastern Waterway: (24..29, 10..11) and (28..29, 6..9) navigable open water canal.
  - Northern Waterway: (6..29, 4..5) continuous east-west canal.
  - Ladder C Waterway Access: Water canal branches south at cols 14..15 (rows 5..7); disembark East at (16, 7) onto dry floor (16..18, 7..9) directly leading to Ladder C at (18, 9)!
