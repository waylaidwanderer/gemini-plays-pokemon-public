# Cerulean Cave (Unknown Dungeon) 1F - Layout & Topology

## Overview
- Ground floor of Cerulean Cave (Unknown Dungeon), unlocked post-game after entering the Hall of Fame.
- Multi-tier cavern with elevated terraces, rock barriers, and multiple ladder connections to 2F.

## Verified Landmarks & Collision Boundaries (1F)
- **Entrance / Exit Threshold**: Located at (24, 17) / (25, 17). Stepping South onto row 17 threshold warps outside to Cerulean City canal at (4, 11).
- **South Corridor (Rows 14-15)**: Open east-west corridor spanning from column 17 to column 25.
  - (21, 14) - (25, 14): Flat walkable cave ground.
  - (21, 15) - (25, 15): Flat walkable cave ground.
- **Verified Entrances, Ladders & Docks (1F)**:
  - **Cave Entrance / Exit**: Located at (24, 17) / (25, 17) [Exits south into Cerulean City northwest waterway pool].
  - **Dock Ramp at (25, 9)**: Functional dock ramp with bumper facing south into deep subterranean water at (25, 10).
  - **Central Island Dock**: Located at (11, 13) with dock ramp facing south into water at (11, 14).
  - **North Dock**: Located at (15, 3) <-> Waterway at (15, 4).
  - **Northeast Dock Ramp**: Located at (23, 3) <-> Waterway at (23, 4).
  - **Ladder B**: Located at (23, 7) <-> 2F (22, 6). Central elevated sector.
  - **Ladder C**: Located at (18, 9) <-> 2F (19, 7). Central elevated sector.
  - **Ladder at (17, 15)**: Located at the west end of the south corridor.
  - **Ladder D**: Located at (27, 1) <-> 2F (29, 1). Northeast corridor.
  - **Ladder E**: Located at (7, 1) <-> 2F (9, 1). Northwest sector mainland.
  - **SW Ladder**: Located at (3, 11) <-> 2F (3, 11). Southwest sector leading to Ladder A.
- **Verified Water Traversal & Canals**:
  - Continuous northern water canal across rows 4-6, columns 8-28.
  - Verified water path: (25, 10) -> (28, 6) -> (24, 4) -> (16, 4) -> (12, 6) -> (8, 6..15).
  - Deep water channel extends north along column 8 from row 15 to row 6, connecting west across row 6 towards Southwest Sector (cols 0-3).
- **Physical Barriers & Water**:
  - (24, 13) and (25, 13) are solid rock boulders (not water dock ramps).
  - Rows 16-17 (cols 20-23) are elevated cliff/rock wall bases facing south.
  - Columns 24-26 (rows 10-11) are deep subterranean water channels.
