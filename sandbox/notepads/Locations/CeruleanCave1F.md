# Cerulean Cave (Unknown Dungeon) 1F - Layout & Routing

## Entrance & Key Landmarks
- South Entrance/Exit Warp: (24, 17) [Connects to Cerulean City NW water pool at (4, 11)].
- Water Canal: Spans columns 25-27, rows 13-17 leading into central waterways.
- Central Platform: Spans rows 7-13 across columns 16-24.

## Ladder Connectivity Matrix (1F <-> 2F & B1F)
- Ladder B: (23, 7) <-> 2F (22, 6) [Central-East landing].
- Ladder C: (18, 9) <-> 2F (19, 7) [Central transit hub].
- Ladder E: (7, 1) <-> 2F (9, 1) [NW corridor].
- Ladder D: (27, 1) <-> 2F (29, 1) [Isolated NE landing].
- Ladder A2: 1F Western Ridge landing <-> 2F (3, 11) [SW sector target down ladder].
- Ladder to B1F: Located on 1F Western Ridge adjacent to Ladder A2 landing; leads directly down to Mewtwo's lair on B1F.

## Route to Mewtwo (Fastest Verified Path)
1. From 1F Entrance (24, 17), walk North to (23, 7) and ascend Ladder B (or Ladder C at (18, 9)) to 2F.
2. On 2F, traverse Central Hub (19, 7) via North S-Bypass (18, 1..3) -> (22, 2) -> Row 1 West -> Row 3 West (3..9, 3) -> South along Column 3 to Ladder A2 at (3, 11).
3. Descend Ladder A2 at (3, 11) to 1F Western Ridge.
4. On 1F Western Ridge, take the ladder down to B1F.
5. On B1F, surf through the waterway to Mewtwo's platform and capture with Master Ball!
