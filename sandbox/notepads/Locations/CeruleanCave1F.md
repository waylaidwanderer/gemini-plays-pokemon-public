# Cerulean Cave (Unknown Dungeon) 1F - Layout & Topology

## Entrances & Warps
- **South Main Entrance**: (24, 17) <-> Cerulean City NW Waterway landing at (4, 11) on foot. Entered by landing on the west pavement at (5, 12)/(4, 12) and walking up into (4, 11). Warps player onto the 1F Main Floor thoroughfare at (24, 17).
- **NW Water Pool Warp**: (27, 6) <-> Cerulean City NW water pool when surfing directly through the water. Leads to the isolated NE landing.

## Connected Component Graph (1F)
- **South Floor Thoroughfare**: Spans cols 1-26, rows 16-17. Connects south entrance (24, 17) west to South Ramp (1, 13) and north corridors.
- **Ladder C**: Located at (18, 9) <-> 2F Central Hub (19, 7). Reachable on foot from the South Floor via central corridors.
- **Ladder B**: Located at (23, 7) <-> 2F East landing (22, 6).
- **Ladder E**: Located at (7, 1) <-> 2F West sector (9, 1). Reachable via Northern Waterway.
- **Ladder D**: Located at (29, 1) <-> 2F NE pocket (29, 1). Isolated landing.

## 2F to B1F Progression Route
- 2F West Sector (reachable from Ladder E at (7, 1) -> 2F (9, 1)) connects directly via Row 1 / Row 3 to Ladder A at (1, 3).
- Ladder A at (1, 3) descends directly to B1F, providing unobstructed access to Mewtwo!
