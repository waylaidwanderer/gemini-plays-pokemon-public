# Cerulean Cave (Unknown Dungeon) 1F - Layout & Notes

## Connected Component Graph (1F)
- **1F Layout Structure**:
  1. **Entrance Basin (South-East, rows 14..17, cols 23..29)**: Connected to Cerulean City NW Pool entrance at (4, 11). Contains ascending ramp at (24, 14) / (25, 14) leading up to the Upper Plateau.
  2. **Upper Plateau (East/Central, cols 21..28, rows 3..14)**: Elevated ridge containing Ladder B at (23, 7) <-> 2F (22, 6). Solid rock wall at (20, 6..13) divides Upper Plateau from Central Corridor.
  3. **NE Terrace (cols 23..29, rows 0..2)**: Contains Ladder D at (27, 1) <-> 2F (29, 1). Isolated terrace bounded by ledges/walls with no ground exits.
  4. **Central Corridor & Waterways (cols 8..19, rows 4..17)**: Contains Ladder C at (18, 9) <-> 2F (19, 7) and water ramp at (17, 15). Waterway connects north across rows 1..15 to NW Landing.
  5. **NW Landing (cols 5..8, rows 0..3)**: Reachable via Surfing north along 1F waterway. Contains Ladder E at (7, 1) <-> 2F West Sector (9, 1).

## Verified Ladders & Transitions (1F)
- **Ladder B**: Located at (23, 7) on Upper Plateau. Connects to 2F Southeast Sector at (22, 6).
- **Ladder C**: Located at (18, 9) in Central Corridor. Connects to 2F Central Sector at (19, 7).
- **Ladder D**: Located at (27, 1) on NE Terrace. Connects to 2F NE isolated pocket at (29, 1).
- **Ladder E**: Located at (7, 1) on NW Waterway Landing. Connects to 2F West Sector at (9, 1) (which leads to Ladder A at (1, 3) -> B1F Mewtwo).

## Key Water Access Points
- Water ramp located at (17, 15) connects the Central Corridor to the open water canal.
- Surfing north from (17, 15) through the water channels leads directly to Ladder E at (7, 1).

## Wild Encounters (1F)
- Golbat, Parasect, Hypno, Dodrio, Sandslash, Chansey, Kadabra, Raichu, Ditto.
