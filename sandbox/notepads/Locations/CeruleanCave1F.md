# Cerulean Cave (Unknown Dungeon) 1F - Layout & Routing

## Entrance & Key Landmarks
- South Entrance/Exit Warp: (24, 17) [Connects to Cerulean City NW water pool at (4, 11)].
- Water Canal: Spans columns 25-27, rows 13-17 leading into central waterways.
- Central Platform: Spans rows 7-13 across columns 16-24.

## Ladder Connectivity Matrix (1F <-> 2F & B1F)
- Ladder B: (23, 7) <-> 2F (22, 6) [Central-East landing; 2F landing is in an isolated eastern sector separated by Column 20 rock wall from the Central Hub].
- Ladder C: (18, 9) <-> 2F (19, 7) [Central transit hub; connects directly to 2F Central Hub and Northern Bypass].
- Ladder E: (7, 1) <-> 2F (9, 1) [NW corridor].
- Ladder D: (27, 1) <-> 2F (29, 1) [Isolated NE landing].
- Ladder A2: 1F Western Ridge landing <-> 2F (3, 11) [SW sector target down ladder].
- Ladder to B1F: Located on 1F Western Ridge adjacent to Ladder A2 landing; leads directly down to Mewtwo's lair on B1F.

## Route to Mewtwo (1F Waterway Canal & Western Ridge)
1. From 1F Entrance (24, 17), walk to water shoreline at (25, 17) / (25, 16) and activate Surf on HYDROS.
2. Surf North along the central water canal (cols 25-28) up to northern channel (rows 1-3).
3. Surf West along northern channel (rows 1-3) to Northwest Canal (cols 1-9).
4. Land on 1F Western Ridge platform.
5. Descend the ladder on 1F Western Ridge directly down to B1F.
6. On B1F, surf through the waterway to Mewtwo's platform and capture with Master Ball!
