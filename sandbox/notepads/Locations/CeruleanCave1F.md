# Cerulean Cave (Unknown Dungeon) 1F - Layout & Topology

## Overview
- Ground floor of Cerulean Cave (Unknown Dungeon).
- Connects to outside via entrance at (24, 17).
- Contains water channels, one-way ledges, and multiple ladders connecting to 2F.

## Verified Topology & Landmarks (1F)
1. **Entrance Area**: (24..25, 17) entrance mat. Passage extends north to (24..25, 14).
   - Tile (24, 14) connects west into Column 22/20.
2. **Central South Corridor**:
   - Column 20 runs north-south from (20, 14) north through (20, 10) towards northern chambers.
   - Rows 16-17 run west across columns 16-22 towards the Southwest Sector.
   - Column 19 (rows 10-15) is a vertical rock wall barrier.
3. **Ledges**:
   - (21, 11): One-way ledge facing south/down.
   - (17, 15): One-way ledge facing south/down.
4. **Verified Ladders on 1F**:
   - SW Ladder: (3, 11) -> leads to 2F SW Sector (crucial path to Ladder A at 2F (1, 3) -> B1F Mewtwo).
   - Ladder B: (23, 7) <-> 2F (22, 6)
   - Ladder C: (18, 9) <-> 2F (19, 7)
   - Ladder D: (27, 1) <-> 2F (29, 1)
   - Ladder E: (7, 1) <-> 2F (9, 1)

## Strategic Objective
- Follow passages towards the southwest to reach SW Ladder at (3, 11).
- Ascend to 2F SW Sector, take western path to Ladder A at (1, 3), descend to B1F for Mewtwo.
