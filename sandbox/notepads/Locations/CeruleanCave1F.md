# Cerulean Cave (Unknown Dungeon) 1F - Layout & Topology

## Overview
- Ground floor entrance and expansive water/terrace network of Cerulean Cave.
- Entrance / Exit warp to Cerulean City / Route 24 waterway basin at (25, 17).

## Verified Ladders (1F)
1. **Ladder B**: Located at (23, 7) <-> 2F Southeast Sector (22, 6).
2. **Ladder C**: Located at (18, 9) <-> 2F Central Sector (19, 7).
3. **Ladder D**: Located at (27, 1) <-> 2F Eastern Corridor (29, 1).
4. **Ladder E**: Located at (7, 1) <-> 2F Northern Ridge (9, 1).
5. **Unmapped Ladder(s)**: Target of systematic exploration across western/southwestern 1F ground and waterways to access 2F West Maze (Ladder A to B1F Mewtwo).

## Verified Waterways, Ramps & Land Sectors (1F)
- **North Pocket**: Open cave floor across cols 7..16 (rows 0..3) containing Ladder E at (7, 1) and North Pocket Ramp at (15, 3).
- **North Canal**: Continuous water channel spanning rows 4..5 across cols 15..23 connecting North Pocket Ramp (15, 3) to North Terrace Ramp (23, 3).
- **North-East Terrace**: Elevated land sector across cols 23..28 (rows 1..2) containing Ladder D at (27, 1) and North Terrace Ramp at (23, 3).
- **Central Water Basin**: Waterway spanning rows 6..10 (cols 18..24) connecting to Ladder B terrace (23, 7) and Ladder C island (18, 9).
- **South Entrance Basin**: Waterway across rows 11..17 (cols 24..27) connecting southern entrance at (25, 17) to Central Basin.

## Systematic 1F Exploration Protocol
- Methodically explore all water branches heading South, West, and Central from the North Canal (Row 4..5) and Central Basin (Row 6..10).
- Map all ground-level terraces, ramps, and search for unmapped ascending ladders across western 1F.
