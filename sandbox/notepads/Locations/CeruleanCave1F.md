# Cerulean Cave (Unknown Dungeon) 1F - Layout & Topology

## Overview
- Ground floor of Cerulean Cave (Unknown Dungeon), unlocked post-game after entering the Hall of Fame.
- Features extensive subterranean water channels, elevated rock terraces, and five ladder connections to 2F.

## Verified Entrances, Ladders & Docks (1F)
1. **Cave Entrance / Exit**: Located at (25, 17) [Exits south into Cerulean City northwest waterway pool].
2. **SW Ladder (Path to Mewtwo)**: Located at (3, 11) <-> 2F (3, 11). Accessible via dock at (8, 11) -> walk west along row 11 to (3, 11). Ascends to 2F Southwest Sector & Western Corridor leading to B1F Ladder A (1, 3).
3. **Central Island Dock**: Located at (11, 13).
4. **North Dock**: Located at (15, 3) <-> Waterway at (15, 4).
5. **Northeast Dock Ramp**: Located at (23, 3) <-> Waterway at (23, 4).
6. **Ladder E**: Located at (7, 1) <-> 2F (9, 1). Northwest sector mainland highway.
7. **Ladder D**: Located at (27, 1) <-> 2F (29, 1). Northeast corridor.
8. **Ladder B**: Located at (23, 7) <-> 2F (22, 6). Central elevated sector.
9. **Ladder C**: Located at (18, 9) <-> 2F (19, 7). Central elevated sector.

## Verified Routing Protocols
- **Optimal Route to Mewtwo (B1F)**:
  1. Enter 1F from Cerulean City canal at (25, 17).
  2. Surf immediately west across the southern water basin to dock at (8, 11).
  3. Dismount at (8, 11) and walk west to SW Ladder at (3, 11).
  4. Ascend SW Ladder (3, 11) to 2F (3, 11).
  5. On 2F, follow Column 0 north through the Western Corridor directly to Ladder A at (1, 3).
  6. Descend Ladder A (1, 3) to Cerulean Cave B1F and navigate to Mewtwo.
