# Cerulean Cave (Unknown Dungeon) 1F - Layout & Topology

## Overview
- Main ground floor and waterway network of Cerulean Cave.
- Contains the main entrance/exit leading out to Cerulean City NW basin.
- Connects to 2F via multiple ladders: Ladder B (23, 7), Ladder C (18, 9), Ladder D (27, 1), Ladder E (7, 1), and Southwest Ladder (3, 11).
