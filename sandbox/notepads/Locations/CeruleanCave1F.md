## Verified Waterways, Ramps & Land Sectors (1F)
- **North Pocket**: Open cave floor across cols 7..16 (rows 0..3) containing Ladder E at (7, 1) and North Pocket Ramp at (15, 3).
- **North Canal**: Continuous water channel spanning rows 4..5 across cols 15..28 connecting North Pocket Ramp (15, 3) to North Terrace Ramp (23, 3).
- **North-East Terrace**: Elevated land sector across cols 23..28 (rows 1..2) containing Ladder D at (27, 1) and North Terrace Ramp at (23, 3).
- **Central-East Terrace**: Land sector across cols 21..25 (rows 6..10) containing Ladder B at (23, 7) and Ramp at (25, 9) to the East Basin.
- **Central Terrace**: Land sector across cols 11..18 (rows 8..14) containing Ladder C at (18, 9) and South-Central Ramp at (11, 13).
- **Western Water Canal**: Continuous water channel running south along Columns 8..9 across rows 6..15.
- **East Water Basin**: Water basin spanning cols 24..29 across rows 10..11, connecting via East Channel (cols 28..29, rows 5..9) north to the North Canal.
