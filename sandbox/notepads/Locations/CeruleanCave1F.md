# Cerulean Cave (Unknown Dungeon) 1F - Layout & Topology

## Overview
- Ground floor of Cerulean Cave (Unknown Dungeon).
- Connects the cave entrance, extensive waterway canal system, mainland, and multiple ladders to 2F.

## Verified Landmarks & Ladders (1F)
1. **Cave Entrance**: Located at (4, 11) connecting from Cerulean City / Route 24 river basin.
2. **Ladder E**: Located at (7, 1) <-> 2F (9, 1) on Northern Mainland.
3. **North Dock**: Located at (15, 3) connecting Northern Mainland to Central Waterway at (15, 4).
4. **Ladder D**: Located at (27, 1) <-> 2F (29, 1) in isolated Northeast Alcove.
5. **Ladder B**: Located at (23, 7) <-> 2F (22, 6) on Central Mainland.
6. **Ladder C**: Located at (18, 9) <-> 2F (19, 7) on Central Mainland.
7. **South Dock**: Located at (11, 13) connecting Central Mainland to South Waterway at (11, 14).
8. **SW Ladder**: Located at (3, 11) <-> 2F (3, 11) in Southwest Sector. Ascends to 2F isolated western corridor leading directly to Ladder A (1, 3) and B1F Mewtwo!

## Verified Traversal Route to Mewtwo (B1F)
1. From 1F (7, 1) (Ladder E), walk east across Northern Mainland to North Dock at (15, 3).
2. Activate Surf facing South into Central Waterway at (15, 4).
3. Surf south along Column 15 to (15, 7), west along Row 7 to Column 8, and south down Column 8 canal to Row 14.
4. Surf west to Southwest Sector SW Ladder at (3, 11) or disembark at South Dock (11, 13).
5. Climb SW Ladder (3, 11) to 2F (3, 11).
6. Walk north along 2F isolated western corridor (Column 0 / 1) to Ladder A at (1, 3).
7. Descend Ladder A (1, 3) to B1F Mewtwo chamber and capture with Master Ball!
