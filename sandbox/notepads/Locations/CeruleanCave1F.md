# Cerulean Cave (Unknown Dungeon) 1F - Layout & Topology

## Overview
- Ground floor of Cerulean Cave (Unknown Dungeon).
- Connects to Route 24 / Cerulean City NW waterway at (25, 17) entrance mat.
- Waterways require Surf to traverse.

## Verified Water Ramps & Navigation (1F)
- **Northeast Water Ramp**: Located at (23, 3). Connects the northeast elevated ridge (Ladder D at (27, 1)) south directly into the 1F water network at (23, 4).
- **Central Water Ramp**: Located at (11, 13). Connects the central terrace (Ladder C at (18, 9)) directly south into the 1F water network at (11, 14).
- **North Water Ramp**: Located at (15, 3). Connects the northern water channel at (15, 4) north directly onto the northern terrace (Ladder E at (7, 1)) at (15, 2).
- **Waterway Network**: Continuous waterways spanning across rows 4-7 (cols 8-29) and rows 14-15 (cols 8-11+).
