# Cerulean Cave (Unknown Dungeon) 1F - Layout & Topology

## Overview
- Ground floor of Cerulean Cave containing an extensive waterway system, elevated terraces, and 4 ladder connections to 2F.
- Exterior entrance at (24, 17) / (25, 17) connects to Cerulean City / Route 24 canal.

## Verified Ladders (1F)
- **Ladder B**: Located at (22, 6) / (23, 7) on Central Terrace <-> 2F (22, 6).
- **Ladder C**: Located at (18, 9) on Central-West Island <-> 2F (19, 7).
- **Ladder D**: Located at (27, 1) on Northeast Terrace <-> 2F (29, 1).
- **Ladder E**: Located at (7, 1) on Northwest Pocket <-> 2F (9, 1).

## Verified Ramp & Shoreline Matrix (1F)
- **East Ramp (25, 9)**: Connects Central Terrace ground at (25, 8) south into East Basin water at (25, 10).
- **South-Central Ramp (21, 11)**: Connects West Canal/South water at (20, 11) onto South-Central Terrace at (21, 10..12). (Separated from East Basin by rock walls at (22..23, 11)).
- **North Ramp (23, 3)**: Connects North Canal water at (23, 4) north onto North Pocket ground at (23, 2).
- **Northwest Ramp (15, 3)**: Connects North Canal water at (15, 4) north onto Northwest Pocket ground at (15, 2).
- **Central-West Ramp (11, 13)**: Connects South Water Channel at (11, 14) north onto Central-West Island at (11, 12).

## Verified Topographical Features & Constraints (1F)
- **Central Terrace North Cliff**: Row 6 across cols 21..25 is an elevated cliff ledge overlooking Row 5 water. Direct Surfing north off Row 6 is blocked ("No SURFing on HYDROS here!").
- **East Basin**: Water basin spanning cols 24..29 (rows 10..17), bounded east by Column 30 rock wall.
- **North Canal**: Horizontal blue water channel across rows 4..5 (cols 15..28).
- **West Canal**: Vertical blue water channel along cols 8..9 (rows 6..14).
- **South Water Channel**: Open water channel across rows 14..15 (cols 8..11+).
- **Northwest Pocket (cols 5..16, rows 0..2)**: Connected via Northwest Ramp at (15, 3). Terminated on the west by solid rock wall along Column 4 (4, 0..3). Contains Ladder E at (7, 1).
- **Central-West Island Interior (cols 11..18, rows 8..13)**: Connected via Central-West Ramp at (11, 13). Contains Ladder C at (18, 9). North corridor across Row 8 (11..18, 8) connects Ramp to Ladder C.
