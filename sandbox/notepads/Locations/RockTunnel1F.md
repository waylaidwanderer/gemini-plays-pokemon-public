   - Ladder to B1F located at (23, 11).

   - Western highway spans cols 2-5 (rows 2-21) leading down to southern dead-end wall at row 22.
   - Southern thoroughfare spans cols 2-21 (rows 18-21).

2. Central Rock Divider (cols 24-25, rows 0-15+):
   - Solid unbroken vertical barrier completely separating Western 1F (cols 0-23) from Eastern 1F (cols 26-37).
   - Crossing between Western 1F and Eastern 1F requires traversing B1F.
3. Eastern 1F (cols 26-37):
   - Contains the ladder to B1F and the continuous southern corridor leading to Route 10 South and Lavender Town.
