# Location: Mirage Tower

## Overview
A mystical tower that occasionally manifests in the Route 111 desert. Highly unstable structure made of fragile sand. Contains the ancient fossils: Claw Fossil and Root Fossil on 4F.
- Prerequisites: Go-Goggles (to access desert), Mach Bike (to cross crumbling tiles on 2F), HM06 Rock Smash (to break rocks on 3F).
- Mechanics: Once one of the two fossils is chosen on 4F, the tower collapses and sinks into the desert sand forever (until the other fossil reappears in Desert Underpass post-game).

## Connection Points
- 1F Exit: Route 111 Desert at (10, 15).

## Floor 1 (1F)
- Entrance / Exit mat: (10, 14..15).
- Spawn: (10, 14) facing North on Mach Bike.
- Small rocks at (9, 13) and (11, 13) flanking the entrance path.
- Central rock formation spans cols 6..14, rows 4..12.
- West corridor: cols 4..5, rows 6..13.
- North corridor: row 3, cols 4..15+.
- 2F Ladder: Located at (15, 2) against the north wall.
- Verified 1F Path to Ladder: From entrance (10, 14), Up to (10, 13), Left to (4, 13), Up along col 4 to (4, 3), Right along row 3 to (15, 3), Up to (15, 2) into the ladder.
- Wild Pokémon Encountered: Sandshrew (Lv 20), Trapinch (Lv 20).
## Floor 2 (2F)
- Arrival: 1F Ladder hole at (15, 2) facing West on Mach Bike.
- North Corridor: Solid sand path along row 2 spanning cols 3..15.
- Central Rock Formation: Starts at col 5, row 4.
- Western Corridor: Columns 2..3, running south from row 3.
- Cracked Tiles: Located in the western corridor at cols 2..3, rows 4..6+.
- Wild Pokémon Encountered: Trapinch (Lv 20).
- Puzzle Mechanic: Requires Mach Bike at top speed to cross cracked crumbling tiles without falling to 1F.
- Northeast Alcove: (cols 16..18, rows 3..8) is a dead end! Blocked south at row 9 by a wall of rocks spanning cols 16..18.
- 3F Ladder: Visible at southeast corner at (17..18, 12..13). Accessible ONLY from the south corridor coming from the southwest!
- Progression Route: Must cross the western cracked tile corridor using the Mach Bike at top speed to reach the south corridor and the 3F ladder.
- Puzzle Analysis: Columns 2..3 have 7 consecutive cracked tiles (rows 4..10) along the west wall. Row 3 is blocked by wall at col 3; col 4 has stationary pebble at (4, 8) and (3, 9); col 16 alcove is blocked at row 9 by rocks. 3F ladder at (18, 13) is accessible only via the south corridor past the cracked tiles.
- Strategic Decision: Mirage Tower is an optional fossil dungeon. To avoid getting bogged down by high-speed Mach Bike turning execution, deferring fossil retrieval and proceeding directly to Petalburg Gym for Gym 5 (Norman / Balance Badge / Surf).
