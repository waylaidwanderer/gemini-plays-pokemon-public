# Location: Cave of Origin

## Overview
A sacred, ancient subterranean cavern situated beneath northern Sootopolis City, said to be where life in Hoenn originated and where Legendary Pokémon sleep.
- Story Event: Wallace (former Gym Leader and ancient lorekeeper) is waiting deep within the Cave of Origin to discuss how to quell the catastrophic battle between Groudon and Kyogre.
- Key Objective: Locate Wallace and tell him about Rayquaza at the Sky Pillar so he can unlock the Sky Pillar entrance.

## Connection Points
- 1F South Entrance / Exit: Doorway at (9, 21) exiting out to northern Sootopolis City at (31, 17). Spawns inside at (9, 20) facing North.

## Discovered Topography & Navigation
### 1F (Entrance Hall)
- Entrance / Exit: (9, 20) facing North from Sootopolis City (31, 17). Exit doorway is at (9, 21).
- Central Northbound Canyon (rows 5..20, cols 8..10): A 3-tile wide dirt corridor leading straight North to the cave doorway at (9, 4..5) leading down to B1F.

### B1F (First Descent)
- Entrance Area: Spawns at (11, 17) facing North. Light patch at (11, 18) leads back up to 1F.
- Lower Thoroughfare (row 15, cols 6..16): East-west hallway connecting the entrance to the western ramp.
- Central Ridge (rows 13..14, cols 8..16): Impassable south-facing rock ridge separating the lower thoroughfare from the upper plateau. The eastern end at col 15 is a dead end.
- Western Ramp (cols 5..7, rows 12..15): Open, unobstructed dirt ramp connecting the lower thoroughfare north onto the upper plateau.
- Upper Plateau (rows 6..12, cols 5..16): Wide elevated cavern floor spanning across the northern half of B1F.
- Ladder to B2F: Located at (14, 5) entered from (14, 6) stepping North in the northeast corner of B1F.

### B2F (Elemental Crystal Chamber)
- Entrance / Return Ladder: Spawns at (9, 3) facing North. The ladder directly at/above (9, 3) returns up to B1F at (14, 5)! Do not re-enter (9, 3) when navigating B2F.
- Upper Northern Highway (row 3, cols 4..14): Horizontal corridor running along the northern rock wall.
- Western Sector: Col 6 forms a short dead-end pocket terminating at (6, 5) against blue crystals at (6, 6).
- Eastern Sector (cols 10..14, row 3): Open upper highway extending east toward the red glowing crystal corridor (Groudon aura).
- Central Terraces (rows 4..8): Stepped rocky terraces with south-facing ledges.
