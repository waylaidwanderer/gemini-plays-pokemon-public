# Location: Cave of Origin

## Overview
A sacred, ancient subterranean cavern situated beneath northern Sootopolis City, said to be where life in Hoenn originated and where Legendary Pokémon sleep.
- Story Event: Wallace (former Gym Leader and ancient lorekeeper) is waiting deep within the Cave of Origin to discuss how to quell the catastrophic battle between Groudon and Kyogre.
- Key Objective: Locate Wallace and tell him about Rayquaza at the Sky Pillar so he can unlock the Sky Pillar entrance.

## Connection Points
- 1F South Entrance / Exit: Light patch at (9, 21) exiting out to northern Sootopolis City at (31, 17). Spawns inside at (9, 20) facing North.

## Progression Checklist - Cave of Origin
1. [x] Enter Cave of Origin 1F from Sootopolis City with Steven Stone's escort (Turn 17877 at 9, 20).
2. [x] Navigate through 1F and B1F to locate ladder to B2F (Turn 17900 at 14, 6).
3. [ ] Navigate lower floors (B2F+) to locate Wallace.
4. [ ] Discuss the super-ancient Pokémon with Wallace and select "SKY PILLAR" to direct him to awaken Rayquaza.

## Discovered Topography & Navigation - 1F
- Entrance Area: Spawns at (9, 20) on 1F facing North. Exit light patch at (9, 21).
- Central Northbound Canyon (rows 5..20, cols 8..10): A 3-tile wide dirt corridor flanked by rocky ridges on the west (cols 6..7) and east (cols 11..12). Extends straight North to the northern cave doorway at (9, 4..5) leading down to B1F.
## Discovered Topography & Navigation - B1F
- Entrance Area: Spawns at (11, 17) facing North. Light patch at (11, 18) leads back up to 1F.
- Lower Thoroughfare (row 15, cols 6..16): East-west hallway connecting the entrance to the western ramp.
- Central Ridge (rows 13..14, cols 8..16): Impassable south-facing rock ridge separating the lower thoroughfare from the upper plateau. The eastern end at col 15 is a dead end.
- Western Ramp (cols 5..7, rows 12..15): Open, unobstructed dirt ramp connecting the lower thoroughfare north onto the upper plateau.
- Upper Plateau (rows 6..12, cols 5..16): Wide elevated cavern floor spanning across the northern half of B1F.
- Ladder to B2F: Located at (14, 5) entered from (14, 6) stepping North in the northeast corner of B1F.
## Discovered Topography & Navigation - B2F
- Upper Northern Highway (row 3, cols 4..14): Horizontal corridor running along the northern rock wall.
- Return Ladder to B1F: Located directly north of spawn at (9, 2).
- Western Sector: Col 6 forms a short dead-end pocket terminating at (6, 5) against blue crystals at (6, 6).
- Eastern Sector (cols 10..14, row 3): Open upper highway extending east past the return ladder (9, 2) toward the red glowing crystal corridor (Groudon aura).
- Central Terraces (rows 4..8): Stepped rocky terraces with south-facing ledges.
## Discovered Topography & Navigation - B3F
- Entrance Area: Spawns at (14, 5) facing South from B2F ladder/warp.
- Upper Highway (rows 5..6, cols 7..14): Wide open dirt corridor leading west across the upper terrace.
- Western Slope & Canyon (cols 5..7, rows 6..12): Unobstructed dirt corridor bypassing the central rock formation on the west, connecting the upper highway down to the southern floor.
- Southern Terraces: South-facing jump ledges descending from the upper terrace into the southern floor.
- Southern Floor (rows 12..14+): Cavern floor wrapping east beneath the central rock plateau toward the ladder down to B4F.
- Ladder to B4F: Located at (11, 16..17) along the southern corridor beneath the central rock plateau.
## Discovered Topography & Navigation - B4F (Lowest Basin)
- Entrance Area: Spawns at (9, 6) facing South from B3F doorway at (9, 5).
- Central Southbound Canyon (cols 8..10, rows 6..12+): A 3-tile wide open dirt corridor flanked by vertical basalt rock ridges, leading south into the lowest chamber where Wallace is located.
## Wild Encounters - Cave of Origin
