# Location: Pacifidlog Town

## Overview
A peaceful settlement floating on wooden rafts atop a coral reef in the ocean of southern Hoenn.
- Connection Points:
  - East: Route 131 (leading to Sky Pillar).
  - West: Route 132 (ocean currents flowing west toward Slateport City).
- Key Landmarks:
  - Pokémon Center: Located on a central floating raft platform.
  - Mirage Island Watcher: Old man in southeast house looking for Mirage Island.
