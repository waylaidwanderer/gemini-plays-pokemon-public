# Vermilion City - Overworld Layout & Mapping

## Map Coordinates & Dimensions
- **Dimensions:** Width = 40 tiles (columns 0 to 39), Height = 30 tiles (rows 0 to 29).
- **North Connection (Route 6):** Enters Vermilion City on columns 18-20, row 0.
- **East Connection (Route 11):** Located on columns 30-39, rows 11-15, leading east to Route 11.

## Key Landmarks & Buildings
- **Poké Mart:** Located at columns 24-25, rows 12-13. Entrance door is at `(25, 13)` facing south onto the pavement.
- **Old Man's Construction Plot:** Located at columns 28-31, rows 6-9. A fenced plot of land where an old man and his Machop are tamping the ground. The old man is at `(30, 7)`.
- **Diglett's Cave Entrance Building:** Located on Route 11 at columns 34-35, rows 12-13 (using Vermilion coordinate space). It is a brown 2x2 cave-like building on the overworld. The doorway at `(34, 13)` is blocked by a cuttable bush, which was successfully cut and cleared on Turn 45329 (note: it regrows immediately if the player walks far away or reloads the map).
- **Route 12 Snorlax Signpost:** Located at `(37, 13)`. It reads: "NOTICE! ROUTE 12 may be blocked off by a sleeping POKéMON. Detour through ROCK TUNNEL to LAVENDER TOWN."
- **Sleeping Snorlax Obstacle:** The 2x2 overworld graphic at `(34-35, 12-13)` (Route 12 intersection) is the sleeping Snorlax blocking eastern traversal to Route 12!

## Verified Traversal & Paths
- Row 14 is a horizontal street, walkable from Column 19 to Column 39, enabling direct traversal from Saffron's South exit to the Route 11 east exit and Diglett's Cave area.
