# Mt. Gerhana

## Geography & Connections
- **Southern Entrance (`6, 30` interior <- `27, 14` Azluf Town)**: Connects to Azluf Town at `(27, 14)` (unlocked after winning Round One of the Eclipse Tournament, verified Turn 28074-28076). Stepping `Down` from `(6, 30)` exits back to Azluf Town.
- **Northern Ladder (`9, 19` interior <-> `13, 9` exterior clearing)**: Stepping onto the base of the northern ladder at `(9, 19)` (above the wooden stairs at `8-10, 21-22`) warps out to an exterior mountain clearing of **Mt. Gerhana** at `(13, 10)` in front of the cave mouth at `(13, 9)` (verified Turns 28090-28092).
  - **Exterior Mountain Pass (`13, 10` -> `6, 14` -> `5, 9` -> `2, 6`)**: Descending the mountain steps (`13, 11-13`) and following the canyon pass west along `row 14` (`cols 6-13`) and north along `cols 4-6` (`rows 7-14`) leads to a second cave entrance at `(2, 6)` with a wooden signboard at `(4, 7)` (interacted from `4, 8`) reading verbatim: *"Route 2 / A hidden path"* (verified Turns 28092-28095). Stepping into `(2, 6)` exits to the closed-off northern enclave of **Route 2** at `(38, 9)` directly behind the Pokémon Day-Care yard (verified Turn 28098).
- **Eastern Underpass Bridge (`cols 16-24, rows 23-25`) & Upper North-South Bridge (`cols 19-21, rows 20-27`)**:
  - From the main cavern floor east of Roark (`14-15, 23-25`), a horizontal wooden bridge at `cols 16-24, rows 23-25` passes directly underneath the elevated north-south wooden bridge (`cols 19-21`) to reach the eastern cavern floor (`cols 25-32, rows 23-29`, verified Turn 28106).
  - **Northeastern Stairs (`28-30, 21-22`) & Floor Ladder (`29, 18` -> B1F `37, 15`)**: Ascending the wooden stairs at `(28-30, 21-22)` reaches an enclosed upper terrace (`cols 27-31, rows 17-20`, with an inert small rock at `31, 20`). From here, an orange item sphere is visible on an elevated northern ledge at `(24, 13)` next to wooden stairs at `(22, 13-14)` (`latest_grid.png` Turn 28115). Stepping north to `(29, 17)` above the floor ladder and pressing `Down` twice onto `(29, 18)` descends to **Mt. Gerhana B1F** at `(37, 15)` (verified Turns 28114-28116).
