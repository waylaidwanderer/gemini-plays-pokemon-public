# Rocket Hideout Infiltration & Access Notes

## Main Hideout Entrance
- Main Game Corner Casino: Located in Central/South Celadon City at x=24..30, y=33..36.
- Secret Poster Switch: On the North Wall of the Main Casino floor, guarded by a Rocket Grunt. Defeating the Grunt and pressing A on the poster reveals the true secret staircase to Rocket Hideout B1F.

## Secondary Loop (Building 17, 27)
- Entrance at Celadon City (17, 27) contains a staircase at (4, 4) that connects to a local entrance corridor loop in B1F between (5, 4) and (2, 41).
### Column 2 Control Highway & Spin Tile Exploration Protocol (Discovered Turn 45194)
- Column 2 (x=2) is a continuous vertical column of yellow/red STOP TILES spanning y=3 down to y=40.
- Because Column 2 consists entirely of stop tiles, the player can freely walk North/South along x=2 without spinning.
- Adjacent to Column 2 at x=3 is a vertical bank of spin tiles (3, y).
- Test Protocol: From any position (2, y) on Column 2, step Right onto (3, y) to test row trajectory.
- Row y=3 (3,3; 4,3; 5,3): Spin tiles spin UP, bumping into counter wall at y=2. Pressing Left 3 times from (5, 3) returns safely to Column 2 at (2, 3).
- Entrance/Exit Staircase: Located at (5, 4) connecting to Celadon Game Corner (4, 4).
