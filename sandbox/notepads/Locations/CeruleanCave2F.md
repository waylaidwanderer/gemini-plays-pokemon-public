# Cerulean Cave (Unknown Dungeon) 2F - Layout & Topology

## Overview
- Upper floor maze of Cerulean Cave (Unknown Dungeon).
- Consists of isolated elevated sectors connected to 1F via multiple ladders.
- Contains descending Ladder A to B1F (Mewtwo) located in the Western Sector at (1, 3).

## Verified Landmarks & Ladders (2F)
1. **Ladder E**: Located at (9, 1) <-> 1F (7, 1). Northwest plateau (rows 0-7, cols 3-16). Connects west along row 1 to (3, 1) and east to (18, 1).
2. **Ladder A (to B1F Mewtwo)**: Located at (1, 3). Descends directly to Cerulean Cave B1F (Mewtwo). Situated in the Western Corridor (cols 0-1). Accessible from the Southwest Sector via SW Ladder (3, 11).
3. **Ladder D**: Located at (29, 1) <-> 1F (27, 1). Northeast corridor.
4. **Ladder B**: Located at (22, 6) <-> 1F (23, 7). Central sector.
5. **Ladder C**: Located at (19, 7) <-> 1F (18, 9). Central sector.
6. **SW Ladder**: Located at (3, 11) <-> 1F (3, 11). Southwest Sector & Western Corridor (cols 0-1).
