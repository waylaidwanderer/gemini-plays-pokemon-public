# Cerulean Cave (Unknown Dungeon) 2F - Layout & Topology

## Overview
- Upper floor maze of Cerulean Cave (Unknown Dungeon).
- Connects to 1F via multiple ladders and contains the descending ladder (Ladder A) to B1F (Mewtwo).

## Verified Ladders & Exact Arrival Spawns (2F)
1. **Ladder A**: Located at (0, 6) / (1, 3) -> Descends to B1F (Mewtwo's Chamber).
2. **Ladder B**: Located at (22, 6) <-> 1F (23, 7).
3. **Ladder C**: Located at (19, 7) <-> 1F (18, 9).
4. **Ladder D**: Located at (29, 1) <-> 1F (27, 1).
5. **Ladder E**: Located at (7, 1) <-> 1F (7, 1).
6. **Southwest Ladder**: (3, 11) <-> 1F (3, 11).

## Verified Fast Route to Mewtwo (B1F)
1. From 1F, climb Ladder C at (18, 9) -> spawns on 2F at (19, 7).
2. On 2F from (19, 7): Walk Up 2 to (19, 5), Right 2 to (21, 5), Up 1 to (21, 4), Right 1 to (22, 4), Up 3 along Column 22 to Row 1 Northern Highway at (22, 1).
3. Run straight West 21 steps across Row 1 Northern Highway to (1, 1).
4. Walk Down 2 steps into Ladder A at (0, 6) / (1, 3).
5. Descend Ladder A directly to Cerulean Cave B1F (Mewtwo's Chamber)!
