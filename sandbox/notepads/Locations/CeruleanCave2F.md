# Cerulean Cave (Unknown Dungeon) 2F - Layout & Topology

## Overview
- Upper floor maze of Cerulean Cave (Unknown Dungeon).
- Connects to 1F via multiple ladders and contains the descending ladder (Ladder A) to B1F (Mewtwo).

## Verified Landmarks & Topology (2F)
1. **Ladder B**: Located at (22, 6) <-> 1F (23, 7).
2. **Ladder C**: Located at (19, 7) <-> 1F (18, 9).
3. **Ladder D**: Located at (29, 1) <-> 1F (27, 1). Located in isolated northeast alcove.
4. **Ladder E**: Located at (9, 1) <-> 1F (7, 1) in isolated northwest sector.
5. **SW Ladder**: Located at (3, 11) <-> 1F (3, 11). Entrance to Southwest Sector.
6. **Ladder A (to B1F Mewtwo)**: Descending ladder to B1F located in western corridor at (1, 3).

## Verified Southwest Sector Routing to Ladder A (1, 3)
- From SW Ladder (3, 11):
  - Walk north to (3, 9), east along Row 9 to (20, 9).
  - Walk north along Col 20 to Row 5 at (20, 5) / (19, 5).
  - Walk west along Row 5 thoroughfare from (19, 5) all the way to (1, 5).
  - Walk north along Column 1 from (1, 5) through (1, 4) to (1, 3) [Ladder A].
  - Descend Ladder A (1, 3) directly into Cerulean Cave B1F (Mewtwo's Chamber).
