# Route 22 - Layout & Notes

## General Information
- East connection: Viridian City (x=39, y=8..9)
- West connection: Pokémon League Front Gatehouse / Badge Check (x=8, y=5)

## Points of Interest & Encounters
- Wild Pokémon: Rattata, Spearow, Nidoran♀, Nidoran♂, Mankey
- Rival RED Encounter: Triggered when walking west past east entrance (defeated Turn 22069).
- Front Gatehouse: Checks all 8 Kanto Badges sequentially.

## Verified Topology & Navigation Routes (Verified Turn 31679)
- Upper Highway (Row 5): Extends west from Route 22 east entrance (39, 5) to (16, 5). Columns 14-15 are solid statues across rows 4-11, blocking direct westward passage along Row 5.
- Western Ledge Descent: From (16, 5), hop south down the Row 7 ledge (16, 7) into western tall grass (16, 8..11) and descend directly to the Row 12 Lawn at (16, 12).
- Western Lawn (Row 12): Columns 2 to 25 along Row 12 form a continuous, unobstructed green lawn running directly beneath the statue wall (rows 4-11).
- Grand Entrance Carpet (Row 10): Spans columns 2 to 13. Accessible from Row 12 via the open fence gap at (2..5, 11).
- Forecourt Ledge Gap: Located at (11, 9). Allows northward passage from Row 10 carpet (11, 10) through the ledge to the upper forecourt green grass at (11, 8) and paved walkway at (11, 7).
- Gatehouse Entrance: Located at (8, 5). From (11, 7), ride west along the paved walkway to (8, 7) and step north into the front door at (8, 5) to enter Route 22 Gate at (4, 7).
- Row 13 Ledge: Continuous south-facing ledge spanning columns 2..37, with a two-way dirt gap at (33, 13).
- Lower Road: Rows 14 and 15 form the lower paved road leading east back to Viridian City at (39, 14).
