# Route 22 - Layout & Notes

## General Information
- East connection: Viridian City (x=39, y=8..9)
- West connection: Pokémon League Front Gatehouse / Badge Check (x=8, y=5)

## Points of Interest & Encounters
- Wild Pokémon: Rattata, Spearow, Nidoran♀, Nidoran♂, Mankey
- Rival RED Encounter: Triggered when walking west past east entrance (defeated Turn 22069).
- Front Gatehouse: Checks all 8 Kanto Badges sequentially.

## Verified Topology & Navigation Routes (Verified Turn 31671)
- Upper Highway (Row 5): Columns 8 to 39 along Row 5 (y=5) form a continuous, open paved highway leading directly from Route 22 east entrance all the way west into the Gatehouse front entrance door at (8, 5)!
- Cliff Corner Chokepoint: Row 6 (y=6) is blocked at (29, 6) by a rocky cliff corner. To travel west across the north side, always ride along Row 5 (y=5).
- Row 7 Ledge Gap: Located at (31, 7). Connects the central tall grass clearing (Row 8) north onto the Row 5 highway via (31, 6) -> (31, 5).
- Row 13 Ledge Gap: Located at (33, 13). Connects the lower paved road (Row 14) north into the central clearing (Row 12).
- Lower Road: Rows 14 and 15 form the lower paved road leading east to Viridian City.
- Gatehouse Entrance: Located at (8, 5). Entering at (8, 5) triggers the map transition into Route 22 Gate at (4, 7).
