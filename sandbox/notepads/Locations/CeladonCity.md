# Celadon City - Points of Interest & Topology

## Connections
- East: Route 7 at (49, 10..11) [Entered Turn 11727]
- West: Route 16 (Cycling Road gatehouse & Cut tree to Flying house)

## Layout & Landmarks
- Eastern Avenue: 2-tile wide paved road entering from Route 7 at cols 45-49, rows 10-11.
- Northern tree boundary at rows 7-9 (cols 46-54).
- Southern tree boundary at rows 12-15 (cols 46-51).

## Major Buildings & Points of Interest
- **Celadon Pokémon Center**: Healing station & PC.
- **Celadon Department Store**: Multi-floor superstore (Roof drinks: Fresh Water, Soda Pop, Lemonade; Evolution Stones; TMs; Battle items).
- **Rocket Game Corner**: Slots casino; secret switch behind poster unlocks stairs to Team Rocket Hideout.
- **Team Rocket Hideout**: 4 basement floors (B1F-B4F), Lift Key, Giovanni boss fight, Silph Scope item.
- **Celadon Gym**: Located behind a Cut bush in southern Celadon. Leader Erika (Grass-type, Rainbow Badge, TM21 Mega Drain).
- **Celadon Mansion**: Game Freak developers, rear penthouse entrance contains gift Eevee.
- **Celadon Diner**: NPC gives Coin Case needed to hold Game Corner coins.
- **Celadon Hotel**: Southeast building.
