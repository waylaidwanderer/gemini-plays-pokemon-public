- Staircase (13, 3): 1-tile wide wooden staircase descending south from upper terrace at (13, 2) onto lower elevation reddish-brown cave floor at (13, 4). Flanked by purple boulders at (12, 3) and (14, 3).
- Lower Area (Cols 7..14+, Rows 4..5+): Lower elevation dark reddish-brown cave floor accessed via Staircase (13, 3).
- Lower Southern Corridor (Rows 7..10): Reddish-brown cave floor visible south of Row 7 boulders.
