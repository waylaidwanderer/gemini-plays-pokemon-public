# Route 21 Geography & Mechanics

## Overview
- Water route connecting Pallet Town (north) to Cinnabar Island (south).
- Requires HM03 Surf to navigate.
- North entrance connects to Pallet Town water channel at columns 5-6.

## Navigation & Waterway Layout
- Northern Channel: Narrow 2-tile wide water channel at columns 5 and 6, flanked by shoreline walls at column 4 (west) and column 7 (east).
- Heading south toward Cinnabar Island.
- Eastern Landmass: Grassy plateau with tall grass lies to the east across the shoreline (columns 8-11, starting at row 4).
