# Location: Fortree City

## Overview
A unique Hoenn city built among the treetops, where houses are suspended in giant trees connected by wooden ladders and rope bridges. Connected to Route 119 on the west and Route 120 on the east. Home to the Fortree Gym (Leader Winona - Flying-type specialist).

## Connection Points
- West Entrance: Route 119 boundary at (0, 6..7).
- East Exit: Route 120 (leading toward Ancient Tomb, Route 121, and Lilycove City).

## Key Landmarks & Buildings
- Secret Base Decoration Shop: Elevated doorway at (37, 13), ladder at (37, 16). Sells desks, chairs, and ornaments.
- North-Central Treehouse: Doorway at (25, 2) on elevated platform above Gym ladder.
- Invisible Barrier (Kecleon): Blocks the ground corridor to Fortree Gym at (25, 8). Interacting prompts 'Something unseeable is in the way.' Requires Devon Scope from Steven on Route 120.
- Northeast Treehouse: Door at (32, 2) on elevated platform. Wingull delivery resident with Wingull.
- Pokémon Center: Located on western avenue. Blue sliding front doors at (5, 6), approached via row 7 -> (5, 7) facing North. Interior: spawn at (7, 8), Nurse Joy counter at (7, 3), approached from (7, 4) facing North. Fully healed and checkpoint set on turn 9080!
- Poké Mart: Blue-roofed building located at cols 3..6, rows 11..14 south of the Pokémon Center. Exterior entrance door at (4, 14), approached from (4, 15) facing North. Interior entrance mat at (3, 7). Customer counter at (1, 4), approached from (1, 5) facing North. Clerk greets with BUY/SELL/QUIT menu.
- Fortree Gym: Located in the south-central area among the trees. Initially blocked by an invisible barrier (Kecleon) until Devon Scope is obtained from Steven on Route 120.
- Treehouse Ladders & Rope Bridges: Suspended wooden walkways linking elevated treehouses.
- Signpost: Located at (6, 9) south of the Pokémon Center. Text: "FORTREE CITY / “The treetop city that frolics with nature.”"
- Main Avenue: Runs east along row 7 and row 8.
