# Celadon Department Store 1F

## Overview
Ground floor / Service Counter of the massive Celadon Department Store (identified as MART exterior).

## Layout & Landmarks
- Entrance Mat: Cols 16..17, Row 7. Exits south to Celadon City at (10, 13).
- Service Counter: Rows 1..2, Cols 16..17 (houses registers/equipment).
- Floor Directory Board: Located at (14, 1). Accessible from (14, 2) facing North. Text confirmed: "1F: SERVICE COUNTER" [Turn 5502].
- Stairs to 2F: Classic escalator/staircase at (12, 1).
- Display Cases: Along east wall at (19, 2..5).
- Structure to west: Panel at (11, 2..3) (possible elevator).
