# Location: Route 118

## Overview
A major east-west route connecting Mauville City on the west to Route 119 (north) and Route 123 (east). The route is bisected by a large river requiring HM03 Surf to cross.

## Connection Points
- West Exit: Mauville City (connects at cols 0..3, rows 6..8).
- North Fork: Route 119 (leading through tall grass to the Weather Institute and Fortree City).
- East Fork: Route 123 (southern path).

## Landmarks & Features
- Western Shore: Grassy area with ledges, trainers, and sandy beach leading down to the water.
- Good Rod Fisherman: A fisherman on the shore gives the Good Rod.
- Steven Stone Encounter: Steven appears on the eastern shore after surfing across for the first time.
- River: Waterway requiring HM03 Surf.
