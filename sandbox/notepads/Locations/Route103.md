# Location: Route 103

## Overview
A waterway and coastal route located north of Oldale Town. The route is divided by a large body of water into Western (Oldale Town side) and Eastern (Route 110 side) sections.

## Connection Points
- West Section: North exit of Oldale Town (site of Rival Battle #1).
- East Section: Connects to Route 110 via western fork at (79, 10) <-> Route 110 (0, 70).
- Waterway: Divides East and West sections (requires HM03 Surf to cross).

## Landmarks & Features (East Section)
- East entrance path: Begins at (79, 10) connecting seamlessly from Route 110 (0, 70).
- Cut Tree: (72, 8) - [CLEARED] Cleared with HM01 Cut, opening passage north into upper terrace.
- Item Ball (Terrace Alcove): (~50, 2..4) - Located on the far west edge of the upper terrace overlooking the sea, reached only from the high plateau.
- Item Ball (Central Alcove): (~65, 5) - Visible in pine alcove on the upper terrace.
- Berry Patch: Soft soil berry patch with 4 berry plants located on the upper terrace at cols ~57..59, rows ~1..3.
- Cut Tree (Shortcut): (67, 7) - Cut tree north of Marcos connecting lower road to upper terrace.
- Lower Road Topography: Lower dirt road (rows 10-13) dead-ends at the western water lookout (44, 7). Dense pine trees prevent crossing from the lower road directly into the northern upper terrace. Access to the berry patch and Item Ball requires entering via Cut at (72, 8) or (67, 7).
- Water Inlet: Located at cols 46..48 at western end of dirt road.
