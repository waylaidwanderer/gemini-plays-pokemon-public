# Location: Route 103

## Overview
A waterway and coastal route located north of Oldale Town. The route is divided by a large body of water into Western (Oldale Town side) and Eastern (Route 110 side) sections.

## Connection Points
- West Section: North exit of Oldale Town (site of Rival Battle #1).
- East Section: Connects to Route 110 via western fork at (79, 10) <-> Route 110 (0, 70).
- Waterway: Divides East and West sections (requires HM03 Surf to cross).

## Landmarks & Features (East Section)
- East entrance path: Begins at (79, 10) connecting seamlessly from Route 110 (0, 70).
- Cut Tree: (72, 8) - HM01 Cut shrub blocking passage north to upper terrace.
- Berry Tree: Located at (67, 5) in upper terrace.
