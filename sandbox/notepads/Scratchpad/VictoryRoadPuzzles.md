# Victory Road Puzzle Mechanics & Master Log

## Floor 1F Puzzle System
- **Boulder 1 Route to Switch Plate (17, 13):**
  1. Boulder 1 starts at (5, 15).
  2. Push South from (5, 14) to (5, 16).
  3. Push East along row 16 to (9, 16).
  4. Push North from (9, 17) into row 14 at (9, 14).
  5. Push East along row 14 to (16, 14).
  6. Push North from (16, 15) to (16, 13).
  7. Push East from (15, 13) onto Switch Plate at (17, 13).
- **Result:**
  - Depresses Switch Plate at (17, 13), opening Shutter at (15, 7).
  - Ascend ramp at (14, 11..10) onto eastern upper plateau.
  - Pass through opened Shutter at (15, 7) and climb ladder at (17, 1) to 2F.
