# Pokémon Mansion - Comprehensive Switch & Routing Guide

## Switch States & Multi-Floor Effects
- **State A (Default / Exterior Reset)**:
  - 1F (9, 4..5) Shutter: OPEN
  - 1F (18..19, 8) Shutter: CLOSED
  - 1F (12..17, 13) Shutter: OPEN
  - 1F (24..25, 13) Shutter: OPEN
  - 2F (9, 4..5) Shutter: OPEN
  - 2F (21, 17) Shutter: CLOSED
  - 3F (15, 10..11) / (15, 4..5) Shutter: CLOSED

- **State B (Toggled via 2F (10, 5) or 1F (2, 5))**:
  - 1F (9, 4..5) Shutter: CLOSED
  - 1F (18..19, 8) Shutter: OPEN
  - 1F (12..17, 13) Shutter: CLOSED
  - 1F (24..25, 13) Shutter: CLOSED
  - 2F (9, 4..5) Shutter: CLOSED
  - 2F (21, 17) Shutter: OPEN
  - 3F (15, 10..11) / (15, 4..5) Shutter: OPEN (grants access to balcony pits!)

## 3F Balcony Pit Destinations & B1F Routing
- **Left Pit Drop at 3F (16..17, 14)**: Lands on 1F South Wing at (16, 14) [Enclosed display garden sector; TM03 Swords Dance, Scientist Ted].
- **Right Pit Drop at 3F (19, 14)**: Lands on 1F Northern Landing Sector at (18, 14) [In State B, the (18..19, 8) shutter gate is OPEN, granting direct north access to Scientist at (27, 11) and B1F descending staircase!].

## Master Verified Route to B1F & Secret Key
1. Heal at Cinnabar Pokémon Center.
2. Enter Pokémon Mansion 1F at (5, 27).
3. Walk north along red carpet to (5, 10) stairs -> ascend to 2F.
4. On 2F: Walk to (10, 6), face Up, and press A at (10, 5) Mewtwo statue to toggle to **State B**.
5. On 2F: Walk to (6, 1) stairs -> ascend to 3F.
6. On 3F (State B): Walk through open (15, 4..5) shutter gate to (19, 14) (Right Balcony Pit).
7. Step Down into the **Right Balcony Pit at (19, 14)** -> lands on 1F at (18, 14).
8. On 1F (State B): Walk North through open (18..19, 8) shutter gate to northern hallway (row 3), then East to column 26.
9. Walk South along column 26 towards staircase at (25, 14) to enter B1F.
10. Explore B1F and retrieve the **Secret Key**!
