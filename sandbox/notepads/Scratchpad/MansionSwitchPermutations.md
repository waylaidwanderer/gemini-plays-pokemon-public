# Pokémon Mansion - Comprehensive Switch & Routing Guide

## Switch States & Multi-Floor Effects
- **State A (Default / Exterior Reset)**:
  - 1F (9, 4..5) Shutter: OPEN
  - 1F (18..19, 8) Shutter: CLOSED
  - 1F (12..17, 13) Shutter: OPEN
  - 1F (24..25, 13) Shutter: OPEN
  - 2F (9, 4..5) Shutter: OPEN
  - 2F (21, 17) Shutter: CLOSED
  - 3F (15, 10..11) Shutter: CLOSED

- **State B (Toggled)**:
  - 1F (9, 4..5) Shutter: CLOSED
  - 1F (18..19, 8) Shutter: OPEN
  - 1F (12..17, 13) Shutter: CLOSED
  - 1F (24..25, 13) Shutter: CLOSED
  - 2F (9, 4..5) Shutter: CLOSED
  - 2F (21, 17) Shutter: OPEN
  - 3F (15, 10..11) Shutter: OPEN (grants access to balcony pits!)

## 3F Balcony Pit Destinations
- **Left Pit Drop at 3F (16..17, 14)**: Lands on 1F South Wing at (16, 14) [Direct access to Scientist Ted, TM03, and south wing rooms].
- **Right Pit Drop at 3F (19, 14)**: Lands on 1F Landing Sector at (18, 14) [Direct access to north sector via (18, 8) in State B].

## Active Route Plan to B1F & Secret Key
1. From 1F (9, 1): Walk row 1 -> col 12 -> row 11 -> (7, 10) stairs to 2F.
2. On 2F: Navigate to (6, 1) stairs to ascend to 3F Main Sector.
3. On 3F: Toggle switch at (10, 5) to State B -> walk through (15, 10..11) open gate to row 14.
4. Jump down the **Left Balcony Pit at (16..17, 14)** to land at 1F (16, 14).
5. Explore 1F South Wing, toggle switch at (18, 25) as required, locate B1F descending staircase, and enter B1F.
6. Explore B1F to retrieve the Secret Key.
