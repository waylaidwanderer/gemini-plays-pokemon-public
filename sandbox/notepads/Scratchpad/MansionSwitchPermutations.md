# Mansion Switch State Tracking & Routing Scratchpad

## Verified Full Solution to Reach B1F:
1. **Reset to State A**:
   - Go to 1F (2, 5) statue switch and toggle it to STATE A (Default).
2. **Enter 2F Northwest Room in State A**:
   - Take 1F (5, 10) stairs up to 2F (5, 11).
   - On 2F: walk (5, 11) -> (10, 11) -> (10, 4) -> Left through OPEN shutter at (9, 4..5) into NW room!
3. **Toggle to State B from INSIDE 2F Northwest Room**:
   - Walk to 2F (2, 5) statue switch and PRESS IT!
   - This sets global state to STATE B, opening 3F (15, 10..11) shutter!
4. **Ascend to 3F**:
   - Walk from (2, 5) to 2F (6, 1) stairs (inside the NW room).
   - Ascend to 3F (6, 2).
5. **Pass through 3F Open Shutter to Balcony Drop**:
   - Walk east to (15, 5) doorway into East Wing.
   - Walk south to (14, 11), pass through OPEN shutter at (15, 11) to (16, 11).
   - Walk south to (16, 14) and jump down the Balcony Drop Pit!
6. **Descend to B1F**:
   - Land on 1F Enclosed Wing at (16, 14).
   - Walk east to (25, 16), north to (25, 14) descending staircase.
   - Enter B1F!
