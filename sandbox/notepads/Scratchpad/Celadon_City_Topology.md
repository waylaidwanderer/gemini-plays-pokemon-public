# Celadon City Topology & Master Unblocked Routes

- Door at (17, 27) in Game Corner Plaza: Enters True Main Casino Floor ("Celadon Game Corner") at (3, 7).

- Tile (27, 21): Solid signboard obstacle on Row 21.
- Tile (32, 19): Solid fence border post.
- Column 7 Slope Gap (7, 11): Open beige dirt slope gap unlocking Row 8/9 Northern Highway running East across x=7..36 above all building roofs.
- Column 36 Tree Gap (36, 28): Open tree gap connecting Row 12/16 Highway South to Row 30/36 South Street.

2. Walk North 7 steps along Column 10 avenue to Row 13 at (10, 13).
3. Walk West 3 steps along Row 13 to Column 7 at (7, 13).
4. Walk North 2 steps along Column 7 through slope gap (7, 11) to Row 9 Northern Highway at (7, 9).
5. Walk East 29 steps along Row 9 Northern Highway across x=8..36 to Column 36 at (36, 9).
6. Walk South 21 steps down Column 36 through tree gap (36, 28) to South Street at (36, 30) / (36, 36).
7. Walk West 8 steps along South Street to (28, 36) warp -> Warps into Game Corner Plaza at (18, 0).
8. In Game Corner Plaza, walk to door (17, 27) -> Warps into True Main Casino Floor at (3, 7).
