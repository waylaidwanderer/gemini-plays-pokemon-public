# Safari Zone Master Routing & Strategy (Run 6 Direct Route)

## Direct Route to Area 3 (West) - ~484 Steps for Objectives!
1. **Safari Zone Gatehouse**:
   - Pay ¥500, receive 30 Safari Balls, enter Safari Zone Center at (15, 24).
2. **Safari Zone Center (Area 0)** (~15 steps total):
   - From (15, 24), sprint straight WEST along row 24 southern boulevard across cols 15 -> 0.
   - Step into (0, 24) to enter Safari Zone Area 3 (West) on step 16!
3. **Safari Zone Area 3 (West)** (~484 steps remaining):
   - Survey southern clearing for **Gold Teeth** on the ground and collect them.
   - Survey northern/northwestern sector for **Secret House** and speak with attendant to receive **HM03 (Surf)**.
4. **Fuchsia City Delivery**:
   - Return / exit to Fuchsia City and visit Warden's House at (27, 27) to trade Gold Teeth for **HM04 (Strength)**.
