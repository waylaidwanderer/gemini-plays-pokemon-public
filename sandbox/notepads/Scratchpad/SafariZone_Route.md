# Safari Zone - Complete Golden Route to Teeth & Surf

## Phase 1: Center to Area 1 (East)
- Start at Gatehouse entrance at `(15, 25)`.
- Walk UP to `(15, 21)`, RIGHT to Column 28, UP to Row 11, RIGHT to Column 30 to transition to Area 1 (East) at `(0, 22)`.

## Phase 2: Area 1 (East) to Area 2 (North)
- From `(0, 22)`:
- Walk UP Column 20 (bypassing tree graphic at `(20, 4)`) to Row 5 `(20, 5)`.
- Walk LEFT along Row 5 to Column 0, transition to Area 2 (North) at `(39, 31)`.

## Phase 3: Area 2 (North) to Area 3 (West)
- From `(39, 31)`:
- Walk LEFT to Column 15 on Row 28: `(15, 28)`.
- Walk LEFT to Column 13: `(13, 28)`.
- Walk UP Column 13 to Row 21: `(13, 21)` (bypassing Column 15 Row 27 cliff wall!).
- Walk RIGHT to Column 13, DOWN to Row 30: `(13, 30)`.
- Walk LEFT to Column 8 Row 30: `(8, 30)`.
- Walk DOWN Column 8 through the statue gap at `(8, 34)` to Row 35: `(8, 35)`.
- Walk DOWN to Row 36: `(8, 36)` to transition directly into Area 3 (West) at `(26, 0)`.

## Phase 4: Area 3 (West) - Ascent/Descent & Gold Teeth
- From `(26, 0)`:
- Walk LEFT to Column 25, DOWN Column 25 to Row 14: `(25, 14)` (bypassing Row 4 Column 26 ledge!).
- Walk DOWN to Row 18: `(25, 18)`.
- Walk LEFT to Column 21: `(21, 18)`.
- Walk UP to climb East Stairs: `(21, 16)`.
- Walk LEFT across Plateau to West Stairs: `(6, 16)`.
- Walk DOWN to descend West Stairs: `(6, 20)`.
- Walk LEFT to Column 2 Row 20: `(2, 20)`.
- Walk LEFT to Column 0 Row 20: `(0, 20)`.
- Walk UP Column 0 to Row 13: `(0, 13)` (transitions to Safari Zone Center northwest compartment at `(29, 25)`).

## Phase 5: Retrieve Gold Teeth (Center Northwest Compartment)
- From `(29, 25)` inside Center:
- Walk DOWN to `(29, 26)`.
- Walk LEFT to Column 19: `(19, 26)`.
- Walk UP to `(19, 25)` to pick up the **Gold Teeth**!
- Walk RIGHT to Column 29: `(29, 26)`.
- Walk UP to `(29, 25)` to transition back to Area 3 (West) at `(0, 13)`.

## Phase 6: Retrieve Surf (Secret House)
- From `(0, 13)` inside Area 3 (West):
- Walk UP Column 0 to Row 8: `(0, 8)`.
- Walk RIGHT to Column 3: `(3, 8)`.
- Walk UP to enter the **Secret House** at `(3, 8)`.
- Stand in front of NPC at `(2, 7)` inside, talk to him to obtain **HM03 (Surf)**!
