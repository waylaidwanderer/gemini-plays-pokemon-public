# Safari Zone - Complete Golden Route to Gold Teeth

## Gold Teeth Location
- **Gold Teeth:** Located at `(19, 25)` inside **Area 3 (West)** on the southern ground level.
- **CRITICAL INTERACTION:** To pick them up, the player MUST stand at `(19, 26)` (directly below/south of the teeth on the southern ground corridor Row 26), face **UP** (north), and press **A**!

## Step-by-Step Walkable Golden Route (From Safari Center Transition)
1. Transition from Safari Zone Center `(0, 11)` to Area 3 (West) at `(29, 23)` by walking LEFT.
2. Walk LEFT 8 steps along Row 23 to Column 21 at `(21, 23)`.
3. Walk UP 5 steps along Column 21 to Row 18 at `(21, 18)` (ground level).
4. Walk UP 2 steps to climb the East Stairs of the Plateau to `(21, 16)` (plateau level).
5. Walk LEFT on the plateau to the West Stairs at `(6, 16)`.
6. Walk DOWN to descend the West Stairs at `(6, 19)` to `(6, 20)` (western grass).
7. Walk LEFT/RIGHT to the verified open vertical column (to be found by grid-search of Columns 4 to 17).
8. Walk DOWN the open column to Row 26 (the Row 26 Highway).
9. Walk along Row 26 to Column 19 at `(19, 26)`.
10. Stand at `(19, 26)` facing **UP** (north).
11. Press **A** to pick up the Gold Teeth!
