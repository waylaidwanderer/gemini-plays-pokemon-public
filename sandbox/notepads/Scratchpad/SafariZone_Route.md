# Safari Zone - Complete Golden Route to Teeth & Surf

## Battle Flee Strategy (Contingency Plan)
During overworld movement, wild battles can trigger.
1. When a battle starts, wait for the screen to transition.
2. The menu defaults to "BALL" (Safari Zone's equivalent of FIGHT) in the top-left corner.
3. Press DOWN once to go to "THROW ROCK", then RIGHT once to highlight "RUN" (or from top-left, press DOWN and then RIGHT).
   - Menu layout:
     BALL         BAIT
     THROW ROCK   RUN
4. Press A to select RUN and escape.
5. In case of lag or timing delays, press B a few times first to clear submenus before attempting the RUN inputs.

## The Walkable Golden Route
Below is the exact step-by-step path starting from the Safari Zone Gatehouse entrance at `(15, 25)` inside Safari Zone Center.

### Phase 1: Safari Zone Center to Area 1 (East)
1. Start at entrance `(15, 25)`.
2. Walk UP 3 steps to `(15, 22)`.
3. Walk RIGHT 13 steps along Row 22 to `(28, 22)` (utilizes the ground-level Column 28/Row 12-15 eastern corridor).
4. Walk UP 12 steps along Column 28 to `(28, 10)`.
5. Walk RIGHT 2 steps to transition to Area 1 (East) at `(30, 10)`.

### Phase 2: Area 1 (East) to Area 2 (North)
1. Emerge in Area 1 (East) at `(0, 22)`.
2. Walk RIGHT 20 steps along Row 22/24/20 detours to the plateau stairs:
   - Walk DOWN to `(0, 23)` then `(0, 24)`.
   - Walk RIGHT 20 steps to `(20, 24)`.
   - Walk UP 2 steps to `(20, 22)`.
   - Walk UP 2 steps to climb plateau stairs to `(20, 20)`.
   - Walk LEFT 8 steps to `(12, 20)`.
   - Walk DOWN 2 steps to descend stairs to `(12, 22)`.
   - Walk LEFT 4 steps to Column 8 at `(8, 22)`.
3. Walk UP 14 steps along Column 8 to `(8, 8)`.
4. Walk RIGHT 4 steps to climb northern plateau stairs at `(12, 8)` to `(12, 6)`.
5. Walk RIGHT 5 steps on plateau to `(17, 6)`.
6. Walk DOWN 2 steps to descend plateau stairs to `(17, 8)`.
7. Walk RIGHT 3 steps to Column 20 at `(20, 8)`.
8. Walk UP 3 steps along Column 20 (bypassing the tree graphic) to Row 5 at `(20, 5)`.
9. Walk LEFT 20 steps along Row 5 to transition to Area 2 (North) at `(0, 5)`.

### Phase 3: Area 2 (North) to Area 3 (West)
1. Emerge in Area 2 (North) at `(39, 31)`.
2. Walk LEFT 17 steps along Row 31 to Column 22 at `(22, 31)`.
3. Walk UP 9 steps along Column 22 (climbing the Western Southern Plateau stairs at `(22, 23)`) to `(22, 22)`.
4. Walk LEFT 6 steps on the plateau to `(16, 22)`.
5. Walk DOWN 6 steps (descending stairs at `(16, 27)`) to grass at `(16, 28)`.
6. Walk LEFT 4 steps to `(12, 28)`.
7. Walk DOWN 2 steps to `(12, 30)` (to bypass the pond!).
8. Walk LEFT 4 steps to `(8, 30)`.
9. Walk DOWN 5 steps through the statue gap at `(8, 34)` to `(8, 35)`.
10. Walk DOWN 2 steps to transition to Area 3 (West) at `(8, 37)`.

### Phase 4: Area 3 (West) to Safari Zone Center (East Compartment)
1. Emerge in Area 3 (West) at `(9, 0)`.
2. Walk DOWN 13 steps to `(9, 13)`.
3. Walk LEFT 9 steps to transition (warp) to Center (East Compartment) at `(0, 13)`.

### Phase 5: Safari Zone Center (East Compartment) to Gold Teeth
1. Emerge in Center (East Compartment) at `(29, 25)` on the south side of the wall.
2. Walk DOWN 1 step to `(29, 26)`.
3. Walk LEFT 10 steps along Row 26 to `(19, 26)`.
4. Stand at `(19, 26)` facing UP, press A to pick up the **Gold Teeth**!
