## Part 2: Route through Area 0 (Center) to Area 1 (East)
1. Spawn in Center at `(15, 25)`.
2. Walk North to `(15, 23)`.
3. Walk East along Row 23 to Column 29 `(29, 23)`.
4. Walk East along Row 23 (or Row 26) to Column 29 and step Right to transition to **Area 1 (East)** at `(0, 11)`.

## Part 3: Route through Area 1 (East) to Area 2 (North)
1. Enter Area 1 (East) at `(0, 11)`.
2. Walk East and climb onto the plateau using the southern stairs at `(20, 21)` to stand at `(20, 20)`.
3. Walk West on the plateau and descend using the western stairs at `(12, 21)` to land at ground level `(12, 22)`.
4. Walk North to the northern stairs at `(12, 7)` and climb onto the plateau to stand at `(12, 6)`.
5. Walk East on the plateau and descend using the northeastern stairs at `(17, 7)` to land at ground level `(17, 8)`.
6. Walk North and West to the northern exit at `(0, 5)` and transition to **Area 2 (North)** at `(39, 31)`.

## Part 4: Route through Area 2 (North) to Area 3 (West)
1. Enter Area 2 (North) at `(39, 31)`.
2. Walk West on the ground level to the central plateau stairs at `(22, 23)` and climb onto the central plateau at `(22, 22)`.
3. **CRITICAL COLLISION CORRECTION (Column 16):**
   - Note: Traversal along Column 16 from Row 28 to Row 9 is impossible on the ground level because Column 16 is blocked by a solid cliff wall at Row 15.
   - Instead, the northward traversal along Column 16 to Row 9 occurs entirely **ON TOP of the plateau**.
   - Descend from the plateau using the western stairs at `(16, 27)` to land on the ground level at `(16, 28)`.
4. Walk West along Row 28 to Column 13, walk North along Column 13 to Row 9 to reach the northwestern ground level north of Rest House 2 (circumventing the blocked Column 16 on the ground).
5. From the northwestern ground level, walk North of Rest House 2 on Row 9 to Column 35 to bypass the Row 10 tree line.
6. Walk South on Column 35 to Row 13, and climb the eastern plateau stairs at `(32, 13)` or `(33, 13)`.
7. Traverse the plateau South and West, then walk down the West stairs or jump down the south-facing ledges to the southern ground level (Rows 25-35).
8. Walk West along the southern grass corridor, detouring South to Row 34 or 35 to bypass the solid Column 5 tree barrier, then walk West past Column 5 and enter **Area 3 (West)** at columns 0-4, rows 30-35.

## Part 5: Area 3 (West) to Secret House (HM03 & Gold Teeth)
1. Enter Area 3 (West) from Area 2 (North).
2. Walk West and South to find the **Gold Teeth** on the ground (typically around `(19, 7)` or similar in the bottom/middle-left grass area - verify exact location visually).
3. Continue West to the **Secret House** (usually located in the far west/southwest).
4. Enter the Secret House and speak to the attendant to receive **HM03 (Surf)**.
5. Exit the Safari Zone (or wait for step limit to expire) to complete the primary goal.
