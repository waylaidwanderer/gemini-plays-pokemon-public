# Scratchpad: Cerulean Cave Routing & Frontier Ledger

## Route to Mewtwo (B1F) - Verified Topology
1. **2F Northwest Isolation**:
   - Ladder E (7, 1), Ladder B (22, 6), Ladder C (19, 7), and Ladder D (29, 1) sectors on 2F are all physically isolated from Ladder A at (1, 3).
   - Column 2 is a solid vertical rock wall across rows 1-4; Row 4 across cols 3-7 is solid rock.
2. **Access Route to Ladder A (1, 3)**:
   - Ascend via the **Southwest Ladder at (3, 11) on 1F**.
   - Southwest Ladder on 1F is reached by Surfing through the Northern Water Canal (cols 8-14, rows 6-14) into the southwest land sector at (7, 13).
   - Walk from (7, 13) to (3, 11) on 1F.
3. **Ascent to 2F & Ladder A**:
   - Climb 1F SW Ladder (3, 11) -> Arrive at 2F SW Sector.
   - Walk north through open floor corridor (cols 0-7, rows 5 -> 2) -> (1, 2) -> Ladder A at (1, 3).
4. **Descent to B1F & Mewtwo Capture**:
   - Descend Ladder A (1, 3) to B1F.
   - Navigate B1F maze to Mewtwo platform.
   - Interact with Mewtwo to trigger battle.
   - Use Master Ball (Bag Slot 18) to capture Mewtwo!
