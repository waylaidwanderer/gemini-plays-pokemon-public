# Scratchpad: Cerulean Cave 2F Routing & Master Route to Mewtwo

## Master Route via Ladder E (9, 1) -> Ladder A (1, 3) -> B1F
1. From 1F, take Northwest Ramp at (15, 3), walk west along Northern Terrace to (7, 2), step Up to Ladder E at (7, 1).
2. Arrive at 2F Ladder E (9, 1).
3. Walk Left 1, Down 2 to (8, 3).
4. Walk Left 5 along Row 3 to (3, 3) (or from (8, 1) walk Left 5 to (3, 1)).
5. Walk Down along Column 3: (3, 3) -> (3, 4) -> (3, 5).
6. Walk West along Row 5: (3, 5) -> (2, 5) -> (1, 5) -> (0, 5).
7. Walk North along Column 0: (0, 5) -> (0, 4) -> (0, 3).
8. Step East 1 into **Ladder A at (1, 3)** to descend to B1F!
9. Locate Mewtwo on B1F and use the Master Ball (Bag Slot 18) for guaranteed capture!
