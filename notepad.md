- **Post-Battle Match Call Registration:** After obtaining the PokéNav, defeating a Match Call-eligible trainer triggers an automatic overworld dialogue box immediately after the post-battle fade-in (including a ~3s registration fanfare) that must be cleared with `B` before overworld movement can resume.
- **Overworld Rock Smash, Surf, Strength & Dive Sequences:** Facing a breakable boulder or water edge and pressing `A` prints `"Would you like to use ROCK SMASH? / SURF?"` and opens `[YES / NO]` (~1200ms); wait `1500ms` after the first `A` before pressing `A` a second time to confirm `YES`, then wait `1000ms` and press `B` to close `"used ROCK SMASH! / used SURF!"` and play the field move animation (`A, sleep 1500, A, sleep 1000, B, sleep 3500`). Once Strength is active, **every boulder push temporarily locks player movement and aborts the current `press_buttons` call**; in `run_code`, place each boulder push at the end of its own `tools.press_buttons(...)` call and start the next call with `"sleep 1000"`. Would you like to use DIVE?" [YES / NO]`, followed by `sleep 1500, A, sleep 1000, B, sleep 4000`); while underwater beneath light-filtered surface tiles, press **`B`** to surface (`"Light is filtering down from above. Would you like to use DIVE?" [YES / NO]`, followed by `sleep 1500, A, sleep 1000, B, sleep 4000`; never append a stray `"B"` press after underwater movement unless you intend to surface). For **Waterfall** (`HM07`), bumping into a waterfall tile (`MB_WATERFALL`) with `Up` from the base tile (`(18, 68)`) triggers a pushback current that automatically turns the player `Down` (`DIR_SOUTH`); instead, step `Down` 1 tile to `(18, 69)`, take a single `Up` step onto `(18, 68)` without pressing `Up` again, and press **`A`** (`A, sleep 1500, A, sleep 1000, B, sleep 6500`).

[Yes / No]` with the cursor on `Yes`; you must press **`A`** (not `B`) on `"Stop learning [MOVE]?"` to confirm `Yes` and skip the move.
- **Gen 3 Physical/Special Split, Stat-Drop Pivoting, Non-Wrapping 2x2 Menus & Full Restore Bypass:** In Gen 3, Ground (`Mud Shot`), Rock (`Rock Tomb`), and Fighting (`Rock Smash`) are **Physical** (affected by `Intimidate`), whereas Water (`Water Gun`, `Surf`, `Waterfall`) is **Special** (ignores `Intimidate`). Switching a Pokémon out and back in clears all `Intimidate` Attack drops, `Icy Wind`/`Rock Tomb` Speed drops, and `Sand-Attack` Accuracy drops. Neither the 2x2 battle action menu nor the 2x2 move menu wraps around, so corner pairs (`Up, Left` for `FIGHT`/Slot 1, `Up, Right` for Slot 2, `Down, Right` for Slot 4) deterministically select the intended option regardless of prior cursor memory. [Yes / No]` appears (`B` flees, `A` opens the Party menu), whereas in a Trainer battle `"Choose a POKéMON."` opens directly with no `[Yes / No]` prompt.
