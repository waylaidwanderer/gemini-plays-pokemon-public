Safari Zone East Area Route:
1. Enter at (0, 22). Walk East to X=4, South to Y=24, East through grass to X=20, then North to stairs at (20, 21).
2. Go up stairs at (20, 21) to reach plateau. Walk West across the plateau to stairs at (12, 21).
3. Go down stairs at (12, 21) to reach lower ground. Walk West to X=7, then North to Y=7, then East to stairs at (12, 7).
4. Go up stairs at (12, 7) to reach northern plateau. Walk East to stairs at (17, 7).
