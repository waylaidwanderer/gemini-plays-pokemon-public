VICTORY ROAD 1F MASTER ROUTE:
1. Activate STRENGTH from the Start Menu -> POKeMON -> Blastoise.
2. The puzzle boulder is at (5,15). Walk to (5,16) and push it UP to (5,14).
3. Walk around to (4,14) and push it RIGHT along the safe Y=14 corridor all the way to (16,14). (This avoids Blue Rubble on Y=15/16).
4. Push it UP twice to (16,12). (You must walk around via 16,15 and 16,14 to push).
5. Walk around the boulder via the (14,13) gap to reach its left side at (15,12).
6. Push the boulder RIGHT to (17,12).
7. Walk to (17,11) and push DOWN onto the switch at (17,13).
8. Walk West to visually verify the Level 3 barriers at (1,14) and (2,14) have been removed.
9. Proceed to the ladder at (1,1) to 2F West.
