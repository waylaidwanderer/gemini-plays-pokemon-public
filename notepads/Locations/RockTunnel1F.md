# Rock Tunnel 1F - Points of Interest & Notes

## Connections
- North: Route 10 North Entrance at (15, 3) [Entered Turn 3120]
- South: Route 10 South Exit (leads to Lavender Town)
- Ladders: Connects to Rock Tunnel B1F

## Layout & Landmarks
- Entrance Chamber: Located around (15, 3). North wall at rows 0-1, west wall at cols 11-12, east wall at cols 18-20. Open corridor extends south across cols 14-17 from row 3 downwards.
- Darkness Mechanic: Cave is dark without HM05 Flash, but rock wall tiles display textured pixels against the black background, allowing clear grid navigation.

## Verified Encounters & Trainers
- Wild Pokémon: Geodude, Zubat, Machop, Onix
