## Layout & Landmarks
- Entrance Chamber: Located around (15, 3).
- Row 7: Open horizontal corridor connecting entrance (col 15) to east corridor (col 20).
- Central rock divider: Located across rows 8-9 (cols 14-19).
- North-East Corridor: Extends south along col 20 across rows 7-13.
- Horizontal Wall Divider: Spans rows 14-15 across cols 18-31+.
- Row 13 Highway: Wide open horizontal corridor extending east from col 20 past col 31.
- Southern Corridor: Parallel passage across rows 16-17 (cols 16-21, 24-29) leading west to ladder chamber at (17, 11).
- Darkness Mechanic: Cave is dark without HM05 Flash, but rock wall tiles display textured pixels against the black background, allowing clear grid navigation.
