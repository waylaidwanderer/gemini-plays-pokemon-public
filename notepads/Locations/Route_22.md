Route 22 Layout & Navigation:
- Entrance from Viridian City connects to Route 22 around Y=9 (Middle Path).
- The lower path (Route 22 Y=14, 15) is a return-only loop. Do not jump down the one-way south-facing ledges (like Y=13) into it, or you will get stuck in a return basin and have to loop back or use Fly.
- The upper path (Y=4) is a dead end westward, completely blocked by white fences at X=14 and X=15.
- The white fences at X=14 and X=15 block the path from Y=4 down to Y=11.
- To reach the Pokemon League gatehouse, bypass the white fences by walking West along Y=12, being careful NOT to jump down the ledge at Y=13.
