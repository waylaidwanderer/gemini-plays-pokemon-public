Route 22 Layout:
- Upper Path (Y=4 to Y=7): Accessed via the ramp at X=31, Y=7. Blocked westward by solid white pillars at X=15.
- Middle Path (Y=8 to Y=12): Accessed by jumping down the south-facing ledge at Y=7 from the Upper Path. You can bypass the X=15 pillars by walking West along Y=12. This path leads to the far West edge of the route (X=1).
- Lower Path (Y=14, Y=15): Accessed by jumping down the south-facing ledge at Y=13. This is a return-only path back to Viridian City.
