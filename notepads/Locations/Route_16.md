Route 16:
- Connected to Celadon City on the East. The transition between Celadon City and Route 16 is located at Y=18.
- The route is split into an upper and lower section. The lower section leads to a sleeping Snorlax blocking the way at (26, 10). The upper section is accessed by cutting a tree at (34, 9).
- Hidden House: Located in the upper section of Route 16. Enter by going through the gatehouse-like structure on the west end of the upper path. Contains an NPC who gives HM02 (Fly).
