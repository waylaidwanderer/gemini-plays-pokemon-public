- **Cerulean Poké Mart**: Located at (25, 25) on Map 0_3. The door is at (25, 25) with a passable tile in front of it at (25, 26).
- **Unknown Blue-Roofed Building**: Located at Columns 18-23, Rows 24-25. It has no entrance on its south side (Row 25) which consists of solid brick wall (TYPE_2889). We hypothesize this could be the Bike Shop or another landmark, but we must systematically locate its entrance.
