  - (21,15): Stairs UP to 2F East.
- BOULDER PUZZLE (WEST):
  - Boulder SPAWNS at (4,14).
  - Target switch is at (1,16).
  - Solution path: Push Left to (2,14), Down to (2,16), Left to (1,16).
- CENTRAL Pit (Level 1):
  - Low ground isolating the sections of 2F.
