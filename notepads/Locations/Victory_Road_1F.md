Victory Road 1F:
- TOPOLOGY: Raised Platform (Brown) and Lower Floor (Purple) are separated by a ledge. Walking and pushing boulders between them is BLOCKED. They act as solid walls to each other.
- EXIT: (8,17) is an exit warp to Route 23.
- BOULDERS: Boulder 1 is at (8,16). Switch is at (17,13).
- DEAD ENDS: Northern Purple Pit (via stairs at 5,13) is a completely enclosed dead end. Switch at (17,13) is ON the Brown platform. Do NOT drop the boulder into the Purple pit.
- SOLUTION FOR BOULDER 1:
  1. Push boulder Right along Row 16 to (9,16).
  2. Push boulder Up from (9,17) to (9,14). (Note: 8,17 and 9,17 do not warp unless you step Down off them).
  3. Push boulder Right along Row 14 to (15,14).
  4. Navigate to (15,15) and push boulder Up to (15,13).
  5. Navigate to (14,13) and push boulder Right to switch at (17,13).
