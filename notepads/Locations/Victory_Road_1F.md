Victory Road 1F:
- Boulder: (5,15). Switch: (17,13).
- TRUE MASTER PATH (All on Lower Purple Floor):
  1. Walk to (5,14) via X=4. Push DOWN to (5,16).
  2. Walk to (4,16). Push RIGHT to (9,16).
  3. Walk to (9,17). Push UP to (9,15).
  4. Walk to (8,15). Push RIGHT to (12,15).
  5. Walk to (12,16). Push RIGHT to (17,15).
  6. Walk to (17,16). Push UP to (17,13) Switch.
- Ledges: Y=14 (brown) is a raised platform. You can jump south to Y=15, but cannot push boulders onto it.
