Safari Zone (East Area):
- Transition from Center Area (X=29, Y=10) leads to East Area (X=0, Y=22).
Route to North Area:
1. Enter at (0, 22). Walk East to X=4, South to Y=24, East through grass to X=20, then North to stairs at (20, 21).
2. Go up stairs at (20, 21) to reach plateau. Walk West across the plateau to the stairs at (12, 21).
3. Go down stairs at (12, 21) to reach the lower ground and find the path North.
- Rest House is at (25, 9).
