Safari Zone (East Area):
- Transition from Center Area (X=29, Y=10) leads to East Area (X=0, Y=22).
- Transition to North Area is located at the far Northwest corner, accessible via X=-1.
Route to North Area:
1. Enter at (0, 22). Walk East to X=4, South to Y=24, East through grass to X=20, then North to stairs at (20, 21).
2. Go up stairs. Warning: Y=22 (X=7 to 15) is a trap. Walk West to find the path North (TODO: Verify exact route to stairs at 17, 7).
3. Go down stairs at (17, 7). Walk East and jump DOWN (South) over the one-way ledge at (19, 6).
4. Walk North through the grass to reach the Northern Plateau at Y=4.
5. Walk West along Y=4/Y=5 to bypass the tree trunks at X=0, reaching the open path at X=-1.
6. Walk North along X=-1 to enter the North Area.
- Rest House is at (25, 9).
