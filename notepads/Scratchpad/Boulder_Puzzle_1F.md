TRUE VICTORY ROAD PATH:
1. Push Boulder 2 at (2, 10) South to clear West path.
2. Push puzzle boulder at (5, 15) to switch at (17, 13). This opens Level 1 barrier at (10, 12).
3. Go down stairs at (5, 13) to Level 1.
4. Walk East through opened barrier (10, 12), North to (11, 5), West to stairs at (7, 7).
5. Go up stairs to Level 3 North.
6. Walk to ladder at (1, 1) -> go up to 2F (0, 8).
7. On 2F, jump down ledge at (1, 7).
8. Walk North to ladder at (2, 0) -> go up to 3F.
