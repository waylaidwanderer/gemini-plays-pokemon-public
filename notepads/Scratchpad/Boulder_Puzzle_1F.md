Victory Road 1F Puzzle Logic:
- DRB and DPS are floor textures. Walls are marked by thick dark lines or Blue Rubble.
- The entrance boulder is at (5, 15). It must be pushed onto the switch at (17, 13).
- TRUE SOLUTION:
  1. Stand at (5, 16) and push boulder UP to (5, 14).
  2. Walk around to (4, 14) and push boulder RIGHT to (6, 14).
  3. Continue pushing it RIGHT along the clear Y=14 corridor all the way to (17, 14).
  4. Walk to (17, 15) and push it UP onto the switch at (17, 13).
- This will remove the rock barriers blocking the path to the 2F ladder.
