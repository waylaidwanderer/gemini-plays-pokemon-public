# B2F MAIN CAVERN TO ROUTE 4 EXIT

1. **B2F Upper Corridor to South Highway:** From B2F (25,9), walk Down 2 steps to (25,11), Left 5 steps to Column 20 at (20,11).
2. **South Highway to Bottom Cavern:** Walk South down Column 20/21 across Rows 12-23 into B2F Bottom Cavern.
3. **Bottom Cavern to Fossil & Exit:** Walk South/East to Super Nerd at (24,31), defeat Super Nerd, collect Fossil, take True Exit Ladder to Route 4 East!
