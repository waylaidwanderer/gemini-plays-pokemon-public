1. **1F -> B1F:** From 1F (24,23), walk Down to (24,27), Left along Row 27 to (13,27) ladder. Warp to B1F (15,27).
2. **B1F -> B2F Main Cavern:** From B1F (15,27), walk Right along Row 27 to Column 25 (25,27), then Up Column 25 to (25,22) ladder/stairs. Warp down to B2F Main Cavern!
3. **B2F Main Cavern -> Exit:** Follow South Highway (Cols 20-21) South to Bottom Cavern, pass Super Nerd to Fossils, take True Exit Ladder to Route 4 East!
