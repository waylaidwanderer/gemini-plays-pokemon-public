# MT. MOON EXIT ROUTE & EXPLORATION LOG

- Route to Western Highway (B1F): From B1F (15,25), walk Down 3 steps to Row 28 (15,28), walk Left 4 steps along Row 28 to Column 11 (11,28), then walk North up Column 11 (Western Highway) to reach Top Corridor (Rows 2-7).
- From Top Corridor (B1F), navigate East to (21,17) ladder or (25,9) ladder to access B2F Main Cavern.
- In B2F Main Cavern, follow South Highway (Cols 20-21) to B2F Bottom Cavern, pass Super Nerd to Fossils and Mt. Moon True Exit Ladder to Route 4 East!
