# MT. MOON 1F EASTERN HIGHWAY ROUTE

1. **1F Eastern Highway Northbound:** From 1F (27,26), walk Up Column 27 to Row 14/15.
2. **Row 13 Bypass at Col 28-30:** At Row 15, walk East to Column 28-30, then North through Row 13 open gap to Row 10/11.
3. **Row 10 to Row 7 Top Corridor:** Walk North through Column 30 across Rows 8-9 to reach Row 7 Top Upper Corridor on 1F.
4. **Top Corridor to Exit:** Walk West along Row 7 to Column 17 functional ladder or Column 6 Western Chamber to exit Mt. Moon!
