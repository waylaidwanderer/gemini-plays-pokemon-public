# Post-Safari Zone Route & Progression Plan (Turn 73655)

## Immediate Goal: Warp and Retrieve Fuchsia City Freedom
1. Press 'A' on the active menu to execute BLASTOISE's DIG. This immediately warps us out of the Safari Zone, landing us outside the Fuchsia City Pokémon Center at (19, 28) on Map 0_1.

## Phase 1: Teach HM03 Surf to BLASTOISE (GEMMY)
1. **Open start menu, select ITEM.**
2. **Move cursor to HM03 (Surf), press A, and select USE.**
3. **Select GEMMY (Level 58 Blastoise) and press A.**
4. **Replace WATER GUN (redundant 40-power move).**
   - *Blastoise's Current Moves*: DIG (PP: 10), HYDRO PUMP (PP: 5), BITE (PP: 25), WATER GUN (PP: 25).
   - *Decision*: WATER GUN is completely obsolete now that we have Surf (95 power, same type) and Hydro Pump (120 power). We will keep BITE for physical/normal coverage and flinch chance, and DIG for overworld escape utility.
5. **Exact Button Sequence to teach Surf from Item menu**:
   - `Down` (move cursor to Use/teach option) -> `A` -> Select GEMMY -> `A` -> `A` (agree to replace a move) -> `Down` x 3 (move cursor to WATER GUN) -> `A` -> `A` (confirm forget/learn) -> `A` to dismiss text.

## Phase 2: Deliver Gold Teeth to the Safari Warden
1. **Warden's House Location**: Located in southeastern Fuchsia City at (16, 21) or (16, 22)? Let's walk to the Warden's House.
2. **Action**: Stand in front of the Warden at his desk inside the house, open inventory (or speak to him directly; speaking to him with the GOLD TEETH in inventory automatically triggers the exchange).
3. **Reward**: The Warden gives us **HM04 (Strength)**!
4. **Warden's House Boulder**: Once we have Strength, we can teach it to ROCKY (Geodude) or another teammate, and push the boulder inside his house to retrieve a **Rare Candy**!

## Phase 3: Next Major Milestone (Volcano Badge / Blaine)
1. Since we already have 5 Badges (Boulder, Cascade, Thunder, Marsh, Soul), the remaining Gyms are Blaine (Cinnabar Island) and Giovanni (Viridian City).
2. **Path to Cinnabar Island**:
   - Option A: Surf South from Pallet Town along Route 21. This is extremely short and has no complex dungeon!
   - Option B: Surf South from Fuchsia City along Route 19/20, which goes through Seafoam Islands. This is a complex dungeon requiring Strength puzzles.
   - *Decision*: Option A (Pallet Town Route 21) is much faster and bypasses Seafoam Islands entirely! We can simply FLY to Pallet Town, walk to the southern water edge, and Surf South directly to Cinnabar Island! This is extremely efficient and safe.
