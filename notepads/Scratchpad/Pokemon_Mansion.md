Pokemon Mansion Navigation & Discoveries:

MECHANICS (REVISED):
Shutters operate on inverted logic.
- State A (Default):
  - x=13 Dark Grey: OPEN
  - x=9 Dark Grey: CLOSED
  - (16, 16) Yellow: CLOSED
- State B (Toggled by 1F 18,25 switch):
  - x=13 Dark Grey: CLOSED
  - x=9 Dark Grey: OPEN
  - (16, 16) Yellow: OPEN

ROUTE TO 1F MAIN STAIRS (Requires State B):
1. Enter Mansion (State A). You are blocked going North at y=17.
2. Walk East along y=22 to x=18.
3. Walk South to (18, 25) and press Switch -> State B.
4. Walk North to y=18, West to x=16.
5. Walk North through open Yellow shutter at (16, 16) to y=10.
6. Walk West along y=10 through open Dark Grey shutter at x=9.
7. Reach Main Stairs at 1F (5, 10).

STAIRS & WARPS SUMMARY:
- 1F (5, 10) <-> 2F (5, 10) (Main Stairs)
- 2F (7, 10) -> 1F (7, 10) (Drop Hole)
