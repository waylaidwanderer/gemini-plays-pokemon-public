Pokemon Mansion Escape & Re-entry Plan:

THE DEADLOCK:
- B1F Center is trapped in State B due to closed Dark Grey Shutters at x=11 and x=24.
- B1F East is blocked by rubble at y=24..27, so x=24 shutters must be OPEN to cross.
- To reach B1F East, the mansion MUST be in State A (Dark Grey OPEN).

ESCAPE AND ROUTE PLAN:
1. Take stairs at B1F (21, 23) UP to 1F East.
2. In State B, 1F East is trapped by closed shutters at x=20. Exit Mansion South to Cinnabar Island.
3. Re-enter Mansion via Main Entrance.
4. Go to switch at 1F (2, 5) and toggle to State A (Dark Grey OPEN).
5. Take main stairs at 1F (5, 10) DOWN to B1F.
6. In State A, walk East on B1F. Dark Grey shutters at x=11 and x=24 are OPEN.
7. Arrive in B1F East (x=25). Find a switch there to toggle to State B, opening the Yellow Shutters to the Secret Key!
