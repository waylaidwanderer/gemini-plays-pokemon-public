Pokemon Mansion Navigation & Discoveries:

MECHANICS (DEFINITIVE):
- Exiting to Cinnabar Island resets everything to State A.
- Shutter Logic: Dark Grey and Yellow shutters are INVERTED relative to each other.
- State A (Default):
  - x=9 Dark Grey Shutters: OPEN
  - x=13 Dark Grey Shutters: CLOSED
  - Yellow Shutters (e.g., 1F 16,16; 2F 18,8): CLOSED
- State B (Toggled by 1F 18,25 switch):
  - x=9 Dark Grey Shutters: CLOSED
  - x=13 Dark Grey Shutters: OPEN
  - Yellow Shutters: OPEN

ROUTE TO 1F MAIN STAIRS (Requires State A):
1. Enter Mansion at (5, 26) or from any location where y=22 is accessible.
2. Walk East along y=22 to x=10.
3. Walk North along x=10 to y=10.
4. Walk West through the OPEN dark grey shutter at (9, 10).
5. Reach Main Stairs at 1F (5, 10).

Note: The pink floor at (13, 22) is ALWAYS open. y=22 is a clear corridor across the whole mansion. You cannot get trapped.
