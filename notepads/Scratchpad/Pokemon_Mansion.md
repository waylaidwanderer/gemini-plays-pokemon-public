Pokemon Mansion Navigation & Discoveries:

MECHANICS (DEFINITIVE):
- Exiting to Cinnabar Island resets everything to State A.
- Shutter Logic: Dark Grey and Yellow shutters are INVERTED relative to each other.
- State A (Default):
  - (9, 10) Dark Grey Shutter: OPEN
  - x=13 Dark Grey Shutters: CLOSED
  - Yellow Shutters (e.g., 1F 16,16; 2F 18,8): CLOSED
- State B (Toggled by 1F 18,25 switch):
  - (9, 10) Dark Grey Shutter: CLOSED
  - x=13 Dark Grey Shutters: OPEN
  - Yellow Shutters: OPEN

ROUTE TO 1F MAIN STAIRS (Requires State A):
1. Enter Mansion at (5, 26).
2. Walk North to y=22.
3. Walk East along y=22 to x=10.
4. Walk North along x=10 to y=10.
5. Walk West through the OPEN dark grey shutter at (9, 10) to (8, 10).
6. Walk Down to (8, 12).
7. Walk West to (5, 12).
8. Walk North to Main Stairs at 1F (5, 10).

ESCAPE FROM EAST WING:
Walk along y=22 to x=5, then walk South out the front door. (y=22 is clear across the entire floor)
