Pokemon Mansion 1F Mechanics:
- STATE A: Yellow Shutters CLOSED. Dark Grey Shutters OPEN.
- STATE B: Yellow Shutters OPEN. Dark Grey Shutters CLOSED.

Master Route to 2F Stairs at (5, 10):
1. The Mansion MUST be in STATE B (Yellow Shutters OPEN).
2. The main entrance hallway leads North, passing a wall gap at (18, 21).
3. In STATE B, the Yellow Shutter at (16, 16) is OPEN.
4. Pass through (16, 16) to reach the middle section of the mansion.
5. From the middle section (around y=15), walk WEST towards x=5.
6. Once at x=5, find the stairs at (5, 10).

Notes:
- The West Wing South is a dead end blocked by desks at y=17.
- The Central Hallway is a dead end blocked by a solid wall at y=8.
- The switch at 1F (18, 25) controls the global state. (Base is the interactable part).

B1F Mechanics:
- B1F has Dark Grey Shutters at x=11 and x=24. In State B, they are closed and block progress.
- To explore B1F past x=11, the global state MUST be STATE A.
