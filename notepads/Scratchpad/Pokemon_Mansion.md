PUZZLE SOLUTION TO EXPLORE B1F NORTH:
1. Reach 3F.
2. Toggle a switch to STATE A.
3. Find a drop hole (e.g. 19,14) that drops you to 1F East Wing (landing at y < 16).
4. Walk South in 1F East Wing to B1F stairs at (23, 22) and descend.
5. On B1F, the Dark Grey Shutter at (13, 11) will be OPEN!

KNOWN CONSTRAINTS:
- 1F (18, 17) and (19, 17) are solid Statue Bases. You cannot walk North through them.
- Thus, the switch at 1F (18, 25) traps you south of y=16 in State A.
- 1F (2, 5) switch traps you north of y=8 in State A.
