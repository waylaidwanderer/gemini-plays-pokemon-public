Pokemon Mansion Switches & Routing:
- Switch Statue at 3F (10, 4) toggles YELLOW shutters globally.
- State A: 3F (15, 6)/(15, 7) OPEN, 3F (15, 10)/(15, 11) CLOSED. 1F (16, 7) CLOSED. 2F (9, 4) OPEN.
- State B: 3F (15, 6)/(15, 7) CLOSED, 3F (15, 10)/(15, 11) OPEN. 1F (16, 7) OPEN. 2F (9, 4) CLOSED.

- Floor Identification by Stairs (PROVEN):
  - 1F: Pink floor. Stairs UP to 2F at (5, 10). Stairs DOWN to B1F at (7, 10). (x=9 is blocked by dark grey shutters).
  - 2F: Red carpet in center. Stairs DOWN to 1F at (5, 10). Stairs UP to 3F at (7, 10).
  - 3F: Pink floor. Stairs DOWN to 2F at (7, 10). Switch at (10, 4). Drop hole at (19, 14).
  - B1F: Brown/Pink floor. Stairs UP to 1F at (7, 10). Diary at (6, 12). NO STAIRS at (5, 10).

- WARP MECHANIC: You MUST step onto the stairs from below (y=11) facing UP. Stepping from the side does NOT warp you.
