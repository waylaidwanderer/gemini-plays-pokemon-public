Pokemon Mansion Navigation & Discoveries:

MECHANICS (DEFINITIVE):
- Exiting to Cinnabar Island resets everything to State A.
- State A (Default):
  - x=9 Dark Grey Shutters (y=9 to y=16): OPEN
  - x=13 Dark Grey Shutters (y=17 to y=21): OPEN
  - (16, 16) Yellow Shutter: CLOSED
- State B (Toggled by 1F 18,25 switch):
  - x=9 Dark Grey Shutters (y=9 to y=16): CLOSED
  - x=13 Dark Grey Shutters (y=17 to y=21): CLOSED
  - (16, 16) Yellow Shutter: OPEN

ROUTE TO 1F MAIN STAIRS (Requires State A):
1. Enter Mansion at (5, 26).
2. Walk North to y=22. (y=17 is a solid wall blocking direct North)
3. Walk East along y=22 to x=10.
4. Walk North along x=10 to y=10. (x=10 is clear of y=17 wall)
5. Walk West through the OPEN dark grey shutter at (9, 10).
6. Reach Main Stairs at 1F (5, 10).
DO NOT toggle any switches on 1F to reach the Main Stairs.

CURRENT GOAL: Toggle switch at (18, 25) to return to State A, then go to 1F Main Stairs.
