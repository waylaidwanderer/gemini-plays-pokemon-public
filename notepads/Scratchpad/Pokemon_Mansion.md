Pokemon Mansion 1F Routing

FACTS:
- 1F East Wing is completely blocked to the North by a solid wall at y=8. We cannot use the x=16 corridor to reach the North.
- 1F West Wing is blocked by a solid wall at y=17.
- Switch at (18, 25) toggles shutters.
- State A: Dark Grey OPEN, Yellow CLOSED.
- State B: Dark Grey CLOSED, Yellow OPEN.
- The crossing from East to West at x=13 has Dark Grey Shutters at y=17, 18, 19, 20.
- Thus, we MUST be in State A to cross to the West side of x=13.

THE DEFINITIVE PATH TO MAIN STAIRS (5, 10):
1. Must be in STATE A. (Check if Dark Grey shutters at 13,20 are open. If closed, toggle switch at 18,25).
2. Walk West through the open Dark Grey Shutter at (13, 20) [or 19, 18, 17] to reach x=11.
3. Walk North up the x=11 corridor to y=11.
4. Walk West along y=11. This requires passing through (9, 11), which is a Dark Grey Shutter (Open in State A).
5. Continue West to x=5, then walk North to the stairs at (5, 10).
