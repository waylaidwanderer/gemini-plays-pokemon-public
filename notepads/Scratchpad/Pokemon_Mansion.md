Master Route to Secret Key:
1. From 1F South, ensure state is B (Yellow Open, Dark Grey Closed) via switch at (18, 25).
2. Walk North through open Yellow Shutter at (16, 16) to reach the Central Hub (y=15).
3. Walk North to y=7 and cross West through the open Yellow Shutter at (16, 7) to enter the West Wing (Entrance Hallway).
4. In West Wing, walk to (2, 6) and hit the switch at (2, 5) to toggle to State A.
5. State A opens the Dark Grey Shutter at y=8. Walk South to the stairs at (5, 10) and ascend to 2F.
6. Explore 2F/3F for drop holes to the Secret Key.
