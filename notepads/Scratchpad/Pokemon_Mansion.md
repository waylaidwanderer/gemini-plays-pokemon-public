Pokemon Mansion Navigation & Discoveries:

MECHANICS (DEFINITIVE):
- Exiting to Cinnabar Island resets everything to State A.
- "A secret switch! Press it?" -> default is NO ("Not quite yet!"). Must press Up -> A for YES ("Who wouldn't?").
- **CRITICAL DISCOVERY**: OPEN yellow shutters STILL LOOK SOLID visually (vertical yellow/orange lines), but you can walk through them!
- State A (Default):
  - Yellow Shutters (e.g. 16,16, 13,22..23, 9,6/9,7): CLOSED (Solid)
- State B (Toggled by switch):
  - Yellow Shutters (e.g. 16,16, 13,22..23, 9,6/9,7): OPEN (Walkable, but visually look exactly the same as closed!)

PERMANENT WALLS:
- All dark grey tiles with white tracks (e.g. x=9 y=8..21, x=13 y=16..21) are PERMANENT WALLS. They NEVER open.
- y=8 solid wall (x=10 to x=15) blocking North/South in the Middle Section.
- y=17 solid wall (x=1 to x=8) blocking North/South in the West Wing.

ROUTE TO 1F MAIN STAIRS (5, 10):
To reach the stairs from the entrance, State B is REQUIRED so you can cross the Yellow Shutters!
1. From Entrance (West Wing South), walk East along y=27 to the East Wing.
2. Walk North in East Wing to switch at (18, 25). Toggle switch to State B (YES: "Who wouldn't?").
3. Walk North to (14, 22).
4. Cross West through OPEN Yellow Shutter at (13, 22) to reach Middle Section (12, 22).
5. Walk North to (10, 7).
6. Cross West through OPEN Yellow Shutter at (9, 7) to reach West Wing North (8, 7).
7. Walk South to (8, 12), West to (5, 12), North to stairs at (5, 10).
