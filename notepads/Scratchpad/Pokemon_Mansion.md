- State B: 3F (15, 6)/(15, 7) CLOSED, 3F (15, 10)/(15, 11) OPEN. 1F (16, 7) OPEN. 2F (9, 4) CLOSED.

  - 2F: Red carpet in center, checkered floors elsewhere. Stairs at (5, 10) DOWN. Stairs to 3F at (7, 10) and (6, 1).
  - 3F: Switch at (10, 4). Drop hole at (19, 14).
  - B1F: Stairs at (7, 10) UP only. Diary at (6, 12).

- WARP MECHANIC: Stepping onto a staircase tile triggers a warp regardless of the direction of entry.

- Drop from 3F (16, 14) leads to 1F at (16, 14).
- 1F has stairs DOWN at (23, 21) leading to B1F (21, 23).
- B1F South and West are dead ends. The statues here are inactive.

- ESCAPE ROUTE FROM B1F EAST (Dead End):
  - B1F East (accessed from 1F 23,21) has NO connection to B1F West.
  - To escape, return to stairs at B1F (21, 23).
  - Path: Navigate to x=13, y=17. Walk South to y=25, East to x=21, North to (21, 23).
  - You will arrive at 1F SE.

- PROVEN ROUTE TO B1F WEST WING:
  - 1F SE is isolated from the rest of 1F due to a solid wall at y=8.
  - The switch at 1F (18, 24) toggles the global state (identical to 3F 10,4).
  - To escape 1F SE to the West Wing, you MUST go via B1F.
  - Take the stairs at 1F (23, 21) DOWN to B1F (21, 23).
  - On B1F, navigate West to the shutter at x=11.
  - If the shutter is OPEN: Walk West to x=10, North, then West to stairs at (7, 10) to reach 1F West Wing.
  - If the shutter is CLOSED: Return to 1F, press the switch at (18, 24), return to B1F, and proceed West.
