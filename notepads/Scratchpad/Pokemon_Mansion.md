# Pokemon Mansion Verified Routing & Rules
- Yellow Shutters are state-dependent.
  - Horizontal yellow shutters (e.g. 16,16) are OPEN in State B, CLOSED in State A.
  - Vertical yellow shutters (e.g. 13,22) are CLOSED in State B, OPEN in State A.
- Dark grey blocks with white tracks are SHUTTERS.
  - Vertical dark grey shutters (e.g. 9,9) are CLOSED in State B, OPEN in State A.

1. We are in State B. Return to 1F stairs (23, 22).
2. Walk to y=15 corridor. Walk West to x=16.
3. Walk South through OPEN horizontal yellow shutter at (16, 16) to y=26.
4. Walk Right to (18, 26). Face Up and press switch to toggle to State A.
5. In State A, (16, 16) closes, but vertical yellow shutter at (13, 22) OPENS.
6. Walk West to x=14, North to y=22, West through (13, 22) to x=12.
7. Walk North to y=15 corridor, West to x=9.
