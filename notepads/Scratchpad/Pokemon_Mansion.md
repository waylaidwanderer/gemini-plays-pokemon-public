Pokemon Mansion 1F Mechanics:
- STATE A: Yellow Shutters CLOSED. Dark Grey Shutters OPEN.
- STATE B: Yellow Shutters OPEN. Dark Grey Shutters CLOSED.

Master Route to 2F Stairs at (5, 10):
1. The Mansion MUST be in STATE B to cross from East Wing to West Wing at x=13.
2. Toggle switch at (18, 25) to STATE B (Yellow OPEN).
3. Walk West through the OPEN Yellow Shutter at (13, 22) into West Wing.
4. Walk North to the switch at (2, 5).
5. Toggle switch at (2, 5) to STATE A (Dark Grey OPEN).
6. Walk East/South to the Dark Grey Shutters at x=9. They are now OPEN.
7. Walk West through x=9 to reach x=5.
8. Walk North to the 2F stairs at (5, 10).

B1F Mechanics:
- B1F has Dark Grey Shutters at x=11 and x=24.
- In State A, they are OPEN, allowing exploration of B1F West!
- In State B, they are CLOSED.
