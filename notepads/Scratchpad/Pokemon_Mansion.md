# Pokemon Mansion Verified Routing & Rules
- Dark Grey blocks with white tracks (e.g., x=9 on 1F, x=24 on B1F) are PERMANENT SOLID WALLS. They never open.
- Yellow Shutters are state-dependent. They open and close when switches are toggled.
- Not all Yellow Shutters are synced. Some are open in State A, some open in State B.

## MANSION 1F:
1. The Entrance Hallway (x=1 to x=8) is COMPLETELY ISOLATED from the rest of 1F by a permanent column of dark grey walls at x=9.
2. There is absolutely NO WAY to cross between the Entrance Hallway and the rest of 1F.
3. To reach the 2F stairs at (5, 10), you MUST exit the mansion (e.g., via B1F 26,27) and re-enter from Cinnabar Island at (6, 3).
4. The switch at (18, 25) controls global shutter states.

## MANSION B1F:
1. The B1F exit is at (26, 27).
2. To reach the B1F exit from the B1F stairs at (21, 23), you must be in State B.
3. In State B, the yellow shutters at (20, 17) and (21, 17) are OPEN. Walk North from the stairs to y=16.
4. Walk East along y=16 to the pink floor at x=25.
5. Walk South along x=25 to the exit at (26, 27).

## MANSION 2F:
1. Path from 2F South to 2F North (stairs at 6, 1) requires walking around the y=9 wall via the x=10 corridor.
2. Then pass West through the vertical yellow shutters at (9, 4)/(9, 5). These shutters must be OPEN (State B) to pass.

## MASTER PLAN V31 (The Ultimate Route to 3F):
1. 1F: Ensure State B is active. (Toggle switch at 18,25 if needed).
2. 1F -> B1F: Take stairs at (23, 22) down to B1F.
3. B1F: Walk North through open yellow shutter at (21, 17), East to x=25, South to exit at (26, 27).
4. Cinnabar Island: Walk to (6, 3) and enter Mansion.
5. 1F (Entrance Hallway): Walk North to stairs at (5, 10).
6. 2F: State B is active, so yellow shutters at (9, 4)/(9, 5) are open! Walk to stairs at (6, 1).
7. 3F: Reached! Explore for drop hole to B1F West.
