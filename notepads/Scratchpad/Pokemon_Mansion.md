# Pokemon Mansion Verified Routing
- The 1F East and West wings connect freely at y=18 and y=19. The x=11 wall does not block y=18/y=19.
- To exit the Mansion from 1F East Wing, navigate to y=18 or y=19, cross West to x=5, and walk South to the exit at (5, 27).
- If trapped behind closed shutters at x=20 in the South-East, walk North to y=15, cross West to x=18, then walk South to y=18 to reach the crossing.
- After escaping and resetting to State A, we will climb back to 3F and drop down the correct hole at (15, 14) to reach B1F North-West, where the Secret Key is located.
