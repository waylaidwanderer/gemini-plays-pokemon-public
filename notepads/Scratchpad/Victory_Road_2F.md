- EMPIRICAL FACTS (Victory Road 2F):
  - CRITICAL NAV RULE: GameState provides HAT coordinates (X, Y). FEET are at (X, Y+1).
  - TILE LEVELS: Dark Red Blocky = Level 1 (Low). DP Speckled = Level 2 (High).
  - 1-WAY LEDGES: You can jump East from Level 2 (X=1) to Level 1 (X=2). There are also South-facing ledges at Y=9 and Y=11 in the X=2 corridor!
  - 2F West and East are DISCONNECTED. All paths East are blocked by Level 1 Blue Rubble or Level 2 dropoffs at X=8.
  - To cross to 2F East, we MUST solve the 2F boulder puzzle to open the barrier at X=8.
  - 2F BOULDER PUZZLE SOLUTION:
    1. Stand at Feet(4,13). Push boulder Down to (4,15), stepping to (4,14).
    2. Walk to (5,14) and Down to (5,15).
    3. Push boulder Left to (1,15).
    4. Walk to (1,14) and push boulder Down onto the switch at (1,16).
- STRATEGY UPDATE: The 3F ladder at 2F (1,1) is NOT accessible from the South due to a Level 2 wall at Y=4. It is the DROP POINT from 3F West.
- We must cross to 2F East via the Level 2 bridge at Y=9 (accessed via stairs at 5,10), ascend to 3F East, and push a boulder to open the passage to 3F West.
