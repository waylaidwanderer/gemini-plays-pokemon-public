# Erika Rechallenge Plan & Defeat Analysis

## Analysis of Defeat on Turn 13459:
1. **Lack of Status Cures:** We entered the Gym with zero Awakenings or Full Heals. Erika's team relies heavily on Sleep Powder and Poison Powder. Once SHELLBY (Blastoise) was put to sleep, she was completely paralyzed and vulnerable to Vileplume's Petal Dance and Mega Drain.
2. **Lack of HP Recovery:** We only had a single Soda Pop, which we used on SHELLBY. We had no backup healing items to sustain our team once SHELLBY took heavy damage.
3. **Fodder Efficacy:** Our tactical switch-ins and fodder sacrifices (GUSTY, NIBBLES) and TRUFFLE's DIG stall were executed perfectly and bought many turns, but were ultimately unsustainable due to the lack of status/healing items.

## Step-by-Step Rechallenge Plan:
1. **White-out Recovery:** After whiting out, we will wake up in the Celadon Pokémon Center. Our team will be fully healed.
2. **Department Store Shopping:**
   - Walk west to the Celadon Department Store (mapped at Row 10/11 on the west side of the city).
   - Take the stairs/elevator to the upper floors to buy:
     - 5-10 Awakenings (to cure Sleep mid-battle).
     - 5-10 Full Heals or Antidotes (to cure Poison/Paralysis).
     - 5-10 Super Potions or Soda Pops (for HP recovery).
3. **Re-engage Erika:**
   - Walk back to the Celadon Gym (12, 27).
   - Use Cut on the bush at (35, 32) to enter the narrow grass corridor.
   - Enter the Gym, cut the internal bushes, and challenge Erika.
   - Use SHELLBY (Blastoise) to sweep, using Awakenings immediately if she is put to sleep.
