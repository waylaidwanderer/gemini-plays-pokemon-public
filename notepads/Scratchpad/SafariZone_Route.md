# Safari Zone - Golden Speedrun Route & Navigation Index

## 🌍 The Flawless Golden Route
1. **Safari Zone Center to Area 1 (East) (27 steps)**
   - Start at Entrance (15, 25)
   - Walk UP to (15, 21), RIGHT to Column 28, UP to Row 11, RIGHT to Column 30 to transition.
2. **Area 1 (East) to Area 2 (North) (90 steps)**
   - Walk DOWN to Row 24 to bypass Rhydon statues.
   - Walk to Southern Plateau, walk west, descend.
   - Walk north to Northern Plateau, walk east, descend.
   - Walk UP and LEFT along the bypass corridor to transition at (0, 5).
3. **Area 2 (North) to Area 3 (West) (35 steps)**
   - Warp in at (39, 31). Walk Left to Column 22, Down to Row 33, Left to Column 9, and Down to warp.
