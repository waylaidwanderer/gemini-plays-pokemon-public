# Safari Zone Fresh Run - Optimized Speedrun Route

## Part 1: Safari Zone Center (Area 0)
1. Start at (18, 25) in Center.
2. Walk North 3 steps to (18, 22).
3. Walk East 11 steps to (29, 22).
4. Walk East 1 step to enter Area 1 (East) at (0, 22).
Total steps for Part 1: 15 steps.

## Part 2: Safari Zone Area 1 (East)
1. Enter at (0, 22).
2. Walk East 12 steps to (12, 22).
3. Walk Up 2 steps to climb the western plateau stairs:
   - Up to (12, 21) (staircase)
   - Up to (12, 20) (plateau)
4. Walk North 9 steps to (12, 11) (or until the northern edge is reached).
5. Walk Down 1 step to descend the northern stairs to row 10 (ground).
6. From row 10 (ground), walk West to column 0 to transition to Area 2 (North) at (0, 5)!
Total steps for Part 2: ~35-40 steps.

## Part 3: Safari Zone Area 2 (North)
1. Enter Area 2. We need to reach the southern ground corridor to go to Area 3 (West).
2. Follow the "Gold-Standard Speedrun Route to Area 3 (West)":
   - Walk East to column 35 (around Rest House 2).
   - Walk Down column 35 to row 13.
   - Walk West and climb UP the eastern plateau stairs at (32, 13) or (33, 13).
   - Walk south and west along the plateau to reach the southern cliffside.
   - Walk down the stairs or jump down the south-facing ledges to land on the southern ground level (rows 25-35).
   - Walk West along the southern grass corridor to the bottom-left corner of Area 2 (columns 0-4, rows 30-35) to transition to Area 3 (West).
