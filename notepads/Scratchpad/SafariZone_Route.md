# Safari Zone Speedrun Route

## 1. Safari Zone Center (31 steps)
- Start at spawn `(15, 25)` facing UP.
- Walk Left 1 step to `(14, 25)`.
- Walk Up 3 steps to `(14, 22)`.
- Walk Right 1 step to `(15, 22)`.
- Walk Up 5 steps to `(15, 17)`.
- Walk Right 7 steps to `(22, 17)` (ground level).
- Walk Up 7 steps to `(22, 10)` (bypassing center pond on the east side).
- Walk Right 7 steps to `(29, 10)` -> transition to Area 1 (East) at `(0, 22)`.

## 2. Safari Zone Area 1 (East) (Verified Path)
- Enter at `(0, 22)` facing RIGHT.
- Walk Right 5 steps to `(5, 22)`.
- **Bypass Statue at (6, 22):** Walk Left 1 step to `(4, 22)`, Down 2 steps to `(4, 24)`, Right 4 steps to `(8, 24)`, Up 2 steps to `(8, 22)`, Right 12 steps along row 22 to `(20, 22)`.
  *Note: If coming from west, can walk east along row 26/27/28 to column 20 directly to reach southern plateau stairs.*
- **Access Southern Plateau:** Climb stairs at `(20, 21)` to `(20, 20)`. Walk Left 8 steps on the plateau to `(12, 20)`. Descend stairs at `(12, 21)` to ground level at `(12, 22)`.
- **Western Ground Corridor:** Walk Left 4 steps to `(8, 22)`. Walk Up 14 steps along column 8 to `(8, 8)`.
- **Northern Plateau Crossing (East-Bound):**
  - Walk Right 4 steps from `(8, 8)` to `(12, 8)`.
  - Walk Up 2 steps onto the plateau at `(12, 6)` using stairs at `(12, 7)`.
  - Walk Right 5 steps along row 6 to `(17, 6)`.
  - Walk Down 2 steps to descend stairs at `(17, 7)` to `(17, 8)` (ground).
- **Corridor Bypass to Northwest Transition:**
  - Walk Right 3 steps to `(20, 8)` (bypassing column 19 rocky cliff wall at row 8).
  - Walk Up 6 steps to row 2 on column 20: `(20, 2)`.
  - Walk Left 12 steps along row 2 to column 8: `(8, 2)`.
  - Walk Down 3 steps to row 5 on column 8: `(8, 5)`.
  - Walk Left 8 steps along row 5 to column 0: `(0, 5)` -> transition to Area 2 (North) at `(39, 31)`.
