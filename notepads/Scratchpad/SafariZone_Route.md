# Safari Zone - Ultimate Speedrun Route

This document contains the verified, optimal step-by-step route from the Safari Zone Gatehouse to the Secret House in Area 3 (West) to obtain HM03 (Surf), and subsequently the Gold Teeth.

---

## 1. Safari Zone Center to Area 1 (East)
- Start in Safari Zone Center at `(15, 25)` facing UP.
- Walk **Right 14 steps** to `(29, 25)` (open grass).
- Walk **Up 15 steps** to `(29, 10)` (open/tall grass).
- Walk **Right 1 step** to transition to Area 1 (East) at `(0, 22)`.

## 2. Area 1 (East) to Area 2 (North)
- Enter Area 1 (East) at `(0, 22)` facing RIGHT.
- Walk **Right 20 steps** to `(20, 22)`.
- Walk **Up 2 steps** to climb stairs at `(20, 21)` onto the southern plateau at `(20, 20)`.
- Walk **Left 8 steps** along the plateau to `(12, 20)`.
- Walk **Down 2 steps** to descend stairs at `(12, 21)` to ground level at `(12, 22)`.
- Walk **Up 14 steps** to row 8: `(12, 8)`.
- Walk **Up 2 steps** to climb stairs at `(12, 7)` onto the northern plateau at `(12, 6)`.
- Walk **Right 5 steps** along the plateau to `(17, 6)`.
- Walk **Down 2 steps** to descend stairs at `(17, 7)` to northeastern ground level at `(17, 8)`.
- Walk **Right 3 steps** to `(20, 8)`.
- Walk **Up 5 steps** past the tree graphic to row 3: `(20, 3)`.
- Walk **Left 20 steps** along row 3 to `(0, 3)`.
- Walk **Down 2 steps** to `(0, 5)` to transition to Area 2 (North) at `(39, 31)`.

## 3. Area 2 (North) to Area 3 (West)
- Enter Area 2 (North) at `(39, 31)` facing LEFT.
- Walk **Left 17 steps** to `(22, 31)`.
- Walk **Up 8 steps** to `(22, 23)` (stairs).
- Walk **Up 1 step** to climb stairs onto the Western Southern Plateau at `(22, 22)`.
- Walk **Left 4 steps** to `(18, 22)`.
- Walk **Down 5 steps** to `(18, 27)`.
- Walk **Left 2 steps** to `(16, 27)` (descending stairs).
- Walk **Down 2 steps** to descend stairs to ground level at `(16, 28)`.
- Walk **Left 12 steps** along row 28 to `(4, 28)`.
- Walk **Down 7 steps** to `(4, 35)` to transition to Area 3 (West) at `(26, 0)`.

## 4. Area 3 (West) to Rest House 3
- Enter Area 3 (West) at `(26, 0)` facing DOWN.
- Walk **Left 1 step** to `(25, 0)`.
- Walk **Down 18 steps** to `(25, 18)`.
- Walk **Left 4 steps** to `(21, 18)`.
- Walk **Up 2 steps** to climb stairs at `(21, 17)` onto plateau at `(21, 16)`.
- Walk **Left 15 steps** along row 16 to `(6, 16)`.
- Walk **Down 4 steps** to descend stairs at `(6, 19)` to west ground level at `(6, 20)`.
- Walk **Left 5 steps** to `(1, 20)`.
- Walk **Up 6 steps** to `(1, 14)`.
- Walk **Right 10 steps** to `(11, 14)`.
- Walk **Up 3 steps** to enter Rest House 3 at `(11, 11)`.
- Inside Rest House 3, walk Up to the clerk at `(6, 3)` or `(4, 3)` to obtain **HM03 (Surf)**!
