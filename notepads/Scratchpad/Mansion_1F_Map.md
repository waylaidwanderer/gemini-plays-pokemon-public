Mansion Routing:
- B1F North West is blocked by a permanent dark grey wall at x=11 (not a shutter). The 3F drop hole to B1F West is a DEAD END.
- To get the Secret Key: We must reach B1F North East via the B1F stairs at (21, 23). This requires State B to open the B1F yellow shutters at y=17.
- The 1F switch at (18, 25) is a TRAP. Toggling to State B traps you in 1F South Center.
- 1F is PARTITIONED! West Wing (Entrance, 2F stairs) is physically separated from Center/East Wings by permanent walls at x=9 and y=8.
- ESCAPE PLAN: Go to B1F stairs at 1F (23, 22). Descend to B1F. Walk to the South-East corner (26, 27) and exit to Cinnabar Island. Re-enter the front door to reach 1F West Wing.
- From West Wing, ascend to 2F/3F, toggle to State B, and find a drop hole that lands in 1F East so we can reach the B1F stairs in State B!
