Mansion 1F Layout & Routing:
- West Wing (x=1 to 8) is separated from Central Hub by x=9 wall.
- The x=9 wall is SOLID from y=8 to y=17.
- Solid wall from y=8 down to at least y=16 at x=9.
- Central Hub to South Switch path: y=16 has Statues and Yellow Shutters. In State A (Yellow Closed), y=16 is impassable. However, x=10/11/12 is an OPEN corridor bypassing y=16 completely. You can always reach the South Switch (18, 25) from the North by walking down x=12 to y=22, then Right to x=18.
