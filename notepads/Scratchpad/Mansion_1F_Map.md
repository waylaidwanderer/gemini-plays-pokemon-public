Mansion Routing:
- B1F North West is blocked by a permanent dark grey wall at x=11. The 3F drop hole to B1F West is a DEAD END.
- To get the Secret Key: We must reach B1F East via the B1F stairs at (21, 23) while in State B (yellow shutters at B1F y=17 OPEN).
- The 1F switch at (18, 25) is a TRAP. Toggling to State B closes y=16 yellow shutters, trapping you in 1F South Center.
- ESCAPE PLAN (from 1F South): In State A, the yellow shutters at 1F (16,16) and 1F East Wing (24,13) are BOTH OPEN. Walk East to x=24, then North to y=7, then West to reach the West Wing and the 2F stairs.
- 3F DROP HOLE PLAN: Ascend to 3F. Toggle switch to State B (opens 3F East Wing). Find a drop hole in 3F East Wing that lands in 1F East or B1F East to reach the Secret Key!
