Mansion 1F Layout & Routing:
- The B1F stairs are a DEAD END. To reach the Secret Key, we must find a drop hole from 2F or 3F.
- To reach 2F, we must go to the Entrance Hallway stairs at (5, 10).
- The Entrance Hallway connects to the Central Hub via a passage at x=16/17.
- This passage has a Yellow Shutter at (16, 7)/(17, 7).
- In State B (Yellow OPEN), the path from the South Switch (18, 25) to the 2F stairs (5, 10) is CLEAR:
  1. Walk Left to x=14, Up to y=19 (bypassing desk at y=20).
  2. Right to x=16, Up through open Yellow Shutter at (16, 16).
  3. Continue Up x=16 through open Yellow Shutter at (16, 7) to y=7.
  4. Walk Left to x=5, Down to stairs at (5, 10).
