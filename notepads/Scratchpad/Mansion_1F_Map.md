Mansion 1F Layout & Routing:
- West Wing (x=1 to 8) is separated from Central Hub by x=9 wall.
- The x=9 wall is SOLID from y=8 to y=17.
- Solid wall from y=8 down to at least y=16 at x=9.
- Central Hub to South Switch path: At y=16, x=14/15 and x=18/19 are statues. x=16/17 are Yellow Shutters. You MUST pass through the Yellow Shutter at x=16/17 (Open in State B) to reach the South Switch.
