Mansion 1F Layout & Routing:
- The West Wing (x=1 to x=8) is separated from the Central Hub by an x=9 wall.
- The North and South halves of the Mansion are separated by a y=16/17 barrier.
- To reach the 2F Stairs at (5, 10), you MUST cross the x=9 wall NORTH of y=16 (e.g., at y=9 or y=7).
- To cross the x=9 wall North of y=16, the Mansion MUST be in State B (Shutters at x=9 are OPEN).

HOW TO REACH 2F STAIRS FROM CURRENT POSITION (State A, North of y=16):
1. Go South to the switch via the x=12 bypass:
   - Walk Left to x=12. Walk Down to y=22.
   - Walk Right through the OPEN Yellow Shutter at (13, 22) into Central Hub.
   - Walk to the switch at (18, 26).
2. Toggle switch to State B.
3. Go North to West Wing via Central Hub:
   - Walk Up through the OPEN Yellow Shutter at (17, 16) to y=14.
   - Walk Left to x=10, Up to y=9.
   - Walk Left through OPEN shutters at x=9 to reach x=5.
   - Walk Down to stairs at (5, 10).
