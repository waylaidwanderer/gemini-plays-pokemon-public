B1F Secret Key & True Escape Plan:
1. Hit Master Switch at B1F (20,3) to enter State 2.
2. Walk West to B1F NW (9,6) and collect the Secret Key!
3. Return to Master Switch (20,3) and hit it to enter State 1.
4. In State 1, East Corridor (26,17) is OPEN. Walk South down East Corridor to B1F stairs (23,22).
5. From B1F stairs, navigate to South Switch at (18,25). (In State 1, shutter 13,22 is OPEN, making this easy).
6. Hit South Switch to enter State 2!
7. Return to B1F stairs (23,22) and ascend to 1F Center South (21,23).
8. In State 2, 1F shutter at (20,17) is OPEN. Walk North through it.
9. Walk West through 1F shutter (16,7) (also OPEN in State 2) to the Entrance Hall!
10. Exit Mansion!
