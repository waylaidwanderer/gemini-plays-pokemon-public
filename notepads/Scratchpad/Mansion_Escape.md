1F Center South Escape Plan:
1. 1F Center South is a trap in State 1 (shutter at 20,17 is closed).
2. Walk to 1F stairs at (21, 23) via x=13 (Left to 13,14 -> Down to 13,24 -> Right to 21,24 -> Up to 21,23).
3. Go down to B1F.
4. Walk to South Switch at B1F (18, 25) and press A (enters State 2).
5. Return to 1F via stairs.
6. In State 2, shutter at (20, 17) is OPEN. Walk North through it into East Wing North.
7. Walk West through shutter at (16, 7) (also OPEN in State 2) into West Wing.
8. Walk South to the mansion exit.
