- **Objective:** Defeat Lt. Surge's Electric-type team safely. We need ROCKY (GEODUDE) to be Level 15+ so its Rock/Ground typing grants full immunity to Electric moves. BIRBIE (PIDGEY) should also be raised to Level 15 to evolve into Pidgeotto.
- **Methodology:** 
  - Place ROCKY (Level 11) or BIRBIE (Level 9) in Slot 1 (lead).
  - On Turn 1 of any trainer combat on S.S. Anne, immediately switch to GEMMY (WARTORTLE, Lv 27) or SPARKY (PIKACHU, Lv 21).
